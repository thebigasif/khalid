A template sets the layout of your blog: where the contents list sits, which cards the home page shows, and how big the text is. Your colours, fonts and logo still come from your own website through [theme sync](/khalid/features/theme-sync/), so every template looks like your brand.

Khalid 1.0 has one template, Classic. The ten below are being added one or two at a time. The [templates guide on GitHub](https://github.com/thebigasif/khalid/blob/main/docs/templates.md) lists the ones in the version you run, and their options.

## Classic

The look every Khalid blog starts with: a clean list of posts, a contents list beside long posts, and author boxes. It suits any blog that wants to start writing today. [See Classic in the demo blog](/khalid/demo/).

## Ledger

A knowledge blog led by data, with no pictures in its lists. It suits teams that publish studies and long how-to guides. [See the Ledger demo](/khalid/templates/ledger/).

## Atlas

A large portal with many categories, breadcrumbs, a category bar and editor's picks. It suits a blog with hundreds of posts across many topics. [See the Atlas demo](/khalid/templates/atlas/).

## Keystone

A personal-brand guide site with a big featured post, a picture grid and numbered chapters. It suits a founder or expert who writes long guides under their own name. [See the Keystone demo](/khalid/templates/keystone/).

## Compass

A teaching blog where every guide opens with its key points. It suits a business that explains how to do things, step by step. [See the Compass demo](/khalid/templates/compass/).

## Harbor

A busy blog with downloadable offers inside posts and a scrolling row of related posts. It suits a marketing team that turns readers into leads. [See the Harbor demo](/khalid/templates/harbor/).

## Wire

A news and columns site with a lead story, a ranked list of popular posts and a fast feed. It suits a publication that posts often. [See the Wire demo](/khalid/templates/wire/).

## Workbench

A tools and comparisons blog with an at-a-glance table, a review of each tool and pros and cons. It suits anyone who compares products for readers. [See the Workbench demo](/khalid/templates/workbench/).

## Almanac

A resource library of long list guides, each with an "On this page" box and many related links. It suits a shop or platform that teaches its customers. [See the Almanac demo](/khalid/templates/almanac/).

## Meridian

A calm company journal, with a second layout for long reference pages. It suits a company that writes thoughtful posts and evergreen explainers. [See the Meridian demo](/khalid/templates/meridian/).

## Folio

A product team's essays and engineering notes under its own masthead. It suits a team that writes about how it builds. [See the Folio demo](/khalid/templates/folio/).

## How to choose one

Pick the template whose readers sound like yours, then set its name in `site.toml`:

```toml
[template]
name = "classic"
```

Updating Khalid never changes your template. Every template works with scripts turned off, and every page reads on a phone.
