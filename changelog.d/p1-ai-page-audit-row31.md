- `khalid audit` notes when a blog has no AI info page, with the check `ai-page-missing` in the AI search group. The AI info page tells AI tools who runs the site, what it covers and how to cite it. Khalid writes one at `ai/` unless `[seo.ai_page]` turns it off, and a page at `ai/` or `ai-info/` on the blog or on its main site counts.
