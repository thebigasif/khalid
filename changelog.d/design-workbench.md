Changed
- Workbench, the template for tool reviews and comparisons, has a new design: a test lab's bench sheet. It now has colours of its own before any theme sync: a grey-white bench, graphite text and one verdict green. The front page opens on the blog's name in very large, tight type on squared paper over a thick rule. On a wide screen the featured post sits beside the five newest posts, numbered 01 to 05, and its picture keeps a 16 by 9 shape instead of filling the window; on list pages it sits beside its words. The other posts show as ruled cells of one sheet, with the category in the code font. On a phone the menu is one row you can swipe. In a post, each section opens under a green rule, a bold label that opens a paragraph (such as "Price:") is set in the code font, the comparison table has a ruled header row and a first column that stays in place on phones, a cell written all in bold is tinted as the pick, and pros and cons show as two ruled columns with plus and minus marks.
