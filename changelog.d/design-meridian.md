Changed
- Meridian, the company journal template, has a new design built around a journal's masthead: the blog's name set very large in the serif over a heavy ink rule. The front page leads with the newest post beside its picture, then two large stories side by side, the editor's picks numbered on a soft band, and the other posts as an index of wide rows with the category, date and author in a column at the start. A post with a cover opens split across the page, the title beside the picture. The page is now 80rem wide, and Meridian's own palette, fonts and type sizes live in its own tokens file. A synced theme still recolours it all.
