Added
- Webhooks for tags and categories: `tag.renamed` and `tag.removed` when a tag is renamed or taken off every post through the Admin API, and `category.added`, `category.renamed` and `category.deleted` when a category is made, given new names or deleted. A rename carries the old copy as `previous`. The admin's Webhooks screen offers them under "Tags and categories" (KH-API-3).
