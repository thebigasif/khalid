Added
- The Workbench template, for blogs that review and compare tools: a featured post, the newest posts as rows, the newsletter twice on the front page, wide comparison tables, pros and cons, and a closing line with the dates. Pick it with `name = "workbench"` under `[template]`. Its guide is `docs/templates/workbench.md`.
- The newsletter form a template places can show a note, such as how often it goes out.
