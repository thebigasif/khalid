Added
The editor's settings panel now has fields for a post's tags, category and featured switch, and a folded section for who may read it (everyone or members), a reader password, a custom template and code for the page head. Every field saves when you leave it, as the others do.

Fixed
The bulk "Set the author" choice on the Posts screen offers every author of the blog, not only the authors of the posts on the page. After picking "Remove a tag", ticking other posts no longer keeps a tag the newly ticked posts do not have.
