Tags and categories

A category is the one shelf a post sits on, such as Technical SEO. A tag is a loose label, and a post can have many. Each has its own page on your blog that lists its posts. Change them here and every post that uses them changes with them.

Categories

Each category has a name in every language of your site. Your categories live in categories.toml in your site folder.

NameShort namePostsAction

Delete shows only for a category no post is in, so no post loses its shelf by accident.

Add a category

Posts name the category by it in their category field. Lowercase words joined by hyphens. It cannot change later.

Tags

Tags come from your posts: a tag exists while one post has it. Each language has its own tags.

NameLanguagePostsAction
internal linksEnglish3 postsRenameRemove
sitemapsEnglish1 postRenameRemove
interne LinksGerman2 postsRenameRemove

After Rename on a tag, the screen shows only this, until you rename or go back.

Rename "internal links"

3 posts have this tag, drafts included. The new name replaces it in each of them, and live posts change at once. If another English tag already has the new name, the two become one tag.

As readers see it on posts and on the tag's page.

After Remove on a tag.

Remove "sitemaps"

The tag comes off 1 post, drafts included. The posts stay, and live posts change at once. The tag's own page goes with it.

Back to the list

After Rename on a category.

Rename "On-page SEO"

4 posts are in this category. They keep it, and live posts show the new name at once. The short name on-page-seo stays, so no address changes.

Back to the list

After Delete on an empty category.

Delete "Content strategy"

No post is in this category. It goes from categories.toml and its page goes with it.
