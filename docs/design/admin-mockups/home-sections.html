Settings

These settings cover the whole blog. They are saved to site.toml in your site folder, the same file you can edit by hand.

Home page

Choose what your home page shows, top first. Each section shows a few posts and a link to all of them. With no sections, the home page lists your newest posts.

  1. Featured postsPosts marked featured, newest first.
  2. Category: technical-seoThe newest posts in this category.
  3. Newsletter sign-upThe sign-up form, here instead of where the template puts it.
  4. Newest postsYour newest posts, with a link to the archive.
The end of the category's address, such as on-page-seo for /blog/category/on-page-seo/.
