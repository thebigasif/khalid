Navigation

The links in your blog's header and footer, on every page. Readers use them to find their way around.

Header menu

The main links at the top of every page, left to right. On a phone they fold into a Menu button.

  1. Guides/blog/guides/
  2. Technical SEO/blog/category/technical-seo/
  3. Impressum (de)/blog/de/impressum/
The words readers see, such as Guides.
A page on your blog, such as /blog/about/, or a full address, such as https://www.example.com/.

Footer menu

Links at the bottom of every page, such as About or your legal pages. Columns of footer links under a heading are set in site.toml, and saving here keeps them.
