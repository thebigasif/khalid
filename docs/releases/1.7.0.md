# Khalid 1.7.0

Khalid 1.7.0 asks your readers for consent before anything tracks them. It is on for every site, with no setup. Every site also gets a privacy page, and the admin keeps proof of what readers chose. The full guide is `docs/privacy/consent.md`.

This is not legal advice. Khalid follows the rules regulators publish, but only a lawyer can tell you what your own site needs.

## Added

- **Cookie consent, on by default.** Nothing optional loads before a reader agrees: not your own scripts, not videos, not maps. A reader who never answers loads nothing from anyone but your site.
- **A banner only when your site needs one.** Readers see a banner only if your site has its own tracking scripts, such as analytics or ads. Khalid's own visitor counts, fonts, search and share images set no cookie, so a site with no scripts of its own shows no banner at all. The banner offers Accept all and Reject all, the same size, and Choose. It sits at the top of the page and never covers the text.
- **Embeds ask for themselves.** A YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify, SoundCloud, CodePen or Loom embed, or a map from a domain you allow, shows a card instead. The card names who runs it. "Load media" loads that one embed, and "Always load external media" allows them all.
- **Readers can change their mind.** Every page links "Your privacy choices" in its footer. That page lists five categories (Necessary, Preferences, Analytics, Marketing, External media) with a switch for each.
- **Global Privacy Control is honoured.** Global Privacy Control, GPC for short, is a signal some browsers send to say "do not sell or share my data". Khalid keeps Marketing off for those readers. You can make it turn every optional category off instead.
- **A privacy page, written for you.** Every site has one at `privacy/`, made from your real settings: who runs the site, what it collects, its cookies, other companies, how long data is kept and readers' rights. Your own page with the slug `privacy` replaces it. Add your name, email and postal address so the page is complete; `khalid doctor` reminds you until you do.
- **A Privacy screen in the admin.** Set how long a choice lasts (6 months by default), what GPC turns off, whether choices are counted, your own banner words, your contact details, and a category for each of your scripts.
- **Proof of consent.** The Proof tab on the Privacy screen shows how many readers allowed each category, week by week, every version of the banner they saw, and a CSV download for a regulator. Khalid keeps only daily totals, with no address and nothing that names a reader.
- **Static sites ask too.** A site built as plain files gets the same banner from a script under 2 KB. Choices there are not counted.

## What changes for you

- **Tag your scripts.** If `site.toml` holds your own code, give each script a category: `preferences`, `analytics`, `marketing` or `no-tracking`. You can do it on the Privacy screen, under Scripts. A script with no category counts as Marketing, so it waits for the strictest yes. Old `[code]` tables keep working.
- **Your readers are asked once more.** A reader who said yes under the old banner sees the new one once. A reader who said no keeps their no.
- **Videos show a card first.** Every reader now presses "Load media" before a video, a song or a map loads. Feeds and emails carry the title and a link instead of the player.
- **The editor links fewer bare names.** Typing `example.com` in the admin's editor no longer makes a link. `https://example.com` and `www.example.com` still do.

## How to turn things off

- **You use a certified consent tool.** If you run ads, you may need a consent tool that Google certifies, such as Cookiebot, which Khalid is not. Turn on "I use a certified consent tool instead" on the Privacy screen and name the tool, or set it in `site.toml`:

  ```toml
  [privacy]
  external_tool = "Cookiebot"
  ```

  Khalid then shows no banner, writes your scripts as they are and names your tool on the privacy page. Your tool must ask before your scripts run. Embeds still show their cards.
- **A script that tracks nobody.** Tag it `no-tracking` and it runs for every reader without asking. Use this only for code that sets no cookie and sends nothing about the reader anywhere; the privacy page names it.
- **Stop counting choices.** Turn off "Count readers' choices each day" on the Privacy screen, or set `counts = false` under `[privacy]`.
