# Khalid 1.6.1

Khalid 1.6.1 makes pages lighter. Each page now downloads only the styles it uses, and pictures can come in AVIF alone.

## Added

- **AVIF in place of WebP.** Set `avif = "only"` under `[media]` in `site.toml`. Pages then offer AVIF and one JPEG or PNG, at most 1,024 px wide, for the few browsers that cannot read AVIF. Pictures take about half the space on disk. It is off by default, and `avif = true` still offers AVIF beside WebP.

## Changed

- **Each page downloads only the CSS it uses.** A post no longer carries the rules of the home page, and the reverse. On every template demo, a page downloads at most 10 KB of CSS once compressed, where most pages downloaded 10 to 14 KB before. Pages look exactly as before, and your own rules in `theme/site.css` still reach every page.
- **The template demos are lighter.** Their fonts are cut to the letters each demo shows, so no demo page downloads more than 60 KB of fonts, and the demos download shrank from 702 MB to 310 MB.
