# Khalid 1.2.0

Khalid 1.2.0 makes your blog and your admin look professional, and gives every author a proper profile. Nothing changes on an existing blog until you pick a template, and your own `theme/tokens.toml` still wins over a template's colours.

## Ten redesigned templates

All ten templates now have finished, professional designs. Almanac, Atlas, Compass, Folio, Harbor, Keystone, Meridian and Wire are redesigned from the ground up, and Ledger and Workbench got the same polish: clear type, calm colours, picture cards, contents beside long posts, and an author box and related posts after each post. Every template was checked in a real browser on every screen size, from a 320 pixel phone through tablets and laptops to a 27 inch iMac. Each template's guide in `docs/templates/` lists its options, including the few that bring back an older choice.

## A modern admin

The admin now looks like a modern app: white cards on a calm grey page, a slim side menu with icons (a menu button on phones), search with Cmd K or Ctrl K, and your blog's own accent colour. The dashboard greets your blog by name and shows what needs your attention. The sign-in page is one clear card with your blog's name and logo, passkey first, then email and password.

## Finished author profiles

- **Your own profile.** Your account is now your author profile. Edit it under "Your account", and a post you start in the admin names you as its author.
- **Photo upload.** Upload an author photo, crop it square, or pick one from Media.
- **Designed author pages.** Each author page shows a large photo, role, credentials, post count and profile icons, then the author's posts. Search engines learn who the author is.
- **Bylines with photos.** Every byline shows the author's photo, and "Reviewed by" shows the reviewer's photo too.

## Also new

A check script, `scripts/responsive-check.mjs`, takes a screenshot of each template and the admin at eleven widths, so you can see every screen size yourself.

To install or update, follow [docs/deploy.md](../deploy.md). The full list of changes is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md).
