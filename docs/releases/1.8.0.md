# Khalid 1.8.0

Khalid 1.8.0 lets you choose what your home page shows, helps Google find your videos and understand your blocks, schedules pages as well as posts, and puts saved settings to work without a restart.

## Added

- **Choose your home page.** Your home page can now show sections in the order you pick: your newest posts, your featured posts, a category, a collection, a channel, a series in reading order, and your newsletter sign-up. Each section has a "View all" link. Pick them in the admin under Site settings, "Home page", or list them in `site.toml`. Every template shows them in its own style. If you choose none, your home page stays as it is. The guide is `docs/home-sections.md`.
- **Videos in search.** Every post with a video now appears in a video sitemap, a list of your videos made for search engines. Khalid writes it for you on `khalid build` and `khalid serve`. Video blocks and YouTube, Vimeo and Loom embeds all count, so Google can show your posts in its video results.
- **Schedule more than posts.** Pages, glossary terms, case studies and changelog entries can now go live at a time you choose. Put the draft under `drafts/` and add a `publish_at:` time to its front matter, the settings block at the top of the file. `khalid serve` publishes it at that time.
- **Blocks that explain themselves to search engines.** Structured data is a hidden note on a page that tells search engines what it describes, such as an event or a book. Four new blocks write it for you: `:::event`, `:::course`, `::book` and `:::dataset`. Business card and location blocks now write it too. If you also describe the same thing by hand in the post's `schema` field, the page keeps only the block's version, so Google never sees it twice.
- **A reminder to show who runs the blog.** `khalid audit` now warns, on the home page, when readers cannot reach an About or contact page. Readers trust a blog more when they can see who is behind it, and AI tools need someone to credit. Add a page with the slug `about` or `contact`, or link to one on your main site.

## Changed

- **Every webhook now fires.** A webhook is a message Khalid sends to another service when something happens. Scheduling, moving or cancelling a page's publish time now sends one, and so does a change to your settings, templates or routes once the blog is using it. The webhook guide says which command sends each one.
- **A smaller program.** The `khalid` program is about 330 KB smaller. It talks to outside services such as Search Console, SerpDad and your webhooks the way all of them already accept, so nothing changes for you.

## Fixed

- **Settings work without a restart.** Saving your name, look, SEO or privacy settings in the admin now takes effect within a few seconds. The admin asks for a restart only when a setting truly needs one, such as your address or languages. Post checks and the publish button use your new SEO settings straight away too.
- **Settings load with mail and storage keys.** If your site sends mail through Resend, Postmark or SMTP, or keeps pictures in an S3 bucket, the admin's settings screens now open and save. Your keys and passwords are never shown or sent back.
- **A warning for subdomain blogs.** If your blog lives at a subdomain such as `blog.example.com`, `khalid lint` now warns when it tells Google to ignore its links to your main site. List your main site under `internal_domains` in `[seo.links]` to fix it.
- **Fairer advice on short answers.** The writing check for plain answers now warns only when it should. A word like "unusually" no longer counts as the vague word "usually". It now spots a vague start such as "There are" in any sentence of an answer, and each warning says how to fix it.
- **Docker and Ubuntu installs.** The Docker guide now shows how to make the admin's owner inside the image. `install.sh --systemd` now works on Ubuntu. Both are now tested exactly as the guide says.

Nothing changes in your content. Your site picks up these changes when you update.
