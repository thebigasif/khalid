# Khalid 1.6.0

Khalid 1.6.0 adds 18 templates of its own design, so there are 49 to choose from. Each has a live demo blog at serpdad.com/khalid/demos/. Pictures are also much lighter, above all on phones.

## Added

- **Eighteen new templates:**
  - Brief, for a short digest newsletter.
  - Dialogue, for an interview show.
  - Dispatch, for a newsletter that wins sign-ups.
  - Frequency, for a podcast.
  - Gallery, for a studio or an illustrator.
  - Gazette, for local news with many sections.
  - Glossy, for a lifestyle magazine.
  - Hearth, for a small team or a family business.
  - Inkwell, for a newsletter archive.
  - Letterpress, for a newsletter set as printed editions.
  - Manual, for a help centre or product documentation.
  - Margin, for one writer's essays.
  - Masthead, for a magazine, a review or a small paper.
  - Mosaic, for a photo blog.
  - Notebook, for short notes and links.
  - Pantry, for recipes.
  - Quill, for a minimal text blog.
  - Spectrum, for a magazine arranged by topic.

  Each passes the same layout and speed checks as the others, on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.

## Changed

- **Pictures load at the size they are shown.** Every template tells the browser how wide each picture really is, so a small card no longer downloads a large photo. On the demo home pages, pictures weigh 40% less on a desktop.
- **Lighter pictures on phones with sharp screens.** Pictures also come in a 1,152 px size, and AVIF pictures are about 12% smaller with no visible difference. On the demo home pages, pictures on such phones weigh about half what they did.
- **Each page asks for its body font first,** so text shows sooner.
