# Khalid 1.7.1

Khalid 1.7.1 makes every template look right on laptops and phones, and fixes a security flaw in the database Khalid carries inside it. Please update.

## Security

- **Update now.** Khalid keeps its data in SQLite, a small database built into the `khalid` program. Older SQLite had a security flaw, listed as RUSTSEC-2026-0459. Khalid 1.7.1 carries SQLite 3.54, which fixes it. If your site runs `khalid serve`, update to 1.7.1. Your content and settings stay as they are.

## Fixed

- **Headlines read cleanly on a Mac.** Big titles in every template had their letters set too tight, so they ran together on a Mac. They now have a little more room, and the classic template's post titles are a size smaller and a step lighter.
- **Everything lines up with the header.** On a laptop, the privacy banner, the announcement above the header and a post with its contents beside it now start where the header starts. The contents sit close beside the words.
- **No dark bars.** Announcement bars, call-to-action bands and footers that were dark are now light, with dark words, and announcement bars reach both edges of the window.
- **Smaller fixes.** Beacon's donate button no longer drops below the name on a laptop, Fixture's header button sits beside the name, and the gaps between menu links are even.

Nothing changes in your settings or your content. Your site picks up the new look when you update.
