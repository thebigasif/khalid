# Khalid 1.3.0

Khalid 1.3.0 gives your blog the checks and settings people know from Yoast, and makes your posts easier to read and to find. Nothing changes on an existing blog until you use a new setting, with two small exceptions listed under "Good to know".

## Checks that read like an editor

- **Smarter keyword checks.** Your focus keyword now counts in any word form, so "internal link" also counts for "internal links", in English and German. List other words that mean the same with `keyword_synonyms:` and they count too. Khalid also warns when a keyword is too long, too thinly spread, or already used by an older post.
- **Readability checks.** Khalid points out long sentences, long stretches without a subheading, too much passive voice and too few linking words, in English and German. It only reads your own words, not code, quotes or tables.
- **Two scores for every post.** One score for SEO (how well search engines can understand the post) and one for readability, each out of 100. Tips are shown in blue and never cost points.

## Search settings without editing files

- **An SEO settings screen** in the admin, under Site. Set how titles and descriptions look in Google with simple building blocks such as the post title or the year, and see a live preview. Choose which pages show in search, who is behind the blog, your social profiles, verification codes and which AI crawlers to block.
- **Breadcrumbs** (the "Home > Topic > Post" trail above a post) can be set up the way you like: the separator, the name of the first step and a shorter title per post.
- **A bulk SEO editor** fixes the title, description and focus keyword of many posts in one table, and can show only the posts that miss one.
- **SEO workouts** walk you through linking posts that nothing links to yet, one press at a time.
- **The editor helps more.** A Google preview for desktop and phone, previews for Facebook and X, up to four secondary keywords, and suggested links to your other posts that you add with one press.

## Local businesses

If your blog belongs to a shop or office, add your locations once. A location block shows the address, phone, opening hours, a map link and whether you are open now. A locations page lists them all, nearest first if the reader agrees to share where they are.

## Optional AI suggestions

Khalid can suggest titles, descriptions, key takeaways, image descriptions and fixes for some checks. This stays off until you turn it on and add a key from SerpDad, Anthropic or a similar service. Nothing changes in your post until you pick a suggestion.

## Richer WordPress imports

Imports from WordPress now keep what Yoast, Rank Math or All in One SEO stored: the focus keyword, related keywords and synonyms, share cards for X and Facebook, and which posts were your main ones.

## Easier reading and finding

- **Reading aids.** A thin progress bar at the top of each post, a contents list that marks where you are, a "Copy link" button on every heading, and a search box that shows results as you type.
- **New ways to find posts.** Series with "Part 2 of 5" and links between the parts, hub pages that open a topic, a glossary from A to Z, and pages for case studies and your product changelog.
- **New things to put in a post.** Charts drawn from your numbers, embedded tools from sites you trust, a key takeaways box, readers' star ratings on reviews, and a newsletter page with past issues.
- **Smaller pictures.** Turn on AVIF, an image format about half the size of JPEG, with `avif = true` under `[media]`. Browsers that cannot show it get the usual format.
- **A Calendar** in the admin shows live, scheduled and draft posts by day. Drag a draft onto a day to publish it then.

## Good to know

- The longest paragraph a check accepts is now 150 words, as in Yoast. If you set your own number, it stays.
- A post's main score is now its SEO score. Readability has its own score.
