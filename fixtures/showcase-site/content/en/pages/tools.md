We keep our tool kit small. Every tool on this page is one we use on real client work each week, and we say plainly where each one falls short. Nobody pays to be listed here.

## Quillmeter, our readability checker

Quillmeter is a small desktop app that checks a draft as you type. It flags long sentences and rare words in place and leaves the rewriting to you. We use it on the sentence step of [the checklist our editors run on every post](/blog/editing-checklist/).

:::review{item="Quillmeter" type="SoftwareApplication" rating="4" best="5" author="Maren Okafor"}
Quillmeter does one job well. It highlights long sentences and rare words in place, without rewriting anything for you, so the writer stays in charge. It is quick and works offline. It loses a point because it treats every long word as a problem, even the technical terms a specialist reader expects, and because it has no way to share settings across a team.
:::
