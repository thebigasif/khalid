Write to us about a post, a mistake you spotted, or a project you would like help with. Your note goes to the whole team, and the person who knows the subject best replies within two working days.

## Send us a note

:::form{id="contact" button="Send your note"}
- text name: Your name
- email reply_to: Your email address
- textarea message: Your note
:::

We use your address only to reply to this note. We never add it to the newsletter unless you sign up yourself.

## Report a mistake

If a post says something wrong, tell us which post and what you found. We check every report, and when we change a post because of one, we say so at the top of the post. Our [editorial policy](/blog/editorial-policy/) explains how corrections work.
