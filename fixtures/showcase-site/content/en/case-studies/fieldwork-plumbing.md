Fieldwork Plumbing is a fictional plumbing firm we use as a worked example. Its blog had news about the team and little else, and no reader ever called from it.

We planned one cluster on boiler repairs: a long guide, five short posts on the faults people search for, and a call to book a visit at the end of each. Every short post linked to the guide, and the guide linked to all five. The [content brief template](/blog/content-brief-template/) kept each post on its one question.

Three months later, the blog brought in about five calls a week. Most came from the short posts, from readers who had found their fault and wanted it fixed that day.
