From today, anyone can download sample benchmark data from our 2026 content benchmark, free and under an open licence. The file sits on a new page with a data dictionary, our method and a note on how to cite it.

## What we released

The [new download page](/blog/content-benchmark-data/) holds one spreadsheet file in the CSV format, which any spreadsheet app opens. Each row is one blog. Each column is one habit we counted: whether posts carry an author name, how many descriptions were left blank, whether anything was updated in the past year, and whether numbers link to a source.

The page also explains every column in plain words, so you can check a figure before you quote it.

:::callout{kind=warning}
Larkspur Studio and its benchmark are fictional: they exist to show what Khalid can do. The rows in the file are illustrative sample data, not a survey of real blogs. Use them to try out a chart or a formula, never as evidence.
:::

## Why we opened the benchmark data

We publish numbers in almost every post. Readers who want to check them should not have to take our word for it. Opening the benchmark data lets you rebuild our charts, test a different cut, or tell us where we got something wrong.

It also follows our own advice. Our [complete guide to content strategy](/blog/content-strategy-guide/) argues that original data is one of the few things a small team can publish that nobody else has.

## How you may use it

The file is shared under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You may copy it, change it and use it in your own work, for any purpose, as long as you name Larkspur Ledger and link to the data page.

## What comes next

We will add the 2027 rows next autumn, in the same columns, so the two years line up. If a column would help your own planning, [write to us](/blog/contact/) and say what you would do with it.

For the findings themselves, start with [the 2026 content benchmark](/blog/content-benchmark/), which walks through each habit and what it means for a small team.
