term: E-E-A-T
slug: e-e-a-t
---

E-E-A-T stands for experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness. It comes from the guidelines Google gives the people who rate the quality of search results. It is not one ranking signal, but a description of what helpful content shows: who wrote it, why they know the subject, and how they checked it. Our [editorial policy](/blog/editorial-policy/) explains how we show each part.
