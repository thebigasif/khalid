---
term: Largest Contentful Paint
slug: largest-contentful-paint
translation: largest-contentful-paint
---

Largest Contentful Paint, often shortened to LCP, is the time from the moment a reader asks for a page until the biggest image or block of text on the screen has appeared. It is one of the three Core Web Vitals that Google uses to judge how a page feels to load. See [Core Web Vitals for writers](/blog/core-web-vitals/).
