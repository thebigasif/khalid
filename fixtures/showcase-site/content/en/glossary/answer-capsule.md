term: Answer capsule
slug: answer-capsule
translation: answer-capsule
---

An answer capsule is a short paragraph, about forty to sixty words, placed right under a heading that asks a question. It answers that question completely on its own, so a reader in a hurry, or an AI search tool looking for a passage to quote, can take it without reading the rest. See [how AI search chooses its sources](/blog/ai-search-sources/).
