---
term: Structured data
slug: structured-data
---

Structured data is code in a page, usually JSON-LD using the schema.org vocabulary, that describes what the page is in a form machines can read: an article and its author, a recipe, a product and its price, a list of questions and answers. Search engines use it to understand the page and to show richer results. Our [step-by-step guide to structured data](/blog/structured-data/) shows what a blog needs.
