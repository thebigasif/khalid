---
term: Backlink
slug: backlink
---

A backlink is a link to your site from a page on another site. Search engines treat links from relevant, trusted sites as a sign that a page is worth showing. Bought links and link swaps break Google's spam policies. Free tools such as Google Search Console list the sites that link to you; our list of [the best free SEO tools](/blog/best-free-seo-tools/) names a few.
