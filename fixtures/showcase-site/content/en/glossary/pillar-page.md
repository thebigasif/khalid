---
term: Pillar page
slug: pillar-page
---

A pillar page is the main guide on a broad subject, linked to and from a set of shorter posts that each cover one part of it in depth. Together they form a topic cluster. Readers get a map of the subject, and search engines see how the pages relate. Our [SEO writing guide](/blog/seo-writing-guide/) is the pillar page of our writing posts.
