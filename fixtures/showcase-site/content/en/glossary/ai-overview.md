An AI Overview is the answer Google's AI writes at the top of some results pages, with links to the pages it drew on. A page can only be linked from one if it can be crawled and indexed; Google says no special markup is needed. Clear answers and named sources help a page be chosen. Our [guide to optimising for AI search](/blog/ai-search-guide/) goes further.
