---
term: Query fan-out
slug: query-fan-out
---

Query fan-out is the way AI search tools split one question into several related searches, run them all, and combine what they find into one answer. Google has described it for AI Mode. It means a page can be quoted for a sub-question its author never targeted, which rewards posts that answer the follow-up questions too. Our [guide to optimising for AI search](/blog/ai-search-guide/) covers it.
