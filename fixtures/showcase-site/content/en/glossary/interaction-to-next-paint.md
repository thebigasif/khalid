term: Interaction to Next Paint
slug: interaction-to-next-paint
---

Interaction to Next Paint, or INP, is one of the three Core Web Vitals. It measures how long a page takes to show a response after a reader taps, clicks or types, taken over the whole visit. Google calls 200 milliseconds or less good. It replaced First Input Delay in March 2024. Our [notes on Google's talk about debugging INP](/blog/inp-video-notes/) explain how to find slow interactions.
