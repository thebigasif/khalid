---
term: Hreflang
slug: hreflang
---

Hreflang is an attribute that tells search engines which language, and optionally which region, a page is written for, and where its translations live. Each version lists every other version and itself. Search engines then show a reader the version in their language. Our [hreflang guide for a two-language blog](/blog/hreflang-guide/) walks through a working setup.
