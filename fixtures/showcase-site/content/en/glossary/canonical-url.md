---
term: Canonical URL
slug: canonical-url
---

A canonical URL is the one address you want search engines to treat as the main version of a page when the same content can be reached at several addresses, such as with and without tracking parameters. You name it with a `rel="canonical"` link in the page's head. It is a strong hint, not an order. Our [technical SEO audit guide](/blog/technical-seo-audit/) shows how to check it.
