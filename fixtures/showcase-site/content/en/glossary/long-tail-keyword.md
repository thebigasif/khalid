---
term: Long-tail keyword
slug: long-tail-keyword
---

A long-tail keyword is a specific search phrase that few people type each month, usually three words or more, such as "content calendar template for two writers". Each one brings little traffic, but together they make up most searches, and they are often easier to rank for and closer to a real need. Our [keyword research guide](/blog/keyword-research/) shows how to find them.
