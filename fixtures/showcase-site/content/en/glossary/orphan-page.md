---
term: Orphan page
slug: orphan-page
---

An orphan page is a page that no other page on the same site links to. Readers cannot reach it by browsing, and search engines may find it only through a sitemap, if at all. Every new post should get at least one link from an older, related post. Our [internal linking guide](/blog/internal-linking/) shows how to find orphans and fix them.
