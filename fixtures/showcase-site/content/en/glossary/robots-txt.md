---
term: Robots.txt
slug: robots-txt
---

Robots.txt is a plain text file at the root of a site that tells crawlers which paths they may fetch. It controls crawling, not indexing: a page blocked in robots.txt can still appear in results if other sites link to it. To keep a page out of results, let it be crawled and mark it noindex instead.
