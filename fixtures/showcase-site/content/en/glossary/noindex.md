---
term: Noindex
slug: noindex
---

Noindex is a rule, set in a robots meta tag or an HTTP header, that asks search engines to leave a page out of their results. The crawler must be able to fetch the page to see the rule, so a noindexed page should not also be blocked in robots.txt. Thin tag pages and internal search results are common candidates.
