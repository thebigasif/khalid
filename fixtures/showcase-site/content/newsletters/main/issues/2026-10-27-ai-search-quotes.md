# Why AI search quoted the smaller blog

A client asked us why an AI answer quoted a tiny competitor instead of their long guide. The answer was one sentence: the competitor stated the fact plainly, near the top, with its source beside it.

## The lesson

Write the answer first, in one sentence a tool can lift as it is. Then explain. Our post on [how AI search picks which pages to quote](/blog/ai-search-sources/) shows the pattern with examples.

## Three links

- [Search Central's guide to helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), still the best short read on the subject.
- Our own [content brief template](/blog/content-brief-template/), now with a line for the one-sentence answer.
- [Structured data, explained for writers](/blog/structured-data/), for the pages where it helps.

## A reader asks

"Should every post start with a summary?" Not every post, but every post that answers a question. A reader who came for the answer should not scroll to find it.
