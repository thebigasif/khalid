# Titles that earn the click

A title is a promise. This fortnight we rewrote titles for a client's ten best posts, and the ones that named the reader's problem did best.

## The lesson

Name the problem the reader has, then the result they get. Leave the clever wordplay for the post itself. Our guide to [headlines that earn the click](/blog/writing-headlines/) has twelve examples.

## Three links

- [Meta descriptions that sell the click](/blog/meta-description/), the other half of the search result.
- [Search intent, explained](/blog/search-intent/), to know which problem to name.
- [Google's guidance on title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link), on how titles show in results.

## A reader asks

"Is a question a good title?" When the reader would type that question, yes. Otherwise, state the answer.
