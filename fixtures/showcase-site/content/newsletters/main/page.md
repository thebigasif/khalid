# The Larkspur Letter

One short email every other Tuesday with what we learned that fortnight about writing posts people find, read and trust. It takes five minutes to read, and every email has a link to leave.

:::callout{kind=summary}
- **One lesson from client work**, with the before and after.
- **Three links worth your time**, each with a line on why.
- **One question from a reader**, answered in plain words.
:::

No tracking pixels: we never see who opened what. We send it from Bristol, and we never share your address.
