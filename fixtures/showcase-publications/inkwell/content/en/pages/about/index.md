The Seed Tin is the quarterly letter of the Penhallow Seed Library, a seed library in a Cornish harbour village. It has gone out four times a year since spring 2004, one issue each season, and every issue is numbered. The library, its growers, its seeds and the village are made up, to show the Inkwell template. This demo holds the issues from summer 2024 on.

## How the library works

The library lends seed the way a book library lends books. In spring you take a few packets from the tin. You grow them, let a few of the best plants run to seed, and in autumn you bring some seed back. Membership is free, and nobody checks what you return. Read [twenty years of swaps](/blog/seed-swaps-twenty-years/) for how it started.

## What is in each issue

- **The lead story:** one seed, one grower or one season.
- **Growing:** Tomasz Wren's notes on sowing, keeping a variety true and saving its seed.
- **From the tin:** the story of one variety the library keeps.
- **Dates:** the next seed swaps and open days at the old chapel.

The [Issues](/blog/category/issues/) page lists every issue by year. Supplements, such as the seed list before a swap, are under [Supplements](/blog/category/supplements/).

## Writing to the letter

Letters about your own growing are welcome at letters@example.com, and the best go in the next issue. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work as public domain or under CC0. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
