It rained on twenty-four days in August. Runner beans that should be drying on the plant are swelling in the wet instead, and some have started to sprout inside their pods. This issue is about saving runner bean seed in a year like this one.

## Can you save runner bean seed after rain?

Yes, if you act early. Seed that sprouts in the pod is lost, and seed that dries slowly in damp air goes mouldy. So do not wait for the pods to dry outside this year. Once the pods are full and the beans show as bumps, pick them, or pull the whole plant. Then bring them in.

## Growing: drying runner bean seed indoors

Spread the pods in a single layer on newspaper in a warm room with air moving through it. Turn them every few days. When the pods are brittle, shell the beans and leave them out for another two weeks. A dry bean snaps cleanly when you bite it, while a damp one dents. Store dry beans in paper, never plastic, because plastic traps the last of the damp. [Wikipedia's runner bean page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phaseolus_coccineus) has more on the plant.

Throw out any bean that is soft, wrinkled or spotted. It is hard to do. However, one mouldy bean in a packet can spoil the rest.
