This is the winter seed list, sent between issues so you can plan before the swap. It lists what the library has to lend this winter, and also the seed we are short of. Everything on it is free to members, so take what you will grow.

## On the winter seed list this year

- **Beans:** Scarlet Emperor, Penhallow Purple, Annie's Longpod and Trevose Flint maize.
- **Peas:** a few packets of Gwenna's Pea, for growers who will save seed.
- **Squash:** Chapel Butternut, from the good summer of 2025.
- **Tomatoes and peppers:** Doorstep and Mabe Pepper, grown under glass.
- **Garlic:** Harbour Red cloves, ten to a bag.

## What we are short of

The wet August hit the beans hard, so we would love runner and climbing beans of any kind. Please dry them well, then put them in paper envelopes with the name and the year. We are also short of lettuce and leek seed, because few growers let those run to seed. [Wikipedia's page on seed saving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_saving) is a good start if you have never saved seed before.

## How to borrow from the list

Come to the winter swap on Saturday 5 December at the old chapel, from ten until two. First, take up to six packets. Then write your name in the book, so we can ask you for seed next autumn. If you are new, start with something easy, such as [green beans by the bucket](/blog/green-beans-bucket/) or Annie's beans from the spring issue on [broad beans that outlived their grower](/blog/broad-beans-annie-pascoe/).

## Before you come

Read the autumn issue on [saving runner bean seed](/blog/runner-bean-seed-wet-summer/) for how to dry beans after a wet year. Bring a pencil, since every packet needs a name and a date. Finally, bring a cake tin if you have one spare: the library always needs more tins.
