Morwenna started the seed library in 2004 with one biscuit tin of beans from her uncle's allotment. She has edited every issue of The Seed Tin since the first, a single folded sheet. She grows on two allotment plots above the harbour, writes the lead story of each issue and runs the seed swaps at the old chapel.
