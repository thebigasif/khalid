Tessel Rowe Architects is an architecture and interiors practice in the old sail loft on Harrowfield quay. We design houses, interiors, workplaces and public buildings, and most of our work gives an old building a second life. Tessel Rowe, Harrowfield and every project on this site are made up, to show the Plinth template.

Each project on this site has its own post with its photograph, a plan we drew for it, and its place, year, floor area and stage. Studio notes cover materials, methods and news.

## Who works here

- **Ines Tessel** founded the practice and leads the houses and public buildings.
- **Jonah Rowe** leads the interiors and workplaces, and designs the joinery.
- **Priya Anand** runs projects from survey to handover and leads [Brook Row](/blog/brook-row-almshouses/).

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their projects.

## How we work

We start every project with a site visit and a day of [drawing by hand](/blog/drawing-by-hand/). We show clients drawings and models, not renderings, and we stay on the job until the building is finished. Good places to start are [Quarry Lane Library](/blog/quarry-lane-library/) and [Whitecross House](/blog/whitecross-house/).

## Start a project

Write to studio@example.com with a few lines about your building and what you hope to do with it. One of us will reply within a week and visit within two. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
