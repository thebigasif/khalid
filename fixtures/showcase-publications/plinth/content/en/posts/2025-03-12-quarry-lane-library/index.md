Quarry Lane Library is the town library of Harrowfield, a red brick building of 1889 on the corner of Quarry Lane. Our extension doubles its space without hiding the old building. The Victorian library still faces the street. The new reading room sits beside it, set back by four metres, and a glass entrance joins the two.

## What Quarry Lane Library needed

The old library had one large room, a small office and no space for groups. Children's events filled the main room twice a week, so anyone who came to read or study had to leave. The council asked for a quiet room, a room for children and one desk where the staff could see both. They also asked us to keep the old building in use the whole time, so the town never lost its library.

## The plan

We kept the old library as the lending room and put everything new in the extension. The plan below shows the two buildings side by side. The entrance between them is the only door, so the desk can see everyone who comes and goes.

The reading room has tall windows on the north side, so it gets even light all day and no glare on screens. The children's room opens off it but has its own door, so noise stays inside. A small study room at the far end can be booked by groups.

## Why brick

The old library is red brick with stone sills. We used a brick of the same size and a slightly darker colour, laid in the same bond. From the street the extension reads as part of the same family, but no one mistakes it for the old building. Each window is set deep in the wall, a full brick and a half, which gives a seat on the inside and a shadow on the outside. Brick also suits a public building that will be leaned on, kicked and climbed for a hundred years.

## How Quarry Lane Library is used now

The library now runs children's events every day after school without closing the quiet room. Visits rose by about a third in the first full year. The study room is booked most evenings by students from the college up the hill. The council's [library service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_library) now uses Quarry Lane as its model for two smaller branches.
