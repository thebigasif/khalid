We are looking for an architectural assistant to join the studio in January 2027. You will work on two or three houses at a time and help Priya on the [six homes at Brook Row](/blog/brook-row-almshouses/) as they go up on site.

## What our architectural assistant will do

- Survey buildings and draw them up.
- Draw plans, sections and details, by hand first and then on the computer.
- Make models and sample boards for client meetings.
- Visit sites every week with a project architect.
- Spend a week with our plasterer and a week with a joiner in your first year.

Recent work you would join in on includes the [Ropewalk makers' workshops](/blog/ropewalk-workshops/), the [Georgian house on Bow Lane](/blog/bow-lane-townhouse/) and the [new stair in the old chapel](/blog/chapel-gallery-stair/).

## What we look for

You have finished the first part of your training and have at least a year in practice. You draw well by hand, as we explain in our note on [starting with tracing paper](/blog/drawing-by-hand/). You care about how buildings are made, not only how they look. You can explain a drawing to a client who has never read one.

## What we offer

A salary in line with the [RIBA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_British_Architects) guidance for your stage, a four day week in August, and one day a month for study. The studio is in the old sail loft on the quay, with room for six. Every architectural assistant we have hired has stayed to qualify with us.

## A week in the studio

Monday starts with a short meeting where each of us says what they are drawing and what is stuck. Most of the week is spent at the desk and the drawing table, with one or two site visits. On Thursday afternoons we pin up work in progress and talk it through as a group, with tea. Friday ends early. We keep the studio small on purpose, so everyone works on real buildings from the first week and sees them through to the end.

## How to apply

Send a short letter and ten pages of your work to studio@example.com by 30 November 2026. Show us drawings you made, including sketches, and tell us which building in the town you would most like to work on. We will meet everyone on the shortlist in the second week of December, at the studio, and we answer every letter.
