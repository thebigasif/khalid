The Ropewalk shed is forty metres long and ten wide, a steel frame built for a boat yard in the 1970s. The yard closed in 2019. A group of local makers asked us to turn it into the Ropewalk workshops: four bays they could rent at a fair price, close to the town centre.

## Keep the frame, change the skin

The steel frame was sound, so we kept it and replaced only the walls and roof. The south side is now open for most of its length, behind folding doors. The roof carries solar panels that cover most of the power the bays use in summer.

## The four bays of the Ropewalk workshops

Each bay is eight metres by ten, with its own folding door, power and water. Timber partitions divide them, three metres high, so the roof space stays open and the whole shed shares the light from the roof lights.

| Bay | Tenant | What they make | Floor area | Rent band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bay 1 | Fernside Joinery | Furniture and kitchens | 80 m² | Standard |
| Bay 2 | Kiln Twelve | Tiles and tableware | 80 m² | Standard |
| Bay 3 | North Quay Bikes | Bicycle frames and repairs | 80 m² | Reduced, first two years |
| Bay 4 | Harrow Print Collective | Letterpress and screen print | 80 m² | Reduced, first two years |
| Shared end | All tenants | Meeting room, kitchen, washrooms | 60 m² | Included |

## The shared end

The meeting room, kitchen and washrooms sit at the east end. The tenants use the meeting room to show work to clients, and every Friday it becomes the canteen. That room was the makers' own idea, and it has done more for the place than any other part of the design.

## Noise, dust and fire

Four trades under one roof need rules as well as walls. The joiners' bay has its own dust extraction, ducted out through the roof. The kiln sits in a fire rated corner of bay 2. The print collective, the quietest tenant, took the bay next to the meeting room.

## What it cost

Keeping the [steel frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steel_frame) saved about a third of the cost of a new shed and most of the carbon. Fernside Joinery, in bay 1, made the oak cupboards for our [one room flat in the old mill](/blog/mill-street-flat/). The Ropewalk workshops are full, and three more makers are on the waiting list.
