Hello. If you only have room for one pot of flowers, make it this one. Layered bulbs in one pot flower one after another from late winter to May. Gardeners call it a bulb lasagne, and it takes half an hour on a dry October afternoon.

## How layered bulbs work

Each kind of bulb wants its own depth. Large bulbs, such as tulips, go deepest. Middle-sized bulbs, such as daffodils, go above them. Small bulbs, such as crocus and grape hyacinths, go near the top. Each stem finds its way up past the bulbs above it, so the pot holds three times as many flowers as a pot of one kind.

## Layered bulbs, layer by layer

| Layer | Depth | Bulbs | Flowers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bottom | 20 cm | Tulips, 9 to 12 | April and May |
| Middle | 13 cm | Small daffodils, 9 | March and April |
| Top | 7 cm | Crocus or grape hyacinths, 15 | February and March |

## Planting, step by step

1. Use a pot at least 30 cm deep and wide, with drainage holes.
2. Put 5 cm of compost in the bottom, mixed with a handful of grit.
3. Set the tulips pointed end up, almost touching. Cover them with compost.
4. Add the daffodils, then more compost, then the small bulbs.
5. Fill to 3 cm below the rim and water well.

I top my pots with a layer of [leaf mould](/blog/leaf-mould/), which keeps the compost from drying out. The crocus in the top layer also feed the first bees of spring, as the [late flowers](/blog/late-flowers/) feed the last ones. Squirrels love to dig in a fresh pot, so lay chicken wire over it until the shoots appear.

## When to plant layered bulbs

Plant daffodils and crocus in September or October, at the same time as the [garlic box](/blog/plant-garlic/). Tulips are happier planted later, in October or November, when the cold kills off a disease called tulip fire. A layered pot planted in mid October is a fair middle. The [Garden Organic](https://www.gardenorganic.org.uk/) charity has more on growing bulbs without chemicals.
