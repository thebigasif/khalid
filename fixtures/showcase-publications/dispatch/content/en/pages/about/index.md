The Saturday Gardener is a free weekly letter about growing food and flowers in small spaces: balconies, yards, window boxes and the odd allotment. It goes out every Saturday at 7 in the morning, in time for a weekend job. The Saturday Gardener, its writers and its readers' letters are made up, to show the Dispatch template.

## What each issue holds

Every issue brings one job for the weekend, with the reasons behind it and the steps to do it. Once a month Tobias answers three readers' letters, such as the ones on [slugs and yellow leaves](/blog/slugs-yellow-leaves-shady-wall/). Every method is tested on our own pots first, and we say when one fails.

## Who writes it

- **Maren Holt** started the letter and writes most issues from her balcony.
- **Tobias Okafor** grows on an allotment and answers the readers' letters.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists both with their issues.

## Signing up

The letter is free and always will be. Each email has a link to leave in one click, and we never share addresses. If you would rather not give your email, follow the feed instead. Good issues to start with are [what one balcony grew](/blog/balcony-vegetables/) and [plant garlic now](/blog/plant-garlic/).

The photographs come from photographers who share their work under open licences. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
