# The Saturday Gardener: art direction

The Saturday Gardener is a made-up weekly newsletter about growing food and flowers in small spaces. Its photographs should look like a reader's own pictures of the week's job: one plant, one crop or one tool, close up, in daylight.

## Tone

- **One subject per photograph.** A basket of garlic, a row of tomatoes, a snail on a leaf. No people, and no faces.
- **Daylight and true colour.** Outdoor light or a plain lamp, no heavy filters.
- **Little to read.** The peppers carry small price stickers and the herb planter a small maker's mark, both too small to read at card size. No signs or packets.

## Palette

Greens and earth, with one warm note per photograph:

- leaf greens, from the tomatoes, the basil and the snail's leaves;
- browns and creams, from soil, leaves, garlic skins and stone;
- one warm colour where the subject has it: red peppers, orange tulips, the copper can, a red wheelbarrow.

These sit well beside the sample brand's leaf green on oatmeal.

## Subject mix

Of the 10 photographs, 4 are wide views of a place and 6 are close views of a crop or a tool.

- **Wide (4):** the balcony railing, the greenhouse tomatoes, the wheelbarrow of flowers, the watering can on its wall.
- **Close (6):** the garlic, the snail, the fallen leaves, the tulips, the herb planter, the peppers.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latest issue on the front page | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 4:3 by the template. Most sources are 4:3 already. |
| Issue rows in lists | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | A small picture at the end of each row on a wide screen, under the text on a phone. |
| Lead picture on an issue | as taken, 4:3 or 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Shown uncropped under the title. |
| Writer mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one green on a light tint of the same green. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the latest issue fills the width at 4:3, and each row's picture fills the text column at 3:2. Every subject sits in the middle: the garlic and the peppers fill the frame, the snail and the can sit centred, and the leaves are an even texture.

## Sources and licences

Every photograph comes from the WordPress photo directory under CC0, through `scripts/demo-photo.py`. No photograph needs a visible credit. `photos.lock` records each one, and `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the photo credits page list them all.
