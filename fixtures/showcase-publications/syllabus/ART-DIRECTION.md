# First Washes: art direction

First Washes is a made-up free watercolour course. Its pictures should show watercolour itself: the kit, the paper, the marks a brush makes and finished paintings that a student could learn from. They are teaching material, not decoration.

## Tone

- **Paint is the subject.** Each lead picture shows paint, paper or a finished watercolour. No hands at work, no studio portraits and no faces as the subject.
- **Old masters for the lessons on painting.** The colour and landscape lessons show public domain watercolours by Winslow Homer, John Robert Cozens, Albrecht Dürer and Hercules Brabazon Brabazon, each chosen because it shows the lesson's skill: Dürer's grasses for mixing greens, Homer's blue shadows for shadows without black, Cozens's skies for painting skies.
- **Loose, modern strokes for the first marks.** The brushwork and wash lessons show abstract watercolours by Fons Heijnsbroek, released under CC0: big, free strokes on white paper.
- **Nothing to read.** No labels, captions or signs in the picture. A first candidate for the palette lesson, a museum case with a printed label, was turned down for this reason.

## Palette

The cool blues and soft greys of watercolour skies and water, with the warm ochres, reds and greens of the paintings, on the white of the paper. They sit well beside the sample brand's ultramarine and its pale blue surface.

## Subject mix

Of the 15 pictures, 3 show materials, 4 are modern abstract watercolours and 8 are watercolours by painters of the past.

- **Materials (3):** a box of pans with a brush, a sheet of cold pressed paper, a travel palette.
- **Abstract strokes (4):** loose brush marks, layered strokes, a dark ochre study, orange loops over a red band.
- **Paintings (8):** men watching ships, wild grasses, a boy under a palm, a temple at dusk, a grey mountain sky, trees by a lake, a beach under a grey sky, cottages on a bright shore.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a lesson | as the source, mostly 4:3 or 3:2 | 1600 px wide, or the source's width when smaller | Shown at the text width, about 36rem. Three are upright: the paper, the palette and Dürer's grasses. |
| Tutor mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

The Syllabus template shows no pictures in its lists: the outline is a text list of lessons. Only the lesson page shows the lead picture.

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead picture fills the width at its own ratio, with no crop. The paintings are whole works, so cropping them would change them.
