The landscapes module, the fourth and last module of First Washes, opens on Monday 7 September. Its three lessons come out one a week, as the earlier modules did.

## What is in the landscapes module

- **Lesson 11, [painting skies in one go](/blog/painting-skies/):** a whole sky while the paper is wet.
- **Lesson 12, [trees and distant hills](/blog/trees-distant-hills/):** depth from colour and edges.
- **Lesson 13, [your first finished landscape](/blog/first-landscape/):** one painting from start to end.

## Before it opens

The landscape lessons use everything from the first three modules. If you have time this week, paint a few more [graded washes](/blog/graded-wash/) and one more [green ladder](/blog/mixing-greens/). Students who did found the sky lesson much easier last year.

## What you will need

You need nothing new. A half sheet of 300 gsm paper is a good size for the landscape project, so cut a few before you start. Keep your colour chart from Module 3 close by.

## A break in August

Thank you for your patience over the summer break. Helen and I used it to film the new lessons outdoors, so the light in the videos is real daylight. We think you will see the difference.

## Why landscapes come last

A landscape asks for every skill at once: a clean wash for the sky, soft edges for the distance and mixed greens and shadows for the ground. That is why the landscapes module comes last. If one of those skills still feels shaky, go back to its lesson first; the landscapes module will be there when you are ready.

## Questions

If something in an earlier lesson did not work for you, write to us before the landscapes module opens. We read every message, and the most common questions become short notes under the lesson. Many of the old landscapes we show come from the public domain collections on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).
