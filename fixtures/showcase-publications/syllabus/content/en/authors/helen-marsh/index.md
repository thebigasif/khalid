Helen wrote First Washes and teaches most of its lessons. She paints landscapes and coasts, mostly outdoors and mostly in one sitting. Before the course moved online she taught evening classes for adults who had not painted since school. She believes anyone can lay a clean wash with an hour of practice, and she has seen hundreds of students prove it.
