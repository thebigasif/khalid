First Washes is a free online watercolour course for adults who have never painted, or not since school. It has 13 short lessons in four modules, from choosing your paints to a finished landscape. First Washes and its tutors are made up, to show the Syllabus template.

Each lesson is a short video with written notes and one exercise. Work through them in order: every lesson builds on the one before. Each lesson shows where it sits in the course, so you always know how far you have come.

## The four modules

- [Getting started](/blog/category/getting-started/): your kit, your paper and your first marks.
- [Washes and water](/blog/category/washes/): flat and graded washes, wet in wet, and fixing mistakes.
- [Colour mixing](/blog/category/colour-mixing/): three paints, real greens and shadows without black.
- [Landscapes](/blog/category/landscapes/): skies, depth and your first finished painting.

The [list of every lesson](/blog/series/first-washes/) shows the whole course in order. The best place to start is [lesson 1, your watercolour kit](/blog/watercolour-kit/).

## The tutors

- **Helen Marsh** wrote the course and teaches washes and landscapes.
- **Tomás Reyes** teaches colour mixing and wet in wet.

The [tutors page](/blog/authors/) has more about each of them.

## Course emails

Sign up with the form under any lesson and you get each new lesson by email, plus the link to the monthly [live critique evenings](/blog/live-critique-evenings/). Every email has a link to stop them.

## Pictures on this site

Most lessons show a watercolour by a painter of the past, such as Winslow Homer, John Robert Cozens or Albrecht Dürer, whose work is now in the public domain. The rest are photographs shared under open licences. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
