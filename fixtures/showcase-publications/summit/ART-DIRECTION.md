# Easthaven City Summit: art direction

Easthaven City Summit is a made-up summit on streets, transport, housing and public space. Its photographs show the subject of each session: a street, a square, a bus stop, a harbour, a hall. They look like a town on an ordinary day, not like an advert.

## Tone

- **The place is the subject.** Streets, squares, quays and halls fill the frame. People may appear, going about their day, but no face is the subject of a picture.
- **Plain daylight.** Overcast or clear light, true colour. No night shots, no heavy filters, no dramatic skies. The one hall interior is lit as it is, not staged.
- **Little to read.** Street signs and shop names are small and incidental. A first candidate for the street repair post, a repair crew beside a lorry with a large shop logo, was turned down for this reason, as was an old sepia print with a damaged corner. The hire bikes carry a small scheme mark; it is too small to read at card size.

## Palette

Town colours that sit well beside the sample brand's deep street green:

- the brick reds, greys and creams of streets and squares;
- the greens of street trees, hedges and harbour water;
- one or two bright notes, such as a yellow hire bike or an orange repair truck.

Avoid photographs dominated by purple or neon.

## Subject mix

Of the 13 photographs, 4 are close details and 9 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (4):** a tree pit in a brick pavement, a bench around a young tree, a pothole being patched, rows of folding seats.
- **Wider scenes (9):** a shopping street with bicycles, a bus stop on a long road, a town square, a fishing harbour, flats above shops, plane trees over a square, a street crossing by a market, a concert hall from the stage, a row of hire bikes.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a session or update | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the text width, about 36rem. Sources are 4:3 or close to 3:2, so a 3:2 crop loses little. |
| Speaker mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

The Summit template shows no pictures in its lists: the programme is a timetable. Only the session page shows the lead picture.

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead picture fills the width. Each lead has its subject in the middle third: the tree, the bench, the bus shelter, the boats and the repair truck are near the centre, and the streets and squares look the same at any crop.
