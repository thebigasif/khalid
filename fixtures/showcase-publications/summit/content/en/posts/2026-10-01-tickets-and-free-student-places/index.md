Tickets for May 2027 are on sale today. As every year, we keep prices low and set aside free places, because a summit about towns should be open to the people who live in them.

## Tickets and prices

| Ticket | Price | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Day ticket | 45 pounds | Every session on one day, lunch and coffee |
| Three-day pass | 110 pounds | Every session on all three days, lunch and coffee |
| Student place | Free | Every session on all three days, lunch and coffee |
| Resident place | Free | For Easthaven residents on benefits, every session and lunch |

The two workshops and the harbour walk have small rooms, so you choose them when you book. The keynotes and the panel are open to every ticket.

## Free places

We have 120 free places this year, up from 80. Half are for students at any college or university, and half are for Easthaven residents who receive benefits. You do not need to prove anything to us beyond a student email address or a postcode in town. We trust you.

The free places are paid for by our sponsors and by everyone who buys a three-day pass. Thank you.

## How to book

Book on the tickets page of our site. You get an email with a code for each day. Show the code on your phone or on paper at the door of the first session you go to, and you get a wristband for the day.

If you need help to book, call the summit office on weekday mornings, or come to the [Central Library](/blog/category/central-library/) on Saturdays in October, where Ruth will be at a table in the foyer.

## Access

Every venue is step-free, and the main hall has a [hearing loop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_induction_loop). Every talk is filmed and captioned, and the films go online a month later. Tell us when you book if you need anything else. Our access guide is coming in the spring, with photos of every entrance.

Read the [full programme](/blog/the-programme-is-out/) to plan your days.
