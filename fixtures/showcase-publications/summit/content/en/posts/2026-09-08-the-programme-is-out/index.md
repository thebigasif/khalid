The programme for 2027 is out. Over three days, from Thursday 13 to Saturday 15 May, Easthaven City Summit has ten sessions in four venues on the harbourside: two keynotes, four talks, two workshops, a panel and a walk.

## What is in the summit programme

This year's programme follows one question through three days: what makes a town easier to live in? Each day has a theme.

- **Thursday: streets.** Ines Albarran opens with [streets for staying](/blog/streets-are-for-staying/), then come street trees, a bus map workshop and a panel on who pays for public space.
- **Friday: homes and repairs.** A morning walk along [the old harbour edge](/blog/walking-the-old-harbour-edge/), then talks on housing above the shops and [open data for street repairs](/blog/open-data-for-street-repairs/).
- **Saturday: heat and the neighbourhood.** Cool streets in hot summers, a workshop to sketch your own street, and Ines again with the closing keynote.

Every session has its own page with its day, time, room and format. The front page of this site shows the whole programme by day.

## How we chose it

We had 84 proposals this year, almost twice as many as last year. A group of six chose the programme: two council staff, two residents, a student and me. We looked for sessions that leave you with something to do on Monday morning, not only something to think about.

We also asked every speaker to keep their talk to forty minutes, so there is always time for questions. Last year's feedback was clear on that.

## Four venues, one walk apart

The summit uses four venues, all within ten minutes' walk of each other: the Ropeworks, the Central Library, the Harbour Pavilion and Garden Court. Our [guide to getting to the four venues](/blog/getting-to-the-four-venues/) has maps, step-free routes and bike parking.
