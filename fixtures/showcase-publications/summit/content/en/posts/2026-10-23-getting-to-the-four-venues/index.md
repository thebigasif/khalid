The four venues of the summit are buildings on the Easthaven harbourside, all within ten minutes' walk of each other. This guide covers how to reach them and how to move between sessions.

## The four venues

- **The Ropeworks,** 4 Wharf Lane. Our main venue: the main hall and two studios. A former rope factory, ten minutes on foot from the station.
- **Central Library,** Market Square. The lecture theatre and the old reading room, beside the bus station.
- **Harbour Pavilion,** North Quay. An open-sided stage by the water, at the far end of Wharf Lane.
- **Garden Court,** behind the old customs house on Quay Street. A walled garden with a stage under the trees.

Each venue has its own page on this site with its sessions, by day and time. Start with [the Ropeworks](/blog/category/the-ropeworks/).

## By train and bus

Easthaven station has trains every half hour from the city. From the station, walk down Station Road to Market Square for the library, then along Wharf Lane for the Ropeworks and the pavilion.

Buses 1, 4 and 7 stop at the bus station on Market Square. During the summit, bus 4 runs every ten minutes on Saturday as well as on weekdays.

## By bike

There is covered bike parking for 200 bikes at the Ropeworks and racks at every other venue. The town's hire bikes have docks at the station, on Market Square and on the North Quay. The cycle path along the old railway line ends at the swing bridge, a minute from the pavilion.

## Step-free routes

Every venue is step-free. The route from the library to the Ropeworks is flat, but the cobbles on Quay Street are rough. We have marked a smooth route with orange signs on the day.

## By car

There is no parking at any venue. The station car park is the nearest, and it fills by nine. If you have a blue badge, tell us when you book and we will hold a space near the Ropeworks for you. For the history of the quarter, read about the town's old [ropewalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ropewalk), which gave the Ropeworks its name.
