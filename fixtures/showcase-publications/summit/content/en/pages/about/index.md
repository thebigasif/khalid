Easthaven City Summit is a yearly summit on streets, transport, housing and public space, held over three days each May on the harbourside of Easthaven. Easthaven, the summit and every speaker on this site are made up, to show the Summit template.

The summit began in 2019 with forty people in the library's reading room. Next May we expect about nine hundred over three days.

## What we do

Every session is about one thing: making a town easier to live in for the people who live there. We invite people who do the work, such as council staff, designers and researchers, and ask each of them to leave you with something to do on Monday morning.

Each session has its own page with its day, time, room and format. The [programme](/blog/the-programme-is-out/) explains how this year's sessions fit together, and the [speakers page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone who is speaking.

## Who runs it

- **Ruth Okoye** is the programme director. She chooses the sessions and books the venues.
- A group of six, including residents and a student, helps choose the programme each year.
- About forty volunteers run the doors, the coffee and the bike parking on the day.

The summit is a not-for-profit. Ticket sales and our sponsors pay for the venues, the speakers' travel and the free places.

## The venues

We use four venues within ten minutes' walk of each other: the Ropeworks, the Central Library, the Harbour Pavilion and Garden Court. Our guide to [getting to the four venues](/blog/getting-to-the-four-venues/) has the details.

## How to reach us

Write to hello@example.com and Ruth or a volunteer will answer within a week. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
