Rosa edits Dry Light. She picks each picture, finds out where it was made and what the light was doing, and writes the story behind it. She grew up in Tucson and has walked the washes and ridges of the desert since she was a child. She cares most about the small things: a flower, a track in the sand, an animal standing still.
