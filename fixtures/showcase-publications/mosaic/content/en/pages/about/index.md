Dry Light is a photo blog about the deserts and canyons of the American Southwest. Each post is one picture, or two, with a few short notes on the place, the light and how to make a picture like it. Dry Light and its writers are made up, to show Khalid's Mosaic template.

## Whose photographs these are

Almost every photograph on this site was made by staff of the US National Park Service, at Great Sand Dunes, Joshua Tree, Grand Canyon and Saguaro. Work made by the US government is in the public domain, so anyone may use it. One photograph, of Blue Mesa, was shared under CC0 by its maker. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page links to every original. None of the photographers has any part in this site.

## How the front page works

The newest pictures sit at the top as tiles of different sizes: a large one beside a tall one, a row of three, two halves. The words always sit under each picture, so nothing covers it. Each post lists a few facts beside its title: the park, the place, the light and the season.

## Who writes the notes

- **Rosa Quintero** edits the site and writes about desert plants and animals.
- **Eli Brandt** writes about the canyons, the dunes and the night sky.

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists both, with their posts.

## Finding a picture

The pictures are grouped by kind of place: [Dunes](/blog/category/dunes/), [Night sky](/blog/category/night-sky/), [Canyons and rock](/blog/category/canyons/) and [Desert life](/blog/category/desert-life/). The [archive](/blog/archive/) lists them all, newest first. A good place to start is [first light on the dunes](/blog/first-light-dunes/).
