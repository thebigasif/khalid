Kit runs the Bench Test lab. Trained as an electronics technician, Kit spent eight years repairing studio gear before building our bench: a treated booth, a reference speaker, a light meter, a colour chart and a stack of calibrated test files. Kit takes every measurement in our reviews, writes up how each test works and repeats any test that a maker or a reader questions.
