Signe reviews cameras, lenses and lights. She shot documentaries for ten years, mostly in kitchens, offices and back rooms where a light had to fit on a bookshelf. She cares less about the best image a camera can make on a tripod and more about whether it keeps a face sharp while someone walks and talks. She built our autofocus course and the colour chart we film under every light.
