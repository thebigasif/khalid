Sol reviews microphones, audio interfaces, recorders and headphones. Sol has produced and edited more than 600 podcast episodes, most of them recorded by guests at home on whatever they owned. That is why Sol tests gear in an ordinary room as well as in our treated booth, and why every audio review says how a product sounds when the person using it is not an engineer.
