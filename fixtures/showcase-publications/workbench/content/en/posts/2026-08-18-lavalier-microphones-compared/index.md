The best of the five lavalier microphones we compared is the Thistle W2, a wireless pair that scores 8.7 out of 10. If you can live with a cable, the wired Sorrel Pro sounds just as good for less, and the very cheap Pip L1 is a fair start for a first video.

Lavalier microphones are tiny microphones that clip to clothing a hand's width below the chin. Because they sit so close to the mouth, they hear the voice clearly and the room very little, which is why interviews and news reports use them.

## How we tested them

A presenter wore each lavalier in the same place, clipped to a shirt 20 cm below the chin. They read our script seated and then walked our autofocus course, so we could hear clothing rustle. Three listeners rated the recordings blind. We measured self-noise in the booth and tested the wireless pairs for range outdoors and through one brick wall. [How each test works](/blog/gear-testing/) is set out on our methods page.

## Thistle W2: the best wireless lavalier pair

**Price:** $199 for two transmitters and one receiver, in 2026

The Thistle W2 sounded as clear as the best wired microphone in our test, and it held a clean signal at 20 metres for 30 minutes with no dropouts. Each transmitter clips to a collar on its own, with the microphone built in, or takes a small wired lavalier for a hidden look. Each one also records a backup to its own memory, so a dropout never costs you a take.

:::proscons
+ Voice as clear as the best wired lavalier
+ No dropouts in 30 minutes at 20 metres
+ Backup recording on each transmitter
- Transmitter battery lasts just over 7 hours
- The built-in microphone shows on camera; hiding it needs the wired add-on
:::

## Clover Duo: the longest range

**Price:** $249 for two, in 2026

The Clover Duo reached 220 metres in the open and 45 metres through a wall, the best results in this test, and its transmitters ran for nearly ten hours. Its voice sound was a little thinner than the Thistle W2's, and its self-noise slightly higher. Choose it if you film across large spaces, such as a sports field or a warehouse.

:::proscons
+ Best range: 220 m in the open, 45 m through a wall
+ Longest battery life at 9 h 40 min
- Slightly thinner voice sound
- Costs more than the Thistle W2
:::

## Sorrel Pro: the best wired lavalier

**Price:** $129 in 2026

The Sorrel Pro tied with the Thistle W2 for voice clarity and had the lowest noise and the least clothing rustle of all five. It comes with two plugs, XLR for a recorder and 3.5 mm for a camera, so it works with almost anything. For a seated interview where the cable can run down to a recorder such as the [four-input Heron R4](/blog/heron-r4-review/), nothing in this test sounded better.

:::proscons
+ Lowest self-noise at 18 dB-A
+ Least clothing rustle in our walking test
+ XLR and 3.5 mm plugs in the box
- 1.6 m cable limits movement without an extension
:::

## Vetch Mini: a long cable for small rooms

**Price:** $49 in 2026

The Vetch Mini has the longest cable of the wired microphones, 6 metres, so a presenter can sit across a room from the camera. Its sound was clear but a little hissy, and it picked up more rustle when the presenter moved. It is a fair choice for a fixed desk setup on a budget.

## Pip L1: a first step

**Price:** $29 in 2026

The Pip L1 beats any camera's built-in microphone, and that is its job. It was the noisiest in our test and the most prone to rustle. Treat it as a way to learn where to clip a microphone before you spend more.

## Which lavalier microphone should you buy?

Buy the Thistle W2 if the people you film move around: it is wireless, sounds as good as the best wired model and records a backup. For seated interviews with a recorder, the wired Sorrel Pro sounds just as good for less. Choose the Clover Duo for large spaces.

- **For interviews and vlogs where people move:** the Thistle W2.
- **For filming across a large space:** the Clover Duo.
- **For seated interviews with a recorder:** the Sorrel Pro.
- **For a fixed desk on a budget:** the Vetch Mini.

:::callout{kind=tip}
Clip the microphone to the side of the shirt that the speaker turns towards. If they look left at a guest, clip it on the left, so the mouth stays close to the capsule.
:::
