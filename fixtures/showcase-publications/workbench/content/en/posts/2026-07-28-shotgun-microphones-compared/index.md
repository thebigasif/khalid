Of the three shotgun microphones we compared for on-camera use, the Heath S1 is the best, with a score of 8.6 out of 10. It sounds the clearest at two metres and runs for 140 hours on one battery, while the cheaper Bramble 2 is the one to buy on a tight budget.

Shotgun microphones are long, narrow microphones that hear best in the direction they point. On top of a camera they pick up the person in front of the lens and less of the room around them. We mounted each one on the [mirrorless camera we use for vlogs](/blog/pike-x2-review/) and recorded the same presenter indoors and outdoors.

## How we tested them

Each microphone sat in the camera's shoe and fed its 3.5 mm input. A presenter read our script at one metre and at two metres, indoors in our bare room and outdoors in a light breeze. Three listeners rated the recordings blind. We measured self-noise and side rejection in the booth and ran each one from a full charge, or a fresh battery, until it stopped. [Our full test method](/blog/gear-testing/) has the details.

## Heath S1: the best all-round shotgun microphone

**Price:** $149 in 2026

The Heath S1 gave the clearest voice at two metres in our blind test, with a natural middle and no harsh edge on "s" sounds. It is also the quietest of the three, at 12 dB-A, so pauses stay clean when you raise the level in editing. It runs on one AA battery for 140 hours, which in practice means you change it twice a year.

:::proscons
+ Clearest voice at 2 m in our blind test
+ Quietest of the three at 12 dB-A
+ 140 hours on one AA battery
+ Rigid shock mount that kills handling noise
- No safety track
- The power switch is easy to knock off when packing
:::

## Osier SG: the best at rejecting the room

**Price:** $229 in 2026

The Osier SG is the longest and the most directional of the three, with 14 dB of rejection at the sides. In our bare room it picked up the least echo, and outdoors it ignored traffic to the side better than the others. It also records a second, quieter safety track at -20 dB, so a sudden shout does not ruin a take.

Its length of 24 cm is the catch. On a small camera with the [bright Marten prime](/blog/marten-35mm-review/), the tip of the microphone showed at the top of a wide shot, and it made the camera front-heavy on a small gimbal.

:::proscons
+ Best side rejection: 14 dB at 90 degrees
+ Safety track at -20 dB protects against loud surprises
+ Rechargeable over USB-C
- 24 cm long; can show in wide shots on small cameras
- Battery lasts 52 hours, a third of the Heath S1's
- Most expensive of the three
:::

## Bramble 2: the budget choice

**Price:** $99 in 2026

The Bramble 2 is small, light and a clear step up from any camera's built-in microphone. Its sound is thinner than the other two and its self-noise of 19 dB-A shows as a soft hiss when you boost a quiet speaker. Outdoors, its furry cover let through a slight rumble in a breeze that the others blocked. For a first vlog kit, it is still good value.

:::proscons
+ Cheapest, smallest and lightest of the three
+ Safety track, which is rare at this price
- Audible hiss of 19 dB-A on quiet voices
- Thinner voice sound at 2 m
- Battery lasts 31 hours
:::

## Which shotgun microphone should you buy?

For most people who film on a camera, the Heath S1 is the right choice: it sounds the clearest and one AA battery lasts for months. Pick the Osier SG if you film in noisy places and can live with its length, and the Bramble 2 if you are building a first kit on a small budget.

- **For most people who film on a camera:** the Heath S1.
- **For noisy places, or if you can stand a longer microphone:** the Osier SG.
- **For a first kit on a small budget:** the Bramble 2.

:::callout{kind=tip}
A shotgun microphone on a camera is only as good as the distance to the speaker. At more than two metres, every one of these sounded distant. For a seated interview, a clip-on microphone will beat them all, as our [five lavalier microphones compared](/blog/lavalier-microphones-compared/) shows.
:::

## Verdict

The Heath S1 is the shotgun microphone we would put on our own camera. It gave the clearest voice at two metres, has the lowest noise of the three and runs for 140 hours on a single AA battery. It loses points only for the missing safety track and a power switch that is easy to knock.
