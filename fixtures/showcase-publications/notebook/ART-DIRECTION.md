# Maren Holt: art direction

Maren Holt is a made-up gardener who keeps a public notebook. Her photographs should look like pages of a notebook kept on an allotment: close looks at leaves, seeds, birds and the harvest, in plain daylight. Most notes have no picture at all; the stream is text first.

## Tone

- **The plot is the subject.** Leaves, beans, apples, tomatoes, compost bins, a bird. No people.
- **Plain light.** Daylight, true colour, no filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, labels or packets a reader could read.

## Palette

Leaf greens, earth browns and the red of apples, which sit beside the sample brand's leaf green and the pale sage page. The fieldfare on snow is the one cold picture, and it is labelled as a winter trip in its note.

## Subject mix

Seven of the eleven notes have a photograph. The three reading notes and one bird note have none, as a notebook would.

- **Close details (4):** frost on leaves, dried broad beans, green tomatoes, a fieldfare.
- **Wider scenes (3):** a basket of apples on grass, compost bins against a fence, raised beds of kale.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a note | 4:3 or 3:2, one square | 1600 px wide | Shown at the column's width, about 35rem, at its own shape. |
| Picture in the stream | 3:2 | the same file | Cropped to 3:2 in the middle, at most 22rem wide, under the note's first lines. Every subject sits in the middle, so the crop keeps it. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, never a photograph of a person. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the stream picture fills the text column beside the margin, about 290 px wide, still at 3:2. The lead picture on a note fills the column at its own shape.

## Sources and licences

Every photograph comes from Wikimedia Commons through `scripts/demo-photo.py`, under CC0 or marked public domain, so none needs a visible credit. `photos.lock` records each one, and `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the photo credits page list them all the same.
