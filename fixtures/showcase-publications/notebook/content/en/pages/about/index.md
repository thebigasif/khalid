I am Maren Holt, a gardener in Leeds. This is my public notebook: short notes from my allotment, what I cook from it and links to good reading about soil and wildlife. Maren Holt, the plot and everything on this site are made up, to show Khalid's Notebook template.

## What goes in it

I write a note when something happens on the plot or when I read something worth passing on. Most notes take five minutes to read. They go in four sections: [garden](/blog/category/garden/), [reading](/blog/category/reading/), [birds](/blog/category/birds/) and [kitchen](/blog/category/kitchen/). A reading note ends with a link to the article it is about.

## Who writes here

- **Maren Holt** writes the garden, reading and kitchen notes.
- **Sami Okafor**, who has the plot next to mine, writes the [bird notes](/blog/category/birds/).

## Get in touch

Write to notes@example.com about the seed swap or anything in a note. I answer within a week. The photographs on this site are credited on the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page.
