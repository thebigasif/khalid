## What changes this season

Each episode page now quotes one line from the guest at the top, and every transcript is on the page, folded away until you open it. We also record more often in the guest's own workshop, as we explain in [podcast recording in a workshop](/blog/podcast-recording/).

## How we chose them

We chose the season two guests from almost three hundred suggestions. Because listeners asked for more crafts from outside Europe, half of the guests work in Africa or Asia. We also looked for a mix of ages, so the season has both a hat maker in her twenties and a clockmaker in his seventies. Several guests run small schools or courses, so each episode ends with a way for listeners to learn the craft themselves. More about how we choose is in [our answers to common questions](/blog/listener-questions/).

## How to suggest a guest

Write to hello@example.com with the maker's name, their craft and why you think they would be good to talk to. We read every suggestion, and season three is already filling up.
