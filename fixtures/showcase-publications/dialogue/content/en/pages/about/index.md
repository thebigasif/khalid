Open Studio is an interview show with people who make things by hand. Each episode is one conversation with one guest: a potter, a beekeeper, a boat builder, a weaver. We talk about how they learned, what their days are like, and what their material has taught them. Open Studio, its hosts and its guests are made up, to show the Dialogue template.

## Who hosts the show

- **Nadia Osei** hosts most episodes. She worked in local radio for ten years.
- **Ben Hollis** co-hosts the conversations about wood, stone and repair, and edits every episode.

The [hosts page](/blog/authors/) lists both of us with every conversation we have recorded.

## How we choose guests

We look for people who still do the work with their own hands, whatever the size of their business. Many guests are suggested by listeners. If you know someone we should talk to, write to us at hello@example.com.

## How each episode works

Every episode page names the guest, says who they are and how long the conversation runs, and quotes one line from it. Below that you will find the show notes, the chapters and the full transcript. Episodes sit in three topics: [in the studio](/blog/category/in-the-studio/), [in the yard](/blog/category/in-the-yard/) and [out and about](/blog/category/out-and-about/). News about the show is under [show notes](/blog/category/show-notes/).

A good first episode is [Henrik Dahl on glassblowing](/blog/glassblowing/).
