# Open Studio: art direction

Open Studio is a made-up interview show with people who make things by hand. Its photographs show the guest's work and workplace, never the guest's face: the guests are made up, so a real person's face would put words in a stranger's mouth.

## Tone

- **The work is the subject.** Hands at a wheel, a loom, a torch, a pile of stone, a wall of tools. A person may appear, but turned away, veiled or out of focus.
- **Real workplaces.** Sheds, benches and yards with their mess left in. No staged studio shots.
- **Natural or working light.** Daylight through a window, the glow of a flame. True colour, no heavy filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, logos or text that matter in the picture.

## Palette

Warm, earthy colours that sit well beside the sample brand's deep teal and cool paper:

- the browns, ochres and greys of clay, wood and stone;
- the greens of fields and leaves;
- one bright spot per picture at most, such as a flame or a woven stripe.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 7 show hands or a person at work, 2 show a workplace and 2 show tools or materials.

- **Hands or a person at work (7):** throwing a pot, lampworking glass, a beekeeper at a hive, a loom with its cloth, a bike wheel in a truing stand, a wheelwright's workshop with wheels on the beams, a microphone ready to record.
- **Workplaces (2):** a boat builder's shed, a wall of hand tools.
- **Materials (2):** old cloth-bound books, a dry stone wall.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latest guest | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 4:3 beside the guest's name. |
| Guest row | 1:1 | 1600 px wide | A small square; each subject is centred so the square keeps it. |
| Lead picture on a post | as shot, 3:2 or 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the reading column's width, about 37rem. The loom is a tall 3:4. |
| Host mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one teal line on a light teal tint. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the latest guest's picture fills the width at 4:3, and each guest row's square sits above the name. Every subject sits in the middle third: the hands at the wheel, the flame, the hive, the loom's cloth and the truing stand stay in view in a square crop.
