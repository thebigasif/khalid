# Alderbrook Rivers Trust: art direction

Alderbrook Rivers Trust is a made-up river charity. Its photographs should look like the trust's own work on a real river: the water, the wildlife it helps and the volunteers who do the work, in plain daylight. They show what a gift pays for.

## Tone

- **The river and its wildlife are the subject.** Water, banks, fish, birds and otters fill the frame. People appear at work, from behind or small in the frame, never posed portraits.
- **Plain daylight.** Overcast or clear light, true colour. No sunsets and no heavy filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs or labels in the picture. A first candidate for the clean water appeal, a ranger with an agency badge on her sleeve, was turned down for this reason, as was a picture of an otter on a zoo path.

## Palette

The greens and browns of a lowland river, with the grey and white of moving water. These sit well beside the sample brand's deep river green. The kingfisher's blue and orange is the one bright accent, used once.

## Subject mix

Of the 10 photographs, 4 are wildlife or close details and 6 are wider scenes.

- **Wildlife and details (4):** a salmon leaping a fall, a kingfisher catching a fish, an otter on a log, hands sorting river insects in a tray.
- **Wider scenes (6):** an excavator taking out a weir, volunteers carrying young trees, a new wetland pool, canoes on a clean-up, two people sampling a stream, a clear chalk river.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign and news cards | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 3:2 by the template. Sources are 3:2 or 4:3. |
| Lead picture on a post | as shot | 1600 px wide | Beside the campaign panel on wide screens, at full width on phones. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone each picture fills the width. The salmon, the kingfisher, the otter and the excavator sit in the middle third, so a 3:2 crop keeps them. The river, wetland and planting pictures are even scenes that read the same at any crop.
