Alderbrook Rivers Trust is a small charity that brings the river Alder and its streams back to life. We take out old weirs so fish can swim upstream, plant trees on bare banks, test the water and make room for wildlife, with the farmers, villages and volunteers of the valley. The trust, the Alder valley and every campaign on this site are made up, to show the Beacon template.

Each post on this site is either a campaign, with how much it has raised towards its goal, or news from our work on the river.

## Who works here

- **Ruth Ellery** is our chief executive.
- **Tomasz Nowak** runs our work in the river.
- **Priya Shah** looks after our 1,240 volunteers.

The [team page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with what they have written.

## What we do

Our work falls into four areas: [free-flowing rivers](/blog/category/free-flowing-rivers/), [clean water](/blog/category/clean-water/), [wildlife](/blog/category/wildlife/) and [trees and wetlands](/blog/category/trees-and-wetlands/). Good places to start are our biggest appeal, to [take out Hob Mill weir](/blog/free-hob-mill-weir/), and our yearly report on [where every pound went](/blog/where-every-pound-went/).

## How we spend what you give

For every pound we receive, 84 pence goes straight into work on the river. We publish our accounts every year, and we never sell or share supporters' details.

## How to reach us

Write to hello@example.com and a member of the team answers within a week. To give, see our [donate page](/blog/donate/). The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
