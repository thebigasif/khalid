Thank you for thinking of the river. Every gift, large or small, goes into work on the Alder and its streams. Alderbrook Rivers Trust is made up, to show the Beacon template, so this page takes no money and asks for no details.

## Ways to give

- **A one-off gift.** A real charity would link here to its payment provider, or give its bank details.
- **A monthly gift.** Regular gifts let us plan work years ahead, such as five years of fish surveys after a weir comes out.
- **A gift to one campaign.** Name a campaign, such as the [Hob Mill weir appeal](/blog/free-hob-mill-weir/), and your gift goes to it alone.
- **A gift in your will.** One gift in eight we receive comes from a will.

## What your gift does

- £10 plants two trees on a bare riverbank.
- £25 pays for a metre of willow bank to hold the soil.
- £50 buys a metre of otter ledge under a road bridge.
- £460 gives a village a water testing kit and a trained tester for a year.

## Gift Aid

If you pay UK income tax, Gift Aid adds 25 pence to every pound you give, at no cost to you.

## Questions

Write to hello@example.com, and Ruth or a member of the team will answer within a week. To see how we spent last year's gifts, read [where every pound went](/blog/where-every-pound-went/).
