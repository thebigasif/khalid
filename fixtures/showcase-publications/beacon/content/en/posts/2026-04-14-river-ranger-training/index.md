River ranger training starts again this spring. River rangers are the volunteers who walk a stretch of the Alder each month, test its water, count its wildlife and tell us when something is wrong. We have 86 now, and we need 40 more.

## What you will learn in river ranger training

The training takes four Saturdays in May, from 10 in the morning to 3 in the afternoon. Each day is half indoors and half in the river.

1. **Reading a river:** how water, gravel and plants fit together, and what a healthy stretch looks like.
2. **Water testing:** using a kit to measure phosphate, nitrate and cloudiness, and sending results to the map.
3. **River insects:** catching and naming the small animals that show how clean the water is. Rangers use the [kick sampling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kick_sampling) method, the same one that scientists use.
4. **Leading a group:** keeping volunteers safe in and beside water.

## Who can join

Anyone over 16 who can walk a few kilometres on rough ground. You do not need to know anything about rivers. We lend waders, nets and kits, and we pay travel costs for anyone who needs it.

## What rangers do after training

Each ranger takes a stretch of river near home. Once a month, they walk it, test the water and note what they see. It takes about two hours. Rangers also lead groups on planting days and on our [river clean-up day](/blog/river-clean-up-day/).

Rangers find things nobody else would. Last year, a ranger spotted a broken pipe leaking into Fernley Brook, and this autumn two rangers filmed the first [Brackenford salmon](/blog/brackenford-salmon/) in sixty years.

## What rangers say

Most rangers tell us the same thing: they came for the river and stayed for the people. Many walk their stretch with a friend or a neighbour, and some bring their children. Several rangers have gone on to study ecology, and two now work for other river trusts.

## Book a place

Places are free, and there are 24 on each course. Email volunteer@example.com with your name and the part of the valley you live in.
