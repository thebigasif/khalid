# Small Hours: art direction

Small Hours is a made-up personal journal, written to show the Quill template. It is a text blog first: most posts have no picture, and no list page shows one. The four photographs it does have sit at the top of a post, to set the mood of that one piece.

## Tone

- **Quiet and still.** A pen on a page, a rainy window, an empty shore, a reading room. Nothing posed, no faces in close-up.
- **Low, soft light.** Mist, overcast days, lamplight. True colour, no heavy filters.
- **Room for the words.** Each picture has calm areas of tone, so it sits under a large title without competing with it.
- **Nothing to read at a glance.** Handwriting on a page is fine as texture, but no signs or logos.

## Palette

Muted colours that sit well with the sample brand's oxblood ink and warm paper:

- the greys and pale mauves of mudflats and fog;
- the browns and creams of paper, wood and book spines;
- deep shadow in an indoor scene.

Avoid saturated or bright pictures.

## Subject mix

Of the 4 photographs, 2 are details of writing and reading, and 2 are places.

- **Writing and reading (2):** a fountain pen on a page of handwriting; a rainy window over a table with books.
- **Places (2):** a misty estuary at low tide; a long library reading room.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a post | as shot, 3:2 or 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the column's width, about 40rem, with square corners. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one oxblood line on a light tint. No photographs of people. |

No list, card or front page slot shows a photograph.

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead picture fills the width at its own ratio, so nothing is cropped.

## Sources and licences

Every photograph comes from Wikimedia Commons through `scripts/demo-photo.py`, all four under CC0, so none needs a visible credit. `photos.lock` records each one, and `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the photo credits page list them anyway.
