Small Hours is a personal journal kept by Nell Ashby, a writer who lives in a small town on a tidal river, with reading notes from her brother Kit. Small Hours, Nell, Kit and the town of Saltmere are made up, to show the Quill template.

## What you will find here

- **[Essays](/blog/category/essays/):** longer pieces on walking the estuary, writing by hand and learning to sit still.
- **[Notes](/blog/category/notes/):** short notes from the week, written in one sitting.
- **[Reading](/blog/category/reading/):** Kit's notes on books and on the library where he works.

A good place to start is [why I keep handwritten drafts](/blog/handwritten-drafts/), the first post of the year.

## Who we are

- **Nell Ashby** writes the essays and most of the notes. She has kept a notebook every day since 2009.
- **Kit Ashby** works at the town library and writes the reading notes.

## New posts by email

We post about twice a month. To get each new post by email, write to hello@example.com and we will add you to the list. We send nothing else, and you can leave at any time.

## Photographs

Most posts here have no pictures. The few photographs come from photographers who give their work to the public domain; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
