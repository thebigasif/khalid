# Slow Clay: art direction

Slow Clay is a made-up pottery channel. Its thumbnails should look like a potter's working day: clay on the wheel, glaze, the kiln and finished pots, in warm studio light. They are free photographs, never frames from real videos.

## Tone

- **The clay is the subject.** Hands, pots and kiln shelves fill the frame. A potter may appear at work, but no face is the subject of a picture.
- **Warm, plain light.** Daylight or workshop light, true colour, no heavy filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, labels or text in the picture, since the Channel template never puts words on a thumbnail. The kiln shelf photograph has a small factory sign at its top edge; the 16:9 crop of the cards cuts it off. The camera in the filming post carries its maker's name, small, as a real camera does.

## Palette

The browns, rust and cream of clay, with the pale greens and blues of glaze. They sit well beside the sample brand's fired clay accent and cream page. Avoid photographs dominated by bright red, purple or saturated plastic colours.

## Subject mix

Of the 10 photographs, 5 are close details and 5 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (5):** a hand centring clay, a celadon bowl, a crackled blue mug, a row of cups, a camera beside a tripod.
- **Wider scenes (5):** a pot thrown on an outdoor wheel, a potter at the wheel, glazing a tray of cups, kiln shelves loaded with plates, pots waiting for a firing.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail on a card and on Now showing | 16:9 | 1600 px wide | Cut from the middle. Sources are 4:3 or 3:2, so the cut loses the top and bottom edges only. |
| Video poster and lead picture on a post | the photo's own | 1600 px wide | Shown as the screen, at most 60rem wide. |
| Host mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone each thumbnail fills the width at 16:9. Each photograph has its subject in the middle third: the clay on the wheel, the bowl, the mug and the cups are centred, and the kiln shelves and pots are even rows that look the same at any crop.

## The video files

Each video post plays `clip.mp4`, a four second silent clip of a plain cream frame made for this demo with ffmpeg. It holds no footage and no photograph. The poster the player shows before it plays is the post's lead photograph.
