Slow Clay is a pottery channel from a small studio. Two potters film what they make, one step at a time, so that you can follow along at your own wheel. Slow Clay, its studio and its hosts are made up, to show the Channel template.

Each post on this site is one video with its notes: the steps, the recipe or the numbers, written out under the player. A few posts are written only, such as how we film the wheel.

## Who makes it

- **Noor Hadley** throws, trims and glazes, and hosts most videos.
- **Tomas Ferreira** fires the kiln, films and edits.

The [hosts page](/blog/authors/) lists both with their videos.

## Where to start

New to the wheel? Watch the three parts of [your first bowl](/blog/series/your-first-bowl/) in order, starting with [wedging and centring](/blog/first-bowl-centring-clay/). Then try [three ways to dip glaze](/blog/dip-glaze-three-ways/).

Videos sit in four playlists: [throwing](/blog/category/throwing/), [glazing](/blog/category/glazing/), [kiln days](/blog/category/kiln-days/) and [studio notes](/blog/category/studio-notes/).

## When new videos come out

A new video comes out every other Wednesday. Sign up for the newsletter to get each one by email.

## How to reach us

Write to studio@example.com. The thumbnails on this site are photographs that their makers share under open licences, not frames from our videos; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
