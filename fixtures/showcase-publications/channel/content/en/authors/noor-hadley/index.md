Noor makes stoneware for everyday use: bowls, mugs and plates that go in the dishwasher. She trained at a country pottery, set up her own studio in 2012 and has taught evening wheel classes ever since. On the channel she throws, trims and glazes, and she explains each step the way she would to a new student at the wheel next to her.
