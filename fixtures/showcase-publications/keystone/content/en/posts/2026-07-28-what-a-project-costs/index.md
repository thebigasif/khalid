What a project costs at Keystone Studio depends mostly on how many things we design and how many places they appear. Our last 40 projects, finished between 2024 and 2026, ranged from £2,500 to £48,000. Below are the real ranges by kind of project, what moves a price up or down, and how a quote from us is built.

We publish these numbers because small clients often do not ask. They assume design is out of reach, or they fear a bill that grows. Knowing what a project costs before the first call makes the first call better, for both sides.

## What moves what a project costs

What a project costs grows with the number of things we design and the number of places they must work. It grows less with how "big" the client is. A tiny bakery with a van, bags, boxes and labels needs more design than a large office that wants one sign.

### Things that raise the price

- **More places.** Every extra building, floor or shop means more surveys, more signs and more testing.
- **More people deciding.** A council project with three committees takes more meetings than a shop with one owner.
- **Moving dates.** If the opening day is fixed and the building is late, we work faster and longer.
- **Testing on site.** Worth every penny, but it takes days. We explain [why we test every sign on site](/blog/test-signs-on-site/).

### Ways to keep the price down

- **A clear first workshop.** When everyone agrees early, nothing is redrawn later, and what a project costs stays where the quote put it.
- **Keeping what works.** For [the Fernhill identity](/blog/fernhill-bakery-identity/) we kept the handwritten price cards and saved a whole set of printed ones.
- **One decision maker.** One person who can say yes saves weeks.

## How we quote

Every quote from us has the same four parts, so you can compare it with any other studio's.

| Part | What it covers | How it is priced |
|---|---|---|
| Design | Workshop, ideas, design, proofs, handover | A fixed fee for the agreed list of things |
| Making | Printing, sign making, hosting | Our makers' quotes, passed on at cost |
| Changes | Anything beyond the agreed list | A day rate, agreed in writing before we start |
| Visits | Survey days and fitting days on site | Included within 30 miles; travel at cost beyond |

The design fee is fixed, so what a project costs in design never grows after you agree it. If we underestimate the work, that is our cost, not yours. If you add something to the list, we tell you the price before we do it.

## How you pay

We ask for a third when you agree the quote, a third when you approve the design and a third at handover. On our 2026 terms, for projects under £5,000 we ask for half at the start and half at handover. We never charge for the first conversation, and the [client workshop](/blog/first-client-workshop/) is part of every design fee.

## When we are not the right choice

Sometimes the honest answer to what a project costs is "less than you think, and you do not need us". If you need a simple notice printed, a printer's own designer will do it well. If you need a national campaign, a larger studio will serve you better; we explain why in [why we stay a small design studio](/blog/small-design-studio/).

Design fees in the UK vary widely. The [Design Council](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/) publishes research on the value of design to small businesses, if you want a wider view than ours.
