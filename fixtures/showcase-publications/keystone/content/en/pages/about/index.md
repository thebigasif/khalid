Keystone Studio is a design studio of six people in Eastbridge. We design brand identities, signs and wayfinding, packaging and small websites, almost all of them for clients within an hour of our door: a bakery, a library, a ferry company, a theatre, a hardware shop.

This blog is where we write up our projects and explain how we work. We write it for clients, for students and for anyone who has ever wondered why a sign is where it is.

## Who writes here

- **Mara Quill** founded the studio in 2014 and leads its identity work. She writes about how the studio runs.
- **Tobias Reyne** leads signage and wayfinding. He writes about signs, type and the materials they are made from.
- **Hana Kessel** designs packaging and print. She runs the workshop bench and writes about paper and colour.
- **Dev Hallam** designs and builds the studio's websites and writes about small sites and client workshops.

## What we write about

Our posts fall into three groups. **Projects** are write-ups of finished work, each with its client, year and discipline stated at the top. **Process** posts explain one part of how we work. **Studio** posts are about the studio itself. Good places to start are [how a first workshop runs](/blog/first-client-workshop/), [what a project costs](/blog/what-a-project-costs/) and [the Calder Street Library wayfinding](/blog/calder-street-library-wayfinding/).

## How we check what we publish

A second designer reads every post before it goes live, and the post names them. Every client named in a project write-up has read it and agreed to it. When we give a cost or a time, it comes from our own records, and we say so when a figure is a range rather than a fixed price. Nobody pays to appear here.

## How to start a project

Write to hello@example.com with a few lines about what you need and when. A designer answers within two working days and suggests a first conversation, which is free. The photographs on this blog come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
