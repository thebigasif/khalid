Tobias plans how people find their way through a building, then designs the signs that help them. He spent twelve years on hospitals, stations and council buildings before he joined Keystone Studio in 2017, and he still tests every sign at full size on site before it is made. He writes about wayfinding projects, type for signs and the materials that survive weather, salt and chlorine.
