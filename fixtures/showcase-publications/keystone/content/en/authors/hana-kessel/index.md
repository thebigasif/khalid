Hana designs the studio's packaging and printed work, from cider labels to theatre posters. She trained as a bookbinder before she studied graphic design, so she chooses paper by hand before she chooses it by catalogue. She runs the studio's workshop bench, where every box and label is made up as a mock-up first, and she writes about paper, colour and print.
