Mara started Keystone Studio in 2014 to work for the kind of clients she grew up around: bakers, booksellers, ferry crews and the people who run a town's library. Before that she spent nine years at a city agency, where she learned how much a brand loses between the presentation and the shop counter. She leads the studio's identity work and writes about how the studio runs, what projects cost and what a client should keep when the work is done.
