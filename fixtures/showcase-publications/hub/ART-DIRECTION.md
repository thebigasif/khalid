# Tamsin Hale: art direction

Tamsin Hale is a made-up potter who makes videos. Her photographs should look like a working studio: clay, hands, glaze, the wheel and the kiln, in plain light. They show the making, not a lifestyle.

## Tone

- **The clay is the subject.** Pots, hands at work, buckets of glaze, the kiln. People may appear at work, but no face is the subject of a picture.
- **Plain light.** Workshop light or daylight, true colour. No heavy filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs or labels a reader could read at card size. The kiln's small maker's plate is too small to read.

## Palette

Warm clay colours that sit beside the sample brand's terracotta and the template's warm off-white page:

- the greys, creams and pinks of raw and bisque clay;
- the greens and browns of the studio's glazes;
- the orange of fire in the raku picture.

Avoid photographs dominated by cold blue or purple.

## Subject mix

Of the 9 photographs, 4 are close details and 5 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (4):** glaze being stirred, a glazed bowl in burning sawdust, a cup being dipped, a green bowl from above.
- **Wider scenes (5):** a potter's wheel from above, bowls drying on shelves, an electric kiln, a studio with wheels, a potter shaping a large bowl.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a post | 3:2 or 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the column's width, about 38rem. The kiln is the one upright picture, shown whole. |
| Row picture in a list | 1:1 | the same file | Cropped to a square in the middle, about 88 px. Each subject sits in the middle third, so the square keeps it. |
| Creator photo | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, never a photograph of a person, in an ink ring. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead picture fills the width and the row pictures stay square. The wheel, the bowl, the kiln and the glaze bucket are centred, so the square crop keeps them.
