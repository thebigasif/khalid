I am Tamsin Hale, a potter in Bristol. I make stoneware mugs, cups and bowls, and I film the making of them. Tamsin Hale, the studio and everything on this site are made up, to show Khalid's Hub template.

This site is the one link in my profiles. The front page has my links: the videos, the shop, the workshops and my glaze recipes. Under them are my latest posts. Each video has a post with the steps and timings, so you can follow along at your own wheel.

## The studio

The studio is one room in an old mill yard, with six wheels, a wedging bench, a glaze corner and an electric kiln. It is warm in winter and full of clay dust all year. I trained at a country pottery in Devon for three years before I opened it in 2017.

## Who works here

- **Tamsin Hale** throws every pot, films the videos and teaches the [weekend wheel workshops](/blog/weekend-wheel-workshops/).
- **Dev Rao** mixes the glazes, fires the kiln and writes the posts on [glazes and firing](/blog/category/glazes-and-firing/).

## What I make

I make small batches of mugs, cups and bowls in three glazes: oat white, moss green and iron brown. The recipes are in [mixing my own glazes](/blog/mixing-my-own-glazes/). Pots with small faults go in the [seconds sale](/blog/seconds-sale-green-bowls/).

## Get in touch

Write to studio@example.com for workshops, commissions and wholesale. I answer within a week, faster when the kiln is not on. The photographs on this site are credited on the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page.
