The seconds sale is open until 30 November. It has twenty-two green bowls and fourteen cups, each with a small fault, from £18. Every one is safe to eat and drink from and goes in the dishwasher. The faults are about looks, not use.

## Small faults, safe to use

A second is a pot that came out of the kiln a little different from the rest. In this sale the faults are pinholes in the glaze, a foot that is slightly off centre, or a swirl in the bowl's centre that pooled darker than I planned. You will see each fault in the photo of each pot in the [shop](https://www.example.com/tamsin-hale/shop).

A pot with a crack, a sharp edge or glaze that does not cover the inside never leaves the studio. Those go to the garden as plant pots.

## Why I run a seconds sale

Throwing away a good bowl because of a pinhole feels wrong. Most people cannot see the fault until I point it out, and many like the bowls with a darker swirl best. Selling seconds also lets people buy a handmade bowl for less than half the full price.
