# Wavelength: art direction

## Tone and palette

Clear, bright pictures of the thing each post explains: a sunset sky, a harbour at low tide, a leaf against the light, a cell under a microscope, Earth from orbit. Cool blues and greens lead, with one warm sunset. Science pictures from NASA, NOAA and the NIH sit beside a few photographs from the ground. The brand's indigo and lilac mats frame every picture, so the mix reads as one set of figures.

## Subject mix

- Four views of the sky, space and Earth from orbit.
- Three close views of the natural world: a leaf, ice and mountains.
- Two microscope pictures of cells.
- One harbour at low tide.

No pictures of people. Authors are drawn marks.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | On a phone |
|---|---|---|
| Lead picture of a post (figure 01) | 3:2, as delivered, on its mat | Full width of the column, mat padding kept |
| Figures in the text | As delivered, 3:2 or 4:3, square for the cell | Full width, caption under the picture, never over it |
| Cards on the front page and lists | 3:2, cropped from the centre by the browser | One card a row, picture first |
| Lead explainer on the front page | 3:2 | Picture above the title |

The two square pictures (Earth and the T cell) are kept square: they show a whole round object, and cropping would cut it.
