Wavelength explains how the world works, one question at a time. Each post answers one question, such as why the sky is blue or how lightning forms, in plain words, with numbered figures, the key numbers and a footnote for every source. Wavelength and its writers are made up, to show the Lattice template.

## Who writes it

- **Ines Halvorsen** edits the site and writes about Earth and space.
- **Lena Brandt** writes about physics and chemistry.
- **Tomás Reyes** writes about living things.

The [authors page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their explainers.

## How we check our work

Every explainer is read by a second writer and by a teenager before it goes out. If either one stops to ask what a word means, the word goes in the [glossary](/blog/glossary/), and the post links to it. Every number has a footnote that says where it comes from.
