Six things from the coast this Monday morning. The summer seabird survey is the big one, so it comes first.

## The summer seabird survey is published

Volunteers counted 1,140 pairs of puffins on Merrow Head, up from 980 last year. Kittiwakes held steady and razorbills fell slightly. The trust thinks a good year for sand eels helped, and it will repeat the count next June. [Read the full survey](https://www.example.org/coast-trust/seabird-survey)

## A bench with a view on the cliff path

A new oak bench now stands at the highest point of the Merrow cliff path, paid for by a walking club that has met there for 40 years. On a clear day you can see the islands and the lighthouse from the seat. [See where it stands](https://www.example.com/walks/merrow-bench)

## An early ferry to the islands from November

The island ferry adds a 7:15 sailing on weekdays from 2 November, so workers can reach Brackhaven before 8. The last sailing back stays at 18:30, so a day trip is still easy. [See the new timetable](https://www.example.net/ferry/winter-timetable)

## The fish market's Saturday stall is back

The Saturday stall on the quay returns after the summer break, with crab, mackerel and whatever the boats brought in. It opens at 8 and usually sells out by 11, so go early for crab. [Read the market news](https://www.example.com/brackhaven/fish-market)

## Rangers want your old binoculars

The ranger team collects old binoculars to lend to school groups on the cliff path, and it will clean and repair any pair that still focuses. Drop them at the visitor centre in the dunes car park any day this month. [Give a pair](https://www.example.org/rangers/binoculars)

## How to watch seabirds without disturbing them

A one-page guide from the trust: stay back from cliff edges, keep voices low and never use a drone near the colonies. It also lists the three best places on the coast to watch from a safe distance. [Read the guide](https://www.example.org/coast-trust/seabird-guide)
