Six things from the coast this Wednesday, from a morning of thick fog to a long look ahead.

## Fog keeps boats in harbour

Thick fog closed the harbour mouth for most of Tuesday. The fog signal at Merrow Head sounded for eleven hours. The fishing boats went out again at first light on Wednesday. [Read the harbour log](https://www.example.com/harbour/fog-log)

## A ten-year plan for Brackhaven harbour

The harbour board's draft plan covers a new slipway, more space for small boats and a fish market with a roof. The board wants views by the end of January before it decides anything. [Read the draft plan](https://www.example.com/harbour/ten-year-plan)

## The seal pup count is on 21 November

The count on the Ludden sandbanks starts at first light. The trust now has 46 of the 50 counters it needs. Counters meet in the dunes car park at 7 with warm clothes and a flask. [Sign up to count](https://www.example.org/coast-trust/seal-count)

## A new cold-water swimming guide

The swimming group has written a short guide to staying safe in winter water, from how long to stay in to how to warm up. It says to stay in for one minute for each degree of water temperature, and never alone. [Read the swimming guide](https://www.example.com/ludden-swimmers/winter-guide)

## Lifeboat open day on Saturday

The Brackhaven lifeboat station opens its doors on Saturday from 10 until 3, with boat tours, a rope rescue demonstration and cakes made by the crew. Money raised goes to the station's new engine. [Plan your visit](https://www.example.com/lifeboat/open-day)

## What makes sea fog

The weather desk explains why fog forms when warm, damp air drifts over cold sea, and why it can sit on the coast for days. It also explains why the fog often clears by early afternoon. [Read the explainer](https://www.example.org/weather-desk/sea-fog)
