Six things to know from the week, for anyone who missed a morning brief. The clocks go back tonight, so evenings on the beach get shorter.

## The clocks go back an hour tonight

Sunset on Sunday is at 16:41. The harbour office reminds boat owners to check the new tide tables, which use winter time. Lamps on the harbour wall switch on an hour earlier from tomorrow. [Download the winter tide table](https://www.example.net/tides/winter-table)

## The week's best news: kelp is growing back

Divers found kelp over a third of its old beds off Carrow Point, five years after it vanished. The lab calls it the best news for the coast in years. [Read how kelp returns to Carrow Point](/blog/kelp-returns-carrow-point/)

## The Merrow Head light gets new glass

Repairs start on Monday, with a temporary light on a mast until December. The fog signal keeps working all the way through the repairs. [Read about the Merrow Head light](/blog/merrow-head-light-repairs/)

## New charts for the Wend channel by December

The survey boat finished on Thursday. Until the new charts are out, the harbour office asks boats to keep to the buoys. Two of the sandbanks moved more than fifty metres in the gale. [Read the survey notice](https://www.example.org/harbour/channel-survey)

## Seal pup counters still wanted

The trust has 30 of the 50 counters it needs for the November count on the Ludden sandbanks. Training is one evening at the trust's office, with tea and a short film. [Sign up to count](https://www.example.org/coast-trust/seal-count)

## Coming up next week

The lido meeting is on 12 November, the Sunday bus to Ludden starts tomorrow, and the café at Carrow Point opens its winter porch. Rangers also start building fences in the Ludden dunes on Monday. [See the coast diary](https://www.example.com/diary/november)
