Seven things from the Selwick coast this week, from the first autumn gale to a new crew at the lifeboat station. It takes about two minutes.

## The first autumn gale passed without harm

Gusts reached 70 miles an hour at Merrow Head on Wednesday night. The harbour office reports two loose moorings and no injuries. Crews spent Thursday morning checking boats in the outer harbour, and the slipway is open again. [Read the harbour notice](https://www.example.org/harbour/gale-notice)

## Four new volunteers join the lifeboat crew

Brackhaven's lifeboat station passed four new crew members after a year of training. Two of them run the fish market on weekdays. The station still needs shore crew to help launch the boat, and training starts again in January. [Meet the new crew](https://www.example.com/lifeboat/new-crew)

## Paths through the sandhills reopen

The paths at Ludden Sands that closed for nesting birds in spring are open again until March. Keep dogs on a lead on the boardwalk. Rangers have put down new matting on the steepest stretch, so the climb from the car park is easier. [See the reopened paths](https://www.example.net/ludden/paths)

## A bigger winter for the Carrow Point café

The café at the end of the point will stay open at weekends all winter for the first time, with soup and a heated porch. The owners say the porch has room for twenty people and a rack for wet coats. [Read the café's plans](https://www.example.com/carrow-cafe/winter)

## Beach cleans move to Sunday mornings

The Selwick Coast Trust moves its monthly beach clean to Sunday at 10. Bags and gloves are handed out at the slipway. The trust says the new time suits families, and the tide is out for most of the morning this month. [Join a beach clean](https://www.example.org/coast-trust/beach-cleans)

## Spring tides bring the year's lowest water

Tuesday's low tide is the lowest until March, a good day to see the old fish traps off Hollinmouth. Check the times before you go. Rangers will be on the beach from 11 to show people the traps and explain how they worked. [Check the tide times](https://www.example.net/tides/hollinmouth)
