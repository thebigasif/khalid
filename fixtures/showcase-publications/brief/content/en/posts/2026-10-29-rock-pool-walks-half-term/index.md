Six things from the coast this Thursday, with rock pool walks and plenty more to do while the schools are off.

## Free rock pool walks every low tide

Rangers lead hour-long rock pool walks at Hollinmouth each low tide this half term. Bring boots, not nets. Places are free, but numbers are limited, so book a spot the day before. [See the walk times](https://www.example.org/rangers/rock-pools)

## A public meeting on the old lido

Residents meet on 12 November to talk about what should happen to the closed sea pool at Brackhaven. Three plans are on the table. You can also send your views by email before the meeting if you cannot go. [Read the three plans](https://www.example.com/council/lido-plans)

## A Sunday bus to Ludden Sands

A trial bus runs from Brackhaven station to the dunes car park on Sundays until Easter, every two hours from 9. Bikes go free on the rack at the back, two at a time. [See the bus times](https://www.example.net/buses/ludden-sunday)

## The harbour café wins a local award

The café on the quay won best breakfast in the county food awards, for its crab on toast. The owners say the crab comes straight off the boats at the quay each morning. [Read about the award](https://www.example.com/food-awards/winners)

## What lives in a rock pool, on one page

A clear printable sheet of the 20 animals you are most likely to find in a Selwick rock pool, with drawings. It fits in a coat pocket and works well in a clear plastic sleeve. [Print the sheet](https://www.example.org/rangers/rock-pool-sheet)

## A reminder about the incoming tide

The causeway to Gull Rock floods fast. The lifeboat crew asks walkers to check the tide and leave two hours before high water. Two walkers were cut off there last month and had to wait for the lifeboat. [Check before you cross](https://www.example.com/lifeboat/causeway)
