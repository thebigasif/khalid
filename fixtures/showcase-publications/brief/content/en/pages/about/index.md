Tideline is a short digest about the Selwick coast and the sea beyond it. Every weekday morning it brings six or so things worth knowing, from wildlife counts to ferry times, each in a line or two with a link to read more. On Saturday it brings the week in review.

## Everything here is made up

The Selwick coast is not a real place. Brackhaven, Merrow Head, Carrow Point, Ludden Sands, the Wend estuary, the groups, the people and every story on this site are invented. Tideline exists to show the Brief template, a design for short digest newsletters. Every link in an item goes to a reserved example address, not to a real site.

## Who writes it

- **Ines Calloway** edits the weekday morning briefs.
- **Rory Penhallow** writes the Saturday week in review.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists both, with every issue each has written.

## How to read an issue

Each issue opens with its number, its date and how long it takes to read, then a short row of ticks, one for each item. The items are numbered down the margin. The link at the end of each item takes you to the people who know more. Start with [the latest morning briefs](/blog/category/morning-brief/), or read [how Tideline is made](/blog/making-tideline/).
