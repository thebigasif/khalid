You can change the delivery address of an order for free until your bike leaves our workshop. After that, the courier can usually send it to a new place, but it may take an extra day.

## Before your bike leaves the workshop

1. Sign in to your account and open Orders.
2. Choose the order and select Change address.
3. Type the new delivery address and save it.

You will get an email to confirm the change. If the Change address button is missing, your bike has already left, and the next section applies.

## After your bike has left

Open the link in your tracking email. Choose Redirect and type the new street and town. The courier allows one change per parcel. A redirect to another country is not possible, so contact us instead and we will arrange it.

### Other ways to receive it

The tracking page offers two more choices:

- **A neighbour** who can sign for the bike.
- **A collection shop** near you. A bike box is large, so check that the shop takes big parcels.

## If you are moving house

If you move within 30 days of buying your bike, update your home details in your account too. We send your warranty card and service reminders there.

## Why we ask for a signature

A bike is worth a lot and the box is too big for a letterbox, so the courier needs a signature at the new address too. If nobody will be there, choosing a neighbour or a collection shop on the tracking page is often quicker than a second try.

## A delivery address at work

You can send the bike to your work, as long as someone there can sign for a large box. You can change the day as well, on the same tracking page.

For the rest of the steps, see [tracking your delivery](/blog/delivery-tracking/). If you no longer want the bike, read [returns and refunds](/blog/bike-returns/). The [Wikipedia page on package tracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Package_tracking) explains how a courier handles a redirect.
