Your Brindle leaves our workshop almost built, so bike assembly at home is short. You fit three parts: the handlebar, the pedals and the front light. Everything you need is in the small tool kit taped inside the box, and the whole job takes about 20 minutes.

## Open the box and lift the bike out

Stand the box upright with the arrows pointing up. Cut the tape on the top only, so the knife does not touch the frame. Fold the flaps out and lift the bike by the frame, not by the wheels or the cables. Lay the small parts box on a table where you can see everything.

Keep the box and the foam for the first 30 days. You need them if you [send the bike back for a refund](/blog/bike-returns/).

## Bike assembly step one: the handlebar

1. Take the foam off the stem and the handlebar.
2. Undo the four bolts on the stem's front plate with the 4 mm hex key.
3. Set the handlebar in the stem, centred on the marks, with the cables running forward.
4. Tighten the four bolts a little at a time, in a cross pattern, until the gaps above and below the bar are even.

### Check the handlebar is straight

Stand over the front wheel and look down. The handlebar should sit at a right angle to the wheel. If it does not, loosen the two side bolts on the stem, turn the bar, and tighten them again.

## Fit the pedals

The pedals are marked L and R on the end of the thread. The right pedal turns clockwise to tighten and the left pedal turns anticlockwise. Start each one by hand for a few turns, so the thread does not cross, then tighten it with the 15 mm spanner from the kit.

## Fit the front light

Slide the light onto the bracket under the handlebar until it clicks. Push its cable into the plug by the stem. The light turns on with the bike, so you will test it on your first ride.

## Check the brakes before you ride

Squeeze both brake levers. Each should stop firmly before it touches the handlebar. If one does not, read [how to check your brake pads](/blog/brake-pads/) or ask us. That finishes the bike assembly. Next, [set your saddle height](/blog/saddle-height/) so the bike fits you. If the bike has not arrived yet, [follow your delivery](/blog/delivery-tracking/).
