If your Brindle is not the bike for you, bike returns are free within 30 days of delivery, for a full refund. We collect the bike from your door. You can ride it in that time, as you would on a long test ride.

## Start your return

1. Sign in to your account and open Orders.
2. Choose the order and select Return.
3. Tell us why, in a line or two. It helps us make better bikes.
4. Choose a collection day.

You get an email with a label and a collection window.

## Pack the bike

Use the box and foam it came in. Take the pedals off, turn the handlebar in line with the frame, and put the foam back on the frame tubes. These are the steps of [bike assembly](/blog/bike-assembly/) in reverse. Pack the charger and the tool kit in the small box, and tape the label to the outside.

### If you no longer have the box

Tell us when you start the return. We send a new box and foam for a small fee, taken from your refund.

## Your refund

We check the bike within two working days of it reaching us. Your refund then goes back to the card you paid with within five working days. Normal wear from test rides is fine. We take a fee only for damage, and we tell you before we do.

## Swap instead of return

If you would like a different size or model, choose Swap in place of Return. We deliver the new bike when we collect the old one, so you are never without a bike.

## How bike returns differ from warranty claims

A return is for a bike you do not want. A fault is a warranty claim, and a repair is often quicker than a swap: see [what our warranty cover includes](/blog/warranty-cover/). To have the bike collected from another place, [change the delivery address](/blog/delivery-address/) first. The [Wikipedia page on product returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_return) explains how returns work in general.
