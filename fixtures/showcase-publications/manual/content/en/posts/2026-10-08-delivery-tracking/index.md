Your bike leaves our workshop within two working days of your order. When it does, delivery tracking starts: we send you an email and a text message with a link. Use the link to follow your bike and choose a day that suits you.

## Find your tracking link

Look for an email from Brindle with the subject "Your bike is on its way". If you cannot find it, check your spam folder. The same link is in your account, under Orders, with your order number beside it.

## Choose your delivery day

The delivery tracking page shows the days the courier can come. Pick one and you will get a two hour window by text on the morning of delivery. Someone must be in to sign for the bike, since it is a large parcel.

### If no one is in

The courier leaves a card and tries again the next working day. After two tries, the bike goes back to the courier's depot for five days. You can collect it there, or book a new day on the tracking page.

## If your bike is late

Most bikes arrive within five working days. If yours has not arrived after seven, or the tracking has not moved for two days, contact us with your order number. We will find it with the courier and call you back.

## The delivery tracking stages

- **Packed:** your bike is boxed and waiting for the courier.
- **On its way:** the courier has it, and you can choose a day.
- **Out for delivery:** it is on the van today, with your two hour window.
- **Delivered:** someone has signed for it.

Each stage also comes by text, so you do not need to keep checking the page.

## When it arrives

Check the box for damage before you sign. If the box is badly damaged, write that on the courier's screen and send us a photo. Then start [unboxing and assembly](/blog/bike-assembly/). To send the bike somewhere else, see [how to change a delivery address](/blog/delivery-address/). The [Wikipedia page on package tracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Package_tracking) explains how couriers scan a parcel on its way.
