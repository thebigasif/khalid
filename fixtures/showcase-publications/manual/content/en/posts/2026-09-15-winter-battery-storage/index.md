If you will not ride for a month or more, winter battery storage matters more than anything else you do to the battery. A battery kept full or empty for months loses distance for good. Kept half full in a cool room, it keeps its distance.

## Before you put it away

1. Ride or charge the battery until it shows two or three bars, about half full.
2. Turn the bike off and take the battery out.
3. Wipe it with a dry cloth, and wipe the contacts on the bike too.

## The right room for winter battery storage

Keep the battery indoors, in a dry room between 10 and 20 °C. A shed or a garage gets too cold in winter, and a room with a radiator gets too warm. A cupboard in a hallway is often ideal. Keep it out of direct sun.

### What to avoid

- Do not leave the charger plugged in for weeks.
- Do not store the battery empty. It can fall so low that it will not wake up again.
- Do not leave it in a car, which freezes at night and bakes in the sun.

## Check it every month

Once a month, press the button on the side of the battery. If it shows one bar or less, charge it back up to two or three bars. Put a reminder in your phone, since a month passes fast in winter.

## The bike itself over winter

Store the bike somewhere dry if you can. Wipe it down, oil the chain lightly, and pump the tyres to the top of their range so they keep their shape.

## If you ride all winter

Riding in winter is fine. Charge indoors, fit the battery just before you leave, and expect less distance on cold days. The cells warm up as you ride and give back some of the loss.

## When spring comes

Charge the battery fully, then fit it to the bike.
