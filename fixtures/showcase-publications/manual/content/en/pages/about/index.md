The Brindle Help Centre answers questions from people who own, or are about to own, a Brindle electric bike. Brindle and its bikes are made up, to show Khalid's Manual template.

## Who writes it

The articles are written by the people who build and fix the bikes. [Maya Okafor](/blog/authors/maya-okafor/) leads customer support and writes about charging, orders and the warranty. [Jonas Brandt](/blog/authors/jonas-brandt/) leads the workshop and writes the set up guides and the monthly checks.

## How the articles work

Each article answers one question. It starts with what you need before you begin, then gives the steps in numbered sections. Some articles are steps of a longer guide, such as [setting up your new bike](/blog/series/set-up-your-new-bike/) or [the monthly checks](/blog/series/monthly-checks/). We check every article when a part or a policy changes, and the date at the top says when we last did.

## Contact us

If an article does not answer your question, write to help@example.com. The support team replies within one working day, Monday to Friday. Please give your order number if your question is about an order.
