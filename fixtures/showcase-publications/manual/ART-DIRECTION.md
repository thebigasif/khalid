# Brindle Help Centre: art direction

Brindle is a made-up maker of electric bikes, and this is its help centre. Its pictures should show the part of the bike an article is about, so a reader recognises it on their own bike. They are teaching pictures, not adverts.

## Tone

- **The part is the subject.** The chain, the gears, the brake disc, the front light, a tyre being pumped. Close and clear, in daylight.
- **People only at work or riding, never posing.** A rider crossing a park, someone pumping a tyre on a pavement. No faces as the subject.
- **No other brand's bike as the subject.** Brindle is made up, so its articles do not show a real e-bike maker's frame or logo large.
- **Only CC0 and public domain photographs,** so no picture needs a visible credit. Each is still listed on the photo credits page.

## Palette

Steel greys, black rubber and the warm colours of daylight, with a few bright accents such as a red frame or orange light. They sit well beside the sample brand's teal links, its burnt orange numbers and its stone surface.

## Subject mix

Of the 16 articles, 9 have a lead picture. The rest are short answers about orders, settings and the set up steps, where a picture would add nothing.

- **Parts close up (5):** the rear gears, the chain on a gear wheel, a brake disc with tools, the front light, a front wheel.
- **People at work or riding (2):** a rider in a park, someone pumping a tyre.
- **Places and things (2):** a bike in snow by a rack, parcels on grass.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on an article | as the source, mostly 3:2 or 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the text width, about 36rem. One is near square: the tyre pump. |
| Writer mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

On a phone each lead picture keeps its ratio at the full width of the column, so nothing is cropped.
