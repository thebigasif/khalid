Helen edits The Weekly Meridian and writes most of its housing articles. Before she joined the journal, she spent twelve years at a housing association, where she took schemes from a bare site to the day the first tenants moved in. She writes about who gets to live in a city, what makes a block of flats a good place to grow up, and how other cities pay for the homes they need.
