# Hanna Lowe: art direction

Hanna Lowe is a made-up novelist from the Welsh border. Her photographs stand in for book covers and for the places her books are set: orchards, rivers, coast and quiet rooms full of books. They should feel calm, seasonal and a little misty, like the books.

## Tone

- **Places and things, not people.** Orchards, fields, water, a lamp, a pen, a bookshop aisle. No face is the subject of a picture. Author pictures are drawn marks.
- **Soft, true light.** Fog, frost, low sun and lamplight. No heavy filters.
- **No title on a cover.** A book's picture is its cover art only. The title is set under it in type, so no text sits on a picture. The bookshop aisle shows book spines, which are too small to read at card size.

## Palette

Muted greens, greys and the warm browns of wood and fallen leaves, with one or two warm lights:

- the greys and greens of fog, fields and sea;
- the frost white and pale gold of winter mornings;
- the browns of bookshelves and library desks.

These sit well beside the sample brand's pine green and the template's warm paper. Avoid saturated reds and purples, except the festival tent, which is small on the page.

## Subject mix

Of the 12 photographs, 5 are covers or close details and 7 are wider scenes.

- **Covers and details (5):** a frosty orchard path, a lone cypress in fog, a misty field, a lamp and flowers in a window, a pen on paper.
- **Wider scenes (7):** iced apple trees, a foggy beach, a river in mist, an autumn orchard, a bookshop aisle, a festival tent, a library reading room.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Book cover (shelf, front page, book page) | 2:3 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to the middle. Every cover photo is an even texture or has its subject in the middle, so it crops well. |
| Lead picture on an excerpt, note or event | as shot, about 3:2 or 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the text width, about 38rem. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the covers stand two to a row and the lead pictures fill the width. The front page's cover stands under the opening, at most 22rem wide.
