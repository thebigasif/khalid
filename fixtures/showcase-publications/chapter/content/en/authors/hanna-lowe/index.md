Hanna grew up on a fruit farm near the Welsh border and still lives a few miles from it. Her novels follow families who stay in one valley while the world around them moves on. Before she wrote full time she worked in a cider mill, a county archive and a secondhand bookshop. She writes in the mornings, walks in the afternoons and answers letters from readers on Fridays.
