Hanna Lowe is a novelist and essayist from the Welsh border. She has written two novels, a book of essays and a book of stories, all set in the hills, orchards and river valleys where she grew up. Hanna, her books, her publishers and the places named on this site are made up, to show the Chapter template.

## The books

- [The Orchard Year](/blog/orchard-year-novel/), a novel, 2026.
- [Salt Road](/blog/salt-road/), a novel, 2023.
- [A Field Guide to Leaving](/blog/field-guide-leaving-essays/), essays, 2021.
- [Small Hours](/blog/small-hours/), stories, 2018.

Each book has its own page with what it is about and where to order it. Pages from the books are under [excerpts](/blog/category/excerpts/).

## Events

Priya Nair at Fernhill Press looks after Hanna's readings, launches and festival talks. Every event is listed under [events](/blog/category/events/) with its date, place and how to book. Bookshops, libraries and reading groups who would like Hanna to visit can write to events@example.com.

## Letters

Hanna answers letters from readers on Fridays. Write to hello@example.com, or sign up for her letter, which goes out a few times a year when there is news. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
