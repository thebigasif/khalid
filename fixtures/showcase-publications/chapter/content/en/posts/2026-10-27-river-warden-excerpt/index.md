The river was steaming when Tom came down to it, the way it did on the coldest mornings, as if somewhere under the ice it was still warm. He had been the river warden on this stretch for nineteen years, and he had walked it every day. He knew where the bank was undercut and where the otters had their holt, and he knew the river was higher than it should be for January.

He took the gauge reading and wrote it in the book. Then he wrote it again, because he did not believe it the first time.

## The river warden's warning

At the farm on the bend, Mrs Hale was already out with the dogs. She looked at his face and did not ask.

"How long?" she said.

"Two days. Maybe less if it rains on the hills."

"It always rains on the hills."

He helped her move the ewes to the top field, and then the hay, and then, because she asked, the box of photographs from under the stairs. She did not say what was in them. He did not ask that either.

## What it takes

By dusk the river had filled its banks and started on the meadows. Tom stood on the bridge and watched it carry things past: a gate, a feed sack, half a fence, a child's red boot. A river in flood keeps nothing. It only moves it somewhere else, and leaves you to find it.

He thought of his daughter, who had asked him once why he loved a thing that could do this. He had not had an answer then. He was not sure he had one now.

## About this excerpt

This is from the first draft of Hanna's next novel, which has no title yet. It will change before it is a book. The novel's river warden owes a lot to the people who look after real rivers, and you can read about [river flooding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flood) and how it is measured. For how the book began, see [How a novel starts](/blog/how-novels-start/).

Hanna will read more of the river warden's story at the [winter reading in Ludlow](/blog/winter-reading-ludlow/), the first time any of the new novel is read in public. She is still writing the middle of the book, so the river warden in that reading may already be a little different from the one here.
