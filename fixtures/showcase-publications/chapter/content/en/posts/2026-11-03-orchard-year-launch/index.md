The Orchard Year launch is on Thursday 19 November at Castle Street Books in Hereford, from 7 pm. Hanna will read from the new novel, talk about the year she spent on a cider farm, and sign copies. The evening ends with a glass of cider from the farm where she worked.

## The evening, hour by hour

Doors open at 6.30 pm. Hanna reads for about twenty minutes, then talks with the bookshop's owner and takes questions from the room. After that she signs books for as long as there is a queue. The whole evening runs to about 8.30 pm.

You can buy The Orchard Year on the night, or bring your own copy to sign. If you cannot come, the bookshop will hold a signed copy for you, and you can read the [first chapter](/blog/first-frost/) here now.

## Tickets and access

The ticket price comes off the book if you buy one on the night. Seats are limited, so please book ahead. The shop is on one floor with step-free access, and there is a hearing loop.

## Getting there

Castle Street Books is a ten-minute walk from Hereford station. There is a car park on the river side of the castle green, and bike racks outside the shop. Hereford has a long history as a market town for the [cider apples of the county](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herefordshire), which makes it the right place for this book to begin its life.

If you have questions about the launch, or would like a signed copy posted to you, write to Priya at events@example.com.

## More events this winter

If you cannot make the Orchard Year launch, Hanna also gives a talk at the [Winter Book Weekend](/blog/winter-book-weekend/) in Presteigne, and a free [winter reading in Ludlow](/blog/winter-reading-ludlow/) in December. Every event is listed on the events page with its date and how to book.

## Bring a friend

Launches are friendly, informal evenings, and you do not need to have read the book to enjoy one. Many people come because a friend has a ticket, and leave with a copy of their own. Children are welcome with an adult, and the bookshop has a quiet corner with chairs if the evening runs long. The Orchard Year launch is the first time the book is read aloud in public.
