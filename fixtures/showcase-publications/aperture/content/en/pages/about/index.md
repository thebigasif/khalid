Northlight shows one photograph at a time from the fells, fjords, forests and mountain valleys of the north, with the story behind it. Each post is one picture: where it was made, what the light was doing, and how the camera was set. Northlight and its writers are made up, to show Khalid's Aperture template.

## Whose photographs these are

Every photograph on this site was taken by Simo Räsänen, who shares his work on Wikimedia Commons under the name Ximonic. He publishes it under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, which lets anyone use and adapt a photograph if they credit him and share it under the same licence. Each picture here carries his credit, and the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page links to every original. He has no part in this site.

## The camera details

Under each picture is a plate with four details: the camera, the lens, the exposure and the day the picture was made. They come from the data the camera stored in each original file. Every picture on the site was made with the same camera, a Canon EOS 5D Mark IV, with five lenses from a 14mm to a 70-200mm.

## Who writes the stories

- **Ilona Karhu** edits the site and writes most of the stories.
- **Matti Lehto** writes about the mountains and paths.

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists both, with their stories.

## Finding a picture

The pictures are grouped by place: [Lapland](/blog/category/lapland/), [Norway](/blog/category/norway/), [the Dolomites](/blog/category/dolomites/) and [Southern Finland](/blog/category/southern-finland/). The [archive](/blog/archive/) lists them all, newest first. A good place to start is [the lakes below Saana](/blog/lakes-below-saana/).
