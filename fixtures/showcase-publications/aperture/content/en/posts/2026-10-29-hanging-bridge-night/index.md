Spansdalen is a quiet valley in Lavangen, in the north of Norway. A narrow hanging bridge of planks and wire carries the path across the river Spansdalselva. This picture was made at eighteen minutes past ten at night, in early August, with the sky still pale.

## Standing on the bridge

The picture was made from the first planks of the bridge, looking across. At 16 mm the lens takes in the cables on both sides, which run from the corners of the frame toward the far bank. The planks lead straight to the woods. The still river reflects the hill and the sky, so the picture is almost the same above and below the water.

## A bridge that moves

A hanging bridge moves with every step and every gust. To keep the picture sharp, wait until nobody else is on the bridge and the wind drops. Then hold the camera against a cable and breathe out slowly. The shutter here was 1/13 of a second, slow for a hand-held picture, so the stillness mattered.

## Late light in August

After ten at night the sun was below the hills, but the sky still held plenty of light. The ISO went up to 320 and the aperture stayed at f/13 for depth. There is no hard shadow anywhere in the frame, which suits a calm, green scene like this.

## Finding the hanging bridge

The bridge is on a marked path a short walk from the road into Spansdalen. You can read about [suspension bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_suspension_bridge) like this one. Earlier that evening we climbed [the midnight sun stairs](/blog/midnight-sun-stairs/).

A hanging bridge is one of the simplest kinds of bridge: two cables, a deck of planks and a few wires to hold them together. That simplicity makes it photogenic, because every line in the frame has a job. Take a moment on the hanging bridge before you cross, and look along it rather than across. For another bridge, far bigger and newer, see [Kruunuvuorensilta in evening light](/blog/kruunuvuorensilta-evening-light/), and for still water in a deep valley, [the Geirangerfjord from the Ljøen bend](/blog/geirangerfjord-from-ljoen/).
