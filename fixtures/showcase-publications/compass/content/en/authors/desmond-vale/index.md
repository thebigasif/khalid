Desmond spent twelve years building websites for clients before he moved to teaching. He writes "The web" guides: how a page reaches your screen, HTML, CSS and a first taste of JavaScript. His rule is that every example in a guide must run when a reader pastes it into a blank file, so he tests each one in three browsers before it goes live.
