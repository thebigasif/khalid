Slow Miles is a travel diary by two people who like to go slowly. We travel with one carry-on bag each, we take trains and buses where we can, and we walk the parts in between. Slow Miles and its writers are made up, to show the Itinerary template.

## What you find here

Each trip post is a day-by-day itinerary. It starts with the trip at a glance: how many days, the route, when we went and what it cost the two of us. Then each day, or each pair of days, gets its own heading, so you can copy the plan or take only the parts you like.

The notes are about everything else: [our packing list](/blog/carry-on-packing-list/), [how we plan an itinerary](/blog/travel-itinerary-planning/) and [the night trains we would take again](/blog/night-trains/).

## Who writes it

- **Nadia Brandt** plans the routes and keeps the spending sheet.
- **Tomás Ferreira** chooses the walks and writes the trips on foot.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists both of us with our trips.

## Prices and timetables

We give prices in pounds for two people, at the time of the trip. Timetables change every season, so check the operator's own site before you book.

## How to reach us

Write to hello@example.com. We answer every message, usually from a train. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
