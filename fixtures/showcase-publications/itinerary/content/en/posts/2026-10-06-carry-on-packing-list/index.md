This is the carry-on packing list we have used on every trip in this diary. It fits a 40 litre bag each, which means no checked bags, no waiting at the carousel and no lost luggage. Tick it off as you pack, then close the bag and stop thinking about it.

## Clothes

We pack for one week and wash once. Everything goes with everything else, so three tops make many outfits.

- [ ] Three tops that dry overnight
- [ ] Two pairs of trousers, one that zips into shorts
- [ ] One warm layer and a thin rain jacket
- [ ] Four pairs of socks and underwear
- [ ] Walking shoes, worn on the plane
- [ ] Sandals
- [ ] A hat

## Wash bag

Solid soap and shampoo skip the liquids rule, so the bag goes through security without a second look.

- [ ] Toothbrush and a small tube of toothpaste
- [ ] Solid shampoo and soap in tins
- [ ] Sun cream
- [ ] Blister plasters and painkillers
- [ ] Any medicine you take, in its box

## Papers and money

Keep two cards in two bags, so one lost wallet never ends a trip.

- [ ] Passport, and a photo of it on your phone
- [ ] Two bank cards, kept in different bags
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] A little local cash for the first bus

## The extras on our carry-on packing list

These earn their place on every trip, from the desert to the mountains.

- [ ] A quick-dry towel
- [ ] A water bottle with a filter
- [ ] A power bank and one charger with two ports
- [ ] Earplugs and an eye mask for [night trains](/blog/night-trains/)
- [ ] A cotton bag for shopping and laundry

## What we left behind

We stopped taking a laptop, a second pair of shoes and a guidebook. Every country we visit has a laundry, and every guidebook fits on the phone. For long walks we add to the carry-on packing list in the trip itself, as on our [Svaneti trek](/blog/svaneti-trek/) and the [Soča trail](/blog/soca-trail/). To plan the days themselves, read [how we plan an itinerary](/blog/travel-itinerary-planning/).

Airlines set their own cabin bag sizes, so check yours before you fly. The [UK government's hand luggage rules](https://www.gov.uk/hand-luggage-restrictions) cover liquids on flights from the UK.
