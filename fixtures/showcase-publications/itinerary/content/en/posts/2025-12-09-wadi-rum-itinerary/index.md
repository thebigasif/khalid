This Wadi Rum itinerary fits the desert, Petra and the Red Sea into six days, because the three are only two hours apart in the south of Jordan. We went in November 2025, when the days are warm and the desert nights are cool.

## Day 1: Aqaba

We flew into Aqaba, on the Red Sea, and spent the afternoon at the public beach and the fish market. Aqaba is a working port town, so it is cheaper and calmer than you might expect.

## Days 2 and 3: Wadi Rum

A taxi took us an hour inland to the Wadi Rum village, where the camp's jeep met us. The afternoon was a slow drive between red cliffs to rock bridges and old inscriptions. We slept in a camp of goat hair tents and watched the stars from the sand. On day 3 we walked with a guide up Jebel Burdah to the rock arch at the top, which took most of the morning. Then we rode to Petra in the late afternoon. If you change one thing in this Wadi Rum itinerary, make it two nights in the desert.

## Days 4 and 5: Petra

Buy the two-day pass. On the first day we walked the Siq to the Treasury before eight in the morning and had it almost to ourselves. Then we climbed the 800 steps to the Monastery. On the second day we came in by the back trail from Little Petra and walked to the High Place of Sacrifice.

## Day 6: back to Aqaba

The morning bus from Wadi Musa goes back to Aqaba, and we had one more swim before the flight.
