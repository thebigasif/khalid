A Morocco train trip is easy in the north and the middle of the country. South of Marrakesh the line ends, so buses and shared taxis take over. These are our two weeks in February 2026, from Tangier to Aït Benhaddou, with every fare we paid.

## Days 1 and 2: Tangier

We came by ferry from Spain. Tangier is a good first stop, because it is small enough to walk and its medina is easy to learn. We walked the old town walls and ate grilled sardines by the port.

## Days 3 to 5: Fes

The fast train runs south to Kenitra, and a classic train goes on to Fes. Fes has the largest medina we have seen. We hired a guide for the first morning, then got lost on our own on purpose. On day 5 we took a taxi up to the hills for the view over the roofs.

## Days 6 and 7: Meknes and Volubilis

A short train took us to Meknes, then a shared taxi to the Roman town of Volubilis. We stayed in Moulay Idriss, a white hill town above the ruins, and walked down to the mosaics at opening time.

## Days 8 to 11: Marrakesh

The train from Fes to Marrakesh takes seven hours and crosses the whole middle of the country. It is the longest leg of any Morocco train trip, so bring lunch. In Marrakesh we stayed in a riad near the Bahia palace, walked to the gardens early before the crowds and spent our evenings at the food stalls in the main square.

## Days 12 to 14: the High Atlas and Aït Benhaddou

A shared taxi took us over the Tizi n'Tichka pass, the high road through the Atlas. We slept two nights in Aït Benhaddou, the fortified village of earth houses, and walked to the granary at the top at sunrise. A bus took us back to Marrakesh for the flight home.

## Every fare on our Morocco train trip

Fares are for two people, in pounds, from February 2026.

| Leg | How | Time | Fare for two |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tangier to Fes | Fast train, then classic train | 4 h 30 | 34 |
| Fes to Meknes | Train | 45 min | 5 |
| Meknes to Moulay Idriss | Shared taxi | 40 min | 4 |
| Moulay Idriss to Fes | Shared taxi | 1 h 30 | 9 |
| Fes to Marrakesh | Train | 7 h | 42 |
| Marrakesh to Aït Benhaddou | Shared taxi | 4 h | 30 |
| Aït Benhaddou to Marrakesh | Bus | 4 h 30 | 13 |

The whole trip came to £1,640 for two in February 2026, without flights.
