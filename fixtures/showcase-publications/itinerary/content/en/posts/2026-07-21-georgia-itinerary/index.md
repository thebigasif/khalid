This Georgia itinerary covers ten days and three bases, so you spend more time walking than packing. We used minibuses and one shared taxi, and we walked most afternoons. It is the plan we followed in May 2026, with what each part cost the two of us.

## Days 1 and 2: Tbilisi old town

We arrived on the night bus from the airport and slept until noon. The old town is steep, so we walked it in short loops: the sulphur baths, the cable car up to the fortress and the long walk down through Sololaki for dinner. On day 2 we took the metro to the Dry Bridge market, because the old cameras and Soviet badges there are a museum in themselves. We spent the evening on the guesthouse balcony.

## Days 3 and 4: Kakheti and Sighnaghi

A minibus from Samgori station took two hours to Sighnaghi, a hill town with a long wall and a view over the Alazani valley. We walked the wall at sunset and booked a small family winery for the next morning. On day 4 we walked down to the Bodbe monastery and back up through the vineyards, which took most of the day.

## Day 5: back to Tbilisi

A slow day, on purpose. We took the morning minibus back, did our laundry and bought warm layers for the mountains. Mountain weather in May changes by the hour, and our [carry-on packing list](/blog/carry-on-packing-list/) does not cover snow.

## Days 6 to 8: Kazbegi

The minibus to Stepantsminda leaves Didube station when it is full. The road climbs the old Georgian Military Road past the Ananuri fortress and the ski slopes at Gudauri. On day 7 we walked up to the Gergeti Trinity Church, about 90 minutes each way, and on to the glacier viewpoint while the clouds lifted. Day 8 was the Juta valley by shared taxi, then a long walk to the lakes under the Chaukhi peaks. If you can add a day anywhere in a Georgia itinerary, add it here.

## Days 9 and 10: Mtskheta and home

On the way back we stopped in Mtskheta, the old capital, for its two churches and a quiet lunch by the river. The last night was in Tbilisi again, close to the airport bus.

## What our Georgia itinerary cost

The ten days cost us £1,180 for two in May 2026, without flights. Rooms were about half of it, food a third, and transport less than £60 in May 2026. We would follow the same Georgia itinerary again, with one more night in the mountains. If you have four days more, walk on through Svaneti, as we did on our [Svaneti trek](/blog/svaneti-trek/) two months later.

For the story of the church above Stepantsminda, read the [Gergeti Trinity Church article on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergeti_Trinity_Church).
