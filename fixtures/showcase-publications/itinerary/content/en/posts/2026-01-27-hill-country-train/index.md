The hill country train from Kandy to Ella climbs through tea estates, cloud forest and tunnels for seven hours. We built twelve days in Sri Lanka around it in December 2025, then took buses down to the south coast for the second week.

## Days 1 to 3: Kandy

We landed in Colombo and took the train straight to Kandy. Kandy has the Temple of the Tooth, a lake to walk around in the evening and the botanic gardens at Peradeniya, which deserve a whole morning. We stayed in a guesthouse above the lake, so every walk started downhill.

## Day 4: the hill country train to Ella

Book the reserved second class seats two weeks ahead, or take your chance in unreserved third class, as we did on the way back. Sit on the right side for the best views. The hill country train stops at Nuwara Eliya, the old tea town, and arrives in Ella in the afternoon. Bring water and snacks, because the sellers on board run out by noon.

## Days 5 to 7: Ella

Ella is a village of guesthouses on a ridge. We walked to Little Adam's Peak at sunrise, along the tracks to the Nine Arch Bridge, and down to a tea factory. The tour there ended with the best cup of the trip. On day 7 we rode the hill country train one stop and back, just to cross the bridge.

## Days 8 to 12: the south coast

A bus took us down the hills to Tissamaharama for a morning in Yala, then along the coast to Mirissa and the old fort town of Galle. We swam every day and ate rice and curry at the same small place for five nights. Galle's walls are best at sunset, when the whole town walks them.

## What it cost

The trip cost us £1,260 for two in December 2025, without flights. Trains and buses came to £38 in December 2025. If the day trains are full, there is a night train too; it is on our list of [night trains](/blog/night-trains/) we would take again.
