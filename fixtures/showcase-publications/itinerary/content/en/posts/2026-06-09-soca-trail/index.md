The Soča trail follows the river through the green west of Slovenia, where the water runs a colour you will not believe until you see it. We walked it over three days in June 2026, from Bovec up to the spring at Trenta, a month after our week of [car-free Slovenia](/blog/car-free-slovenia/).

## Day 1: Bovec and the Great Soča Gorge

From Bovec the Soča trail follows the river upstream past the Great Soča Gorge, where the water has cut a slot only a few metres wide. It is an easy 12 kilometres. We swam where the Lepenjica joins the river, briefly, because the water is about nine degrees even in June.

## Day 2: to Trenta

The middle stage crosses the river on long swinging footbridges and passes the old village of Soča and its church. We slept in Trenta, at a farm with a dozen rooms and a dinner of trout and polenta.

## Day 3: the spring and back

A last short climb takes you to the spring of the river, a dark pool in the rock that you reach with a cable handrail. The summer bus back to Bovec stops on the road below, so you need no car at either end of the Soča trail.

## What it cost

Three days cost us £360 for two in June 2026, with two nights at farms and dinner each night. The [Triglav National Park site](https://www.tnp.si/en/) has a map of the whole trail.
