# Slow Miles: art direction

Slow Miles is a made-up travel diary by two writers who go slowly, by train, bus and on foot. Its photographs should look like the places a trip passes through, seen by someone who is there: the landscape, the village, the train. They are a postcard from the day, not an advert.

## Tone

- **The place is the subject.** Mountains, rivers, villages, deserts and the inside of a train. People may be small in the frame, but no face is the subject.
- **Daylight.** Clear or cloudy light and true colour. No heavy filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs or captions in the picture. A first candidate for the planning note, a compass on an old printed map, was turned down because the map is full of words, and because a compass belongs to another template's look.

## Palette

The greens of valleys and tea hills, the blues of rivers and sky, and the warm reds and browns of desert and earth houses. These sit well beside the sample brand's forest green and the template's kraft-paper tickets.

## Subject mix

Of the 10 photographs, 7 are wide landscapes, one per trip, and 3 are scenes for the notes.

- **Trips (7):** the Gergeti church below Mount Kazbek, the towers of Ushguli, Aït Benhaddou, the island in Lake Bled, the Soča river, the Nine Arch Bridge near Ella, the cliffs of Wadi Rum.
- **Notes (3):** a packed rucksack by the door, a sleeper cabin on a night train, fields seen through a train window.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ticket picture in lists | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Cropped by the template to 3:2 at the top of each ticket. |
| Newest trip on the front page | free | 1600 px wide | Fills the start of the wide ticket at full height, at least 22rem tall. |
| Lead picture on a post | as taken | 1600 px wide | Shown at the text width, about 44rem. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone every ticket picture is 3:2 at the full width. Each subject sits in the middle third: the church, the towers, the bridge, the island and the rucksack are centred, and the river, desert and fields are even textures that crop well. The rucksack photo is upright, so its ticket shows the middle of it: the bag itself.
