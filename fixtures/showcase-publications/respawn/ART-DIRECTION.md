# Lowpoly Post: art direction

Lowpoly Post is a made-up games site. Every game, studio and event it covers is made up, so its photographs never show a real game: no screens, box art, logos of games or screenshots. Each review and story shows the kind of place or thing its game is about, as a mood picture, or the people and kit around games.

## Tone

- **Places and things, not games.** A farm under a mountain for a farming game, a nebula for a space game, a library for a puzzle game set in one, kites for a kite game, a gravel rally for a rally game.
- **Daylight and true colour.** Plain light and natural colour. The nebula is a telescope picture in its published colours.
- **Little to read.** No signs or words as the subject. The rally car carries sponsor decals, which are small at card size; a first candidate for the farming review, a farm road with a large "Private road" sign, was turned down for this reason.
- **People at play, not portraits.** The game night picture shows a family over a board game; no face is close up.

## Palette

Bright, varied colour that sits well beside the sample brand's deep magenta on white: green fields, blue sky, the orange and blue of the nebula, the warm wood of the library. Avoid pictures that are mostly magenta or red, which would fight the accent and the score bars.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 7 are wide scenes and 4 are close or medium shots.

- **Wide scenes (7):** a farm under a mountain, a nebula, a domed reading room from above, kites in the sky, a misty lake, a tractor in a snowy valley, rain on a window over a field.
- **Close or medium (4):** a rally car on gravel, a family at a board game, a network switch with cables at an event, an old joystick.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a post | as taken, 3:2 to 16:9 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the post width, about 48rem, uncropped. |
| Lead story and cards on the front page | 16:9 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 16:9 from the centre. |
| Row picture on lists | 16:9 | 1600 px wide | 13rem wide, 7rem on a phone. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn in the brand magenta on a light tint. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead and the cards fill the width at 16:9. Each picture has its subject in the middle: the farm buildings, the rally car, the joystick and the board game are centred, and the nebula, kites, lake, rain and snow are even scenes that look the same at any crop.
