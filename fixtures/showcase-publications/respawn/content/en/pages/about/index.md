Lowpoly Post is a small games site with news, reviews and a calendar of the games coming out soon. Lowpoly Post and every game, studio and event on this site are made up, to show the Respawn template.

## Who writes here

- **Noor Haddad** edits the reviews and writes most of them.
- **Felix Brandt** writes the news and reviews racing and action games.
- **Ines Calloway** writes the guides and runs our monthly game night.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their posts.

## How we review

Every review comes from at least twenty hours with the finished game. Each one ends with a score out of 10, which the health bar beside the title shows at a glance. Our page on [how we score games](/blog/how-we-score-games/) explains what each score means. Good places to start are our reviews of [Cinder Valley](/blog/cinder-valley-review/) and [The Lantern Archive](/blog/lantern-archive-review/).

## How we report news

We check every story with the studio or the organiser before it runs, and we say so when a date is not yet confirmed. The release calendar on the front page lists only dates a studio has announced.

## How to reach us

Write to desk@example.com and someone on the desk answers within two working days. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences, and none of them shows a real game. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
