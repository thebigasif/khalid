Ashfield Games has set a date for the Cinder Valley winter update. Frostfall arrives on 11 December on PC and consoles, and it is free for everyone who owns the game. The studio confirmed the date to us this week.

Frostfall is aimed at the game's weakest season. In our [Cinder Valley review](/blog/cinder-valley-review/), we said winter had too little to do. The studio says it heard the same from many players.

## What Frostfall adds

- **Ice fishing** on the frozen lake above the village, with eleven new fish.
- **A winter market** every Friday, where villagers sell seeds you cannot buy the rest of the year.
- **Greenhouses**, so you can keep a few fields growing through the cold months.
- **Two new village stories**, both told over the winter weeks.

## What it fixes

The update also changes the menus on consoles. Moving seeds between chests will take two steps instead of five, and you can sort a chest with one button. The fast cart, which now unlocks in year two, will unlock at the end of year one.

## Why winter needed work

In most farming games, winter is a rest. Fields freeze, crops stop and the player tidies the barn. Cinder Valley took that idea too far, so a whole season felt like waiting. Frostfall gives each winter week a reason to leave the house, from the Friday market to the fishing holes on the lake. The studio says it tested the update with a group of players who had stopped in their first winter, and most of them played on to spring.

## When and how

Frostfall downloads on its own on 11 December. Saved games carry over, and the new content starts on the first day of the next winter in your game. Frostfall is also on our [release calendar](/blog/) on the front page, with the other games due before spring. If you want to know how winter works in real farming, the [Wikipedia page on overwintering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overwintering) is a good start, and it explains the greenhouse idea Frostfall borrows. We will play Frostfall when it arrives and say whether it changes our score, as our page on [scoring games](/blog/how-we-score-games/) promises.
