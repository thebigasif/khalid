The Hollowmere release date is set at last. Fenlight, the small studio behind it, told us the game is out on 20 November on PC and consoles. It was first due in spring and then in August.

Hollowmere is a slow horror game set in a marsh town that has been half under water for a hundred years. You row between the roofs at dusk, looking for a missing sister, and the fog hides more than water.

## Why the delays

Fenlight told us the delays were about the fog. The studio rebuilt how fog moves across the water so it reacts to your boat and your lantern. In the build we played in September, the fog parted around the bow and closed behind it, and it was unsettling in the best way.

## What we know about Hollowmere so far

- **About eight hours long**, by the studio's estimate.
- **No combat.** You hide, row away or wait.
- **One save slot per game**, which the studio says is part of the mood.
- **A setting to turn off sudden scares**, for players who want the mood without the jumps.

## Who Hollowmere is for

Hollowmere is for players who like dread more than shocks. If you enjoy slow exploration games where the place itself is the story, it should suit you. If you want fights or puzzles, look elsewhere: Fenlight told us there are only a few light puzzles, all about finding a way through the flooded streets. The game draws on old stories about [will-o'-the-wisps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will-o%27-the-wisp), the lights said to lead travellers astray over marshes, and a lantern you carry is your only guide.
