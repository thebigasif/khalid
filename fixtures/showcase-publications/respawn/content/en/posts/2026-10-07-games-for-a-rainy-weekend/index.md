These are our five favourite games for a rainy weekend: calm, kind and short enough to finish before Monday. None of them asks for fast hands, and all of them are happy to wait while you make tea.

## Five games for a rainy weekend, at a glance

| Game | Kind of game | Hours to finish | Players | Our score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Lantern Archive | Puzzle | 12 to 22 | 1 | 9 |
| Mossback Ferry | Story and travel | 6 | 1 | 8.5 |
| Cinder Valley | Farming | 40 or more | 1 | 8.5 |
| Sundial Street | Puzzle | 4 | 1 | 8 |
| Little Lighthouse | Building | 8 | 1 or 2 | 7.5 |

Cinder Valley is the odd one out, since you will not finish it in a weekend. You will, however, finish your first season, and that is a fine place to stop.

## The Lantern Archive

A round library at night where every book is a light puzzle. It is our favourite puzzle game of the year, and the hint system means you are never stuck for long. Read our full [Lantern Archive review](/blog/lantern-archive-review/).

## Mossback Ferry

You run a small ferry on a misty river and carry passengers between six villages. Each one tells you a little of their story on the way. There is no way to fail, and the ending is gentle and sad in the right amounts.

## Cinder Valley

A slow farming game on the slopes of a sleeping volcano, with the deepest soil system we have seen. Start it on a rainy Saturday, and you may still be playing at Christmas. Our [Cinder Valley review](/blog/cinder-valley-review/) explains why.

## Sundial Street

A short puzzle game about one street over one day. You move the sun across the sky to change the shadows, and the shadows open doors. It takes an afternoon and leaves you smiling.

## Little Lighthouse

You build a lighthouse one room at a time on a rock in the sea. A second player can join to fish and fetch wood. It is simple, and the storms are lovely to watch from inside.

## How we chose these games for a rainy weekend

Every game here scored at least 7.5 in our reviews, can be paused at any moment, and has no timers or online play. You can read [our score guide](/blog/how-we-score-games/) for what the scores mean. If the rain lasts into the evening, [Hollowmere](/blog/hollowmere-release-date/) arrives on 20 November for a darker kind of quiet. Studies of [video games and mood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game#Benefits) suggest calm games can help people unwind, which is why we keep coming back to them.
