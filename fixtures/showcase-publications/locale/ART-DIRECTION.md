# The Varrow List: art direction

The Varrow List is a made-up guide to Varrow, a made-up harbour city. Every place in it is made up, so its photographs never show a real business: no shop fronts, signs, menus with names or logos. Each listing shows what the place is about, its food, its goods or its kind of room, and the notes show the weather and streets of a harbour town.

## Tone

- **Things and rooms, not shop fronts.** Fish on ice for the fish hall, oysters for the fish restaurant, loaves for the bakery, a potter's wheel for the studio, old books for the bookshop.
- **Daylight and true colour.** Plain light and natural colour. The bookshop and the wine bar are lit indoors, warm and low.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs or brand names. A first candidate for the bookshop showed a toy brand on a box and a first candidate for the wine bar showed a brand on an ice bucket; both were turned down for this reason.
- **Few people, no portraits.** The only person is the potter, seen from above with no face visible.

## Palette

Warm, varied colour that sits well beside the sample brand's brick red on white: blue harbour water, silver fish, warm wood, green lawns, the purple of aubergines. Avoid pictures that are mostly red or orange, which would fight the accent, the area pins and the price marks.

## Subject mix

Of the 14 photographs, 3 are wide scenes and 11 are close or medium shots, since a guide shows the thing you go for.

- **Wide scenes (3):** boats at a pontoon, a beech tree on a lawn, coloured bike racks against a wall.
- **Close or medium (11):** fish on ice, two coffees on a long table, oysters, loaves in baskets, wine glasses, a potter at the wheel, books on a shelf, a cup of tea, a bowl of noodles, aubergines on a stall, rain on an umbrella.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listing row picture | 1:1 | 1600 px wide | 4.5rem square, cropped from the centre. |
| Note card on the front page | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 3:2 from the centre. |
| Lead picture on a place | as taken, 3:2 to 16:9 | 1600 px wide | Beside the At a glance card, uncropped. |
| Lead picture on a note | as taken | 1600 px wide | At the post width, about 48rem, uncropped. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | A map pin in the brand red on a light tint. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the listing rows keep their small square, and the note cards fill the width at 3:2. Each subject sits in the middle: the cup, the bowl, the plate of oysters and the potter's wheel stay whole in a square crop, and the fish, books, aubergines and rain are even textures that look the same at any crop.
