Tumbledown Teahouse leans against the Hillgate Steps halfway between the market and the hill. Its floors slope, its doors stick and nothing in it matches, and that is why people love it. It serves loose leaf tea, cake and the best scones in Varrow.

## The tea at Tumbledown Teahouse

The menu lists about thirty teas, from strong black breakfast teas to green and smoked teas. Each comes in its own pot with a timer, so it is never stewed. Ask for the tea of the month if you cannot choose.

## Cake and scones

- **Scones** come warm, with jam made in the kitchen and thick cream.
- **The cake of the day** is written on the board by the door. The lemon cake on Saturdays is famous on the hill.
- **Toasted tea bread** with butter is the cheapest thing on the menu and one of the best.

A pot of tea with a scone costs a little more than a coffee and cake in town, which is why it is a two on our price scale. You will not need lunch.

## Getting there

The teahouse is halfway down the Hillgate Steps, on the left as you go down, where the steps turn. From the market, climb about forty steps. From the top of the hill, walk down past the park gate. There is no road to the door, so the only way in is on foot. Bus 2 stops at both ends of the steps.

## Good to know

Closed on Tuesdays. There are steps everywhere, inside and out, so it is not suitable for wheelchairs. The window seats on the first floor have a view down the steps to the harbour.

It pairs well with [Quillon Books](/blog/quillon-books/) on a wet day, or with [Ashlar Gardens](/blog/ashlar-gardens/) on a dry one. The [Wikipedia page on afternoon tea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_(meal)) explains the habit, if you are new to it.
