Hullwright's Table sits in the boat shed at the top of the old slipway, where hulls were once hauled out for repair. It seats thirty people at scrubbed tables, and it is the restaurant we send people to when they ask for one good fish dinner in Varrow.

## What to eat at Hullwright's Table

The menu is written each morning after the kitchen has been to [the market on North Quay](/blog/brinewell-fish-hall/). There are usually five main dishes, and at least three are fish.

- **Oysters** from the beds past the breakwater, served plain with lemon and brown bread. In winter they are the best thing on the menu.
- **The catch of the day,** cooked whole over the grill and served with potatoes and green sauce.
- **Fish soup** on Wednesdays and Thursdays, thick and red, with toast.

There is always one meat dish and one dish without meat or fish. The cooks are happy to talk you through what came in that morning.

## The room and the wine

The shed still has its rails and pulleys overhead, and the big doors open onto the slipway in summer. Book a table by the doors for the sunset.

The wine list is short, about twenty bottles, with several by the glass. The house white is a good match for oysters and fairly priced.

## Getting there

The slipway is at the south end of the Old Harbour, past the last of the fish sheds. Walk along the quay from the ferry stop in about ten minutes, or take bus 2 to the harbour and follow the water. After dark the quay is well lit. Taxis can stop at the top of the slipway, which is the step free way in.

## Good to know

Book ahead for Friday and Saturday. Lunch is quieter and a set lunch of two courses costs less than a main course at dinner. The room is step free from the slipway side.

For a drink before dinner, the [Ninth Kiln Wine Bar](/blog/ninth-kiln-wine-bar/) is a fifteen-minute walk uphill. Read more about [oysters on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oyster) if you are new to them.
