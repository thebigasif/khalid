Ashlar Gardens fills the old stone quarry at the top of Hillgate, where the blocks for the harbour wall were cut. When the quarry closed, the town planted it with trees and lawns, and it is now the calmest green space in Varrow. Entry is free.

## What to do in Ashlar Gardens

- **Climb to the quarry rim** for the best view in town: the harbour, the breakwater and, on a clear day, the islands.
- **Sit under the great beech,** the tree in the middle of the main lawn, planted when the gardens opened.
- **Walk the quarry path,** a loop of about twenty minutes past the old cutting faces.

In autumn the beech turns copper and the lawns fill with leaves. Spring brings bluebells under the trees on the north side.

## For children

There is a play area by the south gate with a sandpit, swings and a climbing frame made from quarry stone. The lawns are good for ball games, and the pond has ducks that expect bread.

## Getting there

The main gate is at the top of Hillgate Street, where bus 2 ends. From the harbour, the Hillgate Steps are the short way up and the hardest: ninety steps and two benches to rest on. The south gate on Quarry Road is flat and step free, and it has the play area and the toilets. Bikes can be left at the rack by the south gate, but not ridden in the gardens.

## Good to know

The gates close at dusk, which is before five in midwinter. There are public toilets by the south gate, open until four. The main paths are step free from the south gate, but the Hillgate Steps entrance has ninety steps.

Bring a picnic from the bakery in Kiln Yard, or walk down to [Tumbledown Teahouse on the steps](/blog/tumbledown-teahouse/) after. The [Wikipedia page on quarry parks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarry) explains how old quarries become gardens like this one.
