Millrace Market fills Mill Square every Saturday morning, between the old mill and the river. About sixty stalls sell vegetables from the farms up the valley, cheese, bread, plants and street food. Entry is free, and it is the liveliest place in Varrow at the weekend.

## What to find at Millrace Market

- **Vegetables** from the valley farms, sold by the people who grew them.
- **Cheese** from the hill farms, the same cheese the [Ninth Kiln Wine Bar](/blog/ninth-kiln-wine-bar/) serves.
- **Street food** along the river side: fritters, flatbreads and the noodle stall run by [Weirside Noodle Bar](/blog/weirside-noodle-bar/).

Come before ten for the best choice. After noon some stalls sell off what is left at a lower price.

## The winter market

From the first Saturday in December to the end of February, the market moves into the old mill. There are fewer stalls, about thirty, but it is dry and warm, and the hours stay the same.

## Getting there

The harbour ferry is the best way to arrive: it runs from the Old Harbour every twenty minutes and stops at the end of the square. Bus 5 stops on Mill Street, two minutes away. There is no parking near the square on Saturdays. On foot, follow the river path from the harbour for twenty minutes.

## Good to know

Most stalls take cards. Bring your own bags. The square is step free, and there are toilets in the mill. The ferry from the Old Harbour stops at the end of the square.

Our note on [getting around Varrow](/blog/getting-around-varrow/) has the ferry times. The [Wikipedia page on farmers' markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farmers%27_market) explains how markets like this one work.
