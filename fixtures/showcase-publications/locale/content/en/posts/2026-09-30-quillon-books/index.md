Quillon Books takes up the ground floor of a tall house on Hillgate Street, three small rooms that lead one into the next. It sells secondhand books, most of them cheaper than a sandwich, and it is the best place in Varrow to lose an hour on a wet afternoon.

## The rooms at Quillon Books

- **The front room** holds fiction and poetry, sorted by author.
- **The middle room** has history, travel and a long shelf of books about the sea.
- **The back room** is for children, with a low table and a box of picture books.

There is a reading chair by the fire in the middle room. The owner does not mind if you sit and read for a while, as long as you put the book back where you found it.

## Books about Varrow

The shelf by the till holds old guides, maps and local histories of Varrow and the coast. A few are rare and priced to match, but most are cheap. If you want to know how the harbour looked before the breakwater, this is the place to ask.

## Getting there

Hillgate Street winds up the hill from the market to the park. Quillon Books is halfway up, at number 41, with a green door and a box of cheap books outside. Bus 2 stops opposite. On foot from the harbour, take the Hillgate Steps and turn right at the top: it is the fourth house along.

## Good to know

The shop buys books on Tuesday mornings. There is a step at the door and the rooms are narrow, so it is hard going with a pram. Card or cash.

After the shop, walk five minutes uphill to [Ashlar Gardens](/blog/ashlar-gardens/) or down the steps to [the teahouse](/blog/tumbledown-teahouse/). On a wet weekend, it is the second stop of our [rainy Sunday plan](/blog/rainy-sunday-varrow/). The [Wikipedia page on antiquarian books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiquarian_book_trade) explains how old books are priced.
