Tarry Rope Coffee fills one end of the old rope works on the quay, a room so long that the rope makers once walked its length to twist each line. Today it holds one wooden table that runs almost the whole way down, and some of the best coffee in Varrow.

## The coffee at Tarry Rope Coffee

The beans come from a small roaster in the Kiln Quarter and change every few weeks. Ask for the filter of the day: it is cheaper than a flat white and usually more interesting. Milk drinks are made well and not too hot.

There is a short breakfast menu until eleven: toast from [the kiln bakery](/blog/saggar-bakehouse/), eggs, and a bowl of porridge with harbour honey.

## A good place to sit

The long table is shared, and that is the charm of the place. You will sit beside fish sellers on their break, students and people with laptops. Nobody hurries you, and there are sockets along the wall.

- **Quietest:** weekdays after two.
- **Busiest:** Saturday mornings, when the market across the road is in full swing.
- **Best seat:** the far end, by the window onto the quay.

## Getting there

Rope Walk runs along the back of the quay, one street up from the water. Tarry Rope Coffee is at the harbour end, by the old capstan. Bus 2 stops at the quay, two minutes away, and the hire bikes have a rack at the corner. If you are coming from Hillgate, walk down the steps and turn left at the bottom: it is the long brick building with green doors.

## Good to know

Dogs are welcome. The toilets are up a steep stair, so there is no step-free toilet. Card only, no cash.

The building has a short history panel by the door. If you want more, the [Wikipedia page on ropewalks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ropewalk) explains why rope works were built so long and narrow.
