People ask about choosing places for The Varrow List, and the answer is short: we go, we pay, and we go again. This note explains the rest, so you know what a listing means before you walk across town for it.

## Choosing places: two visits, our own money

Every place on the list has had at least two visits from one of us, on different days and at different times. A café that is lovely at nine on a Tuesday can be a crush at eleven on a Saturday, and we want to know both.

We pay for everything. No place pays to be listed, and no place knows we are coming. If an owner offers a free coffee after we have written about them, we say thank you and pay for it.

## What we look for when choosing places

- **Something you cannot get anywhere else in town.** The best bread, the oldest bar, the quietest bench.
- **Kind people.** A place that is rude to a stranger does not go on the list, however good the food.
- **Honest prices.** Cheap is not the point. A dear place can earn its place if it is worth the money.

## How to read a listing

Each place shows its kind, its price level, its opening hours and its address in the same order every time. The price level runs from one to four signs:

| Price | What it means |
|---|---|
| £ | Under £10 a head |
| ££ | £10 to £25 a head |
| £££ | £25 to £50 a head |
| ££££ | Over £50 a head |

Parks, markets and galleries that cost nothing to enter say Free.

## Keeping it right

Opening hours change with the seasons, and Varrow changes with them. We check every listing in spring and in autumn, and the date on a listing says when we last checked it. If you find a place closed when we say it should be open, write to us and we will fix it within the week.
