Getting around Varrow without a car is easy. The city is small: you can walk from the Old Harbour to The Weirs in twenty minutes. It is also steep, so it helps to know where the buses, the ferry and the bikes go before you set out.

## On foot

Most of the places on The Varrow List are within a short walk of the harbour. The Hillgate Steps are the quickest way up the hill, but there are ninety of them. The road up, Hillgate Street, is longer and gentler.

## Getting around Varrow by bus, ferry and bike

| Way | Where it goes | How often | Single fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harbour ferry | Old Harbour to The Weirs | Every 20 minutes | £1.50 |
| Bus 2 | Station, Old Harbour, Hillgate | Every 12 minutes | £2.00 |
| Bus 5 | Station, Kiln Quarter, The Weirs | Every 15 minutes | £2.00 |
| Bike hire | Racks in all four areas | Any time | £1 per 30 minutes |

A day ticket covers the buses and the ferry, and it is the best value if you make more than two trips.

## Taking a bike

Hire bikes are in racks at the quay, Kiln Yard, the top of Hillgate Street and the market square in The Weirs. They have electric help, which you will want on the hill. Ride on the road: the old streets of the harbour are too narrow and busy for bikes on the pavement.

## Tickets and times

Buy tickets on the bus, on the ferry or with the city's travel app. Drivers take cards and exact cash. Children under twelve travel free with an adult. The buses run from six in the morning until midnight, and the ferry from seven until nine at night. On Sundays both start two hours later. Our note on [choosing places](/blog/choosing-places/) explains the price signs on each listing.

## Which way to go

- **For a first day,** walk the harbour and take the ferry to [Millrace Market](/blog/millrace-market/).
- **For Hillgate,** take bus 2 up and walk down the steps past the teahouse. On a wet day, follow our [rainy Sunday plan](/blog/rainy-sunday-varrow/).
- **For the Kiln Quarter,** walk from the harbour along Glaze Lane.

Times change in winter, when the ferry runs every half hour. The [Wikipedia page on bicycle sharing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle-sharing_system) explains how the hire bikes work.
