Weirside Noodle Bar is a narrow room on Mill Race with a counter, twelve stools and a window onto the weir. The noodles are pulled by hand in the window, and the broth has been simmering since morning. It is the warmest lunch in Varrow on a wet day.

## What to order at Weirside Noodle Bar

- **Pork broth noodles,** rich and cloudy, with a soft egg and greens.
- **Mushroom broth noodles,** made without meat, just as deep in flavour.
- **Cold sesame noodles** in summer, with cucumber and chilli oil.

Every bowl is big enough for lunch. Add an extra egg or more noodles for a small charge.

## The room

You order at the counter and sit where you can. Most people stay twenty minutes, so a seat comes free quickly even when there is a queue. Watching the cooks stretch the dough is half the fun: a single piece becomes a hundred strands in a few seconds.

## Getting there

Mill Race runs along the river from Mill Square to the weir. The noodle bar is at number 7, the narrow building with steamed-up windows. The harbour ferry stops at Mill Square, three minutes away, and bus 5 stops at the end of the street. In the evening the riverside path is lit, and it is a pleasant twenty-minute walk back to the Old Harbour.

## Good to know

No bookings and no tables for groups larger than four. The room is step free. Card only. It stays open until ten every night, which makes it one of the few places in The Weirs for a late meal.

On Saturdays, walk on to [Millrace Market](/blog/millrace-market/) after lunch. The [Wikipedia page on hand-pulled noodles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamian) explains how the noodles are made.
