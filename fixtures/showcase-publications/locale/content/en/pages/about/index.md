The Varrow List is a small guide to Varrow, a harbour city of old fish sheds, potteries and steep streets. It lists the places we would send a friend to, area by area, each with its opening hours and price level. Varrow, its streets and every place on this site are made up, to show the Locale template.

## Who writes here

- **Tova Lindgren** edits the guide and writes the Old Harbour and Hillgate.
- **Idris Pemberton** writes about food and drink in the Kiln Quarter and The Weirs.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists both of us with every place we have written up.

## How we choose places

We visit every place at least twice before it goes on the list, and we pay our own way. No place pays to be listed. Our note on [how we choose places](/blog/choosing-places/) explains what we look for and how the price level works. Good places to start are [Hullwright's Table](/blog/hullwrights-table/) in the Old Harbour and [Saggar Bakehouse](/blog/saggar-bakehouse/) in the Kiln Quarter.

## How we keep it right

Opening hours change. We check every listing twice a year, in spring and autumn, and the date on a listing says when we last did. If you find a place closed when we say it is open, tell us and we will fix it the same week.

## How to reach us

Write to hello@example.com. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences, and none of them shows a place on this list. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
