# Northlight Parade: art direction

Northlight Parade is a made-up four-piece band from Glasgow. Its pictures should look like a small band's own: record covers that are moods, not portraits, and notes from real rooms, roads and boats.

## Tone

- **Covers are moods.** A release's picture is its cover, so it is a texture or a place at dusk or night: rain on glass, a ferry's wake, lights by dark water, stage smoke, the northern lights. No faces and no words.
- **Notes are places.** A note shows where it happened: a studio, a cellar rehearsal room, a mountain road, a ferry, a crowd. People may appear, small or in silhouette, but no face is the subject of a picture.
- **Nothing to read.** No band names, signs or logos large enough to read. Two first picks were turned down for this reason: a stack of records with readable spines, and a turntable playing a real album with its label in view. The pedal board first chosen for the rehearsal post was turned down for its brand names. The turntable that replaced it carries a maker's name on its headshell, too small to read at card size.

## Palette

Night colours with one warm note:

- the greys and blues of rain, sea and dusk;
- the black of a stage, with white smoke or red light;
- the green of the aurora and the gold of lamps.

These sit well beside the sample brand's violet on warm white. The sleeves' records are drawn in ink, so dark covers read as a set.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 5 are covers and 6 are scenes.

- **Covers (5):** raindrops on glass, a ferry's wake, lights through bare trees by water, hands against stage smoke, the northern lights over a sea loch.
- **Scenes (6):** a studio with a mixing desk, a cellar rehearsal room, a mountain pass with three walkers on a bench, a ferry passing an island, a crowd in red light, a turntable.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Release cover on its sleeve | 1:1 | 1600 px wide | Cropped square from the middle. Every cover is an even texture or has its subject in the middle third. |
| Note card and note lead picture | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the card width and at the text width, about 42rem. |
| Member mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the sleeve fills most of the width, still square, with the record peeking out at the side. The note pictures keep 3:2. The ferry, the bench and the mixing desk sit in the middle of their frames, and the covers are textures that look the same at any crop.
