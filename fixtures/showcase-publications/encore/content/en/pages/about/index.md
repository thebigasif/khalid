Northlight Parade is a four-piece band from Glasgow. We play songs about islands, crossings and cities, with guitars, a lot of drums, and strings when we can afford them. Northlight Parade, Fieldnote Records and every venue on this site are made up, to show the Encore template.

This site is where we post our records, our tour dates and notes from the studio and the road. Each record has its own page with the track list, the formats and where to listen.

## Who we are

- **Isla Moran** sings, plays guitar and writes most of the songs.
- **Tomasz Kowal** plays drums and records and mixes the band.
- **Ruth Adeyemi** plays bass and keys and arranges the strings.

The [band page](/blog/authors/) has a page for each of us, with everything we have written here.

## Our records

We have made two albums, an EP and a live album. The newest is [Glass Weather](/blog/glass-weather/), out in October 2026. Our first album, [Northlight Parade](/blog/northlight-parade/), is the place to start if you are new.

## Booking and press

For shows, festivals and press, write to band@example.com and one of us answers within a week. The tour dates on the front page are always up to date.
