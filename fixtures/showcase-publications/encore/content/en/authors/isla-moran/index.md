Isla sings and plays guitar in Northlight Parade. She grew up on the Isle of Lewis, moved to Glasgow to study, and started the band in a flat on Dumbarton Road in 2019. She writes most of the songs on a cheap nylon-string guitar and keeps the tour diary.
