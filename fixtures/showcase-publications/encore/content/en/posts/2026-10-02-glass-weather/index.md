Glass Weather, our second album, is out today. It has eleven songs, it runs for 44 minutes, and we recorded almost all of it live, the four of us in one room with the doors shut against the rain.

We wrote these songs over two winters, mostly on the night ferries between gigs. So the album keeps coming back to the same things: crossings, windows, and the long dark of a Scottish December. It is quieter than our first record and, we think, braver.

## How we made it

Tomasz recorded the band in a church hall in the West End over twelve days in March. We set up in a circle under the organ loft and played each song until a take felt right, usually by the fourth try. Ruth then added strings and a little brass in two afternoon sessions. Tomasz tells the full story in [his notes from the hall](/blog/church-hall-recording/).

The hall has a long, soft echo, so we used fewer microphones than we planned and let the room do the rest. That is why the drums sound far away on "Machair" and right in your ear on "Copper Wire".

## Formats

- **Vinyl:** one record in a gatefold sleeve, pressed in a run of 800, with a printed insert of the photographs from the sessions.
- **CD:** in a card wallet.
- **Download and streaming:** everywhere, from today.

We explain why the vinyl run is small in [a note about our pressing runs](/blog/small-vinyl-runs/).
