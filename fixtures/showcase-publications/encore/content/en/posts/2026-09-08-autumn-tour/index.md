Our autumn tour for [the new album](/blog/glass-weather/) is on sale today. It is our longest run yet: twelve nights in four weeks, from Glasgow up to Stornoway and across to Belfast, with three ferry crossings and one very long drive through the hills.

## Autumn tour dates

Every date, venue and ticket link is in the tour list on the [front page](/blog/), and we keep it up to date as shows sell out. The short version:

- **Scotland:** Glasgow, Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Inverness and Stornoway.
- **Ireland:** Belfast and Dublin.
- **England:** Newcastle, Manchester, Leeds, Bristol and London.

Inverness sold out in the presale, so we are looking for a second night there.

## What to expect

We play Glass Weather in full in the first half, in order, with Ruth's string players at the bigger shows. After a short break we play the old songs, the ones you know from [the live record](/blog/drill-hall-live-album/), and we finish, as always, with the reel.

## Tickets on the autumn tour

In 2026, tickets cost between 16 and 22 pounds, with a fixed price at every venue and no booking fee on our own link. Under-18s can come to every show with an adult. If a show sells out, keep an eye on the tour list; venues sometimes release a few more on the day.

## Merch

The [ferry single](/blog/night-ferry/) on seven-inch is sold only at these shows, along with the new vinyl and a tour poster printed by a friend in Leith. The poster uses a still from [our film shot on the Oban boat](/blog/night-ferry-film/). We take cards and cash.

## Who comes with us

Ruth's string players join us in Glasgow, Edinburgh, Dublin and London. At the smaller rooms we play as a four-piece, with Ruth on bass and keys at once. Each night a local act opens, chosen by the venue. If you play in one of these towns and would like to open a show, write to us through the about page.

If this is your first time at one of our shows, the [island tour diary](/blog/island-tour-diary/) gives a fair idea of what a night with us is like.

## Travelling to a show

Most of the venues are a short walk from a train or bus station, and we list the nearest stop with each date. If you are coming to Stornoway from the mainland, check the [ferry times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stornoway_Ferry_Terminal) before you book anything. We will be on the same boat.
