Night Ferry is out today, the first single from [our album due in October](/blog/glass-weather/). It is the fastest song on the record and the one we have played most on the road. The single comes with a B-side that is not on the album.

## What is on the Night Ferry single

1. Night Ferry (3:58)
2. Harbour Wall (3:15)

Harbour Wall is a slow instrumental we built from a loop of Ruth's bass and a field recording of a ferry's engine room. It did not fit the album, but we liked it too much to leave it in a drawer.

## Where the song came from

Isla wrote Night Ferry on the late crossing from Ullapool after a show in Stornoway. The boat was nearly empty, the café was shut, and the only sound was the engines. She had the chords before we docked and the whole song a week later.

In the studio we tried it slow first, as a ballad. It did not work. So we played it at the speed we play it live, with the drums pushing ahead of the beat, and kept the third take.

## The B-side

Harbour Wall began as a sound check. Ruth left a bass loop running while Tomasz moved microphones, and the loop sounded better than anything we played that day. We added the engine room recording, a little piano and nothing else. It is the first time a B-side has made the whole band agree.

## The video

The single comes with a short video, shot on a real crossing with a borrowed camera. Ruth tells the story in [how we shot the video on deck](/blog/night-ferry-film/).

## Formats

- **Download and streaming:** everywhere from today.
- **Seven-inch single:** a run of 300, sold only at [our November shows](/blog/autumn-tour/). It comes in a plain card sleeve with a hand-stamped label, and each copy is numbered.

Night Ferry was mixed by Tomasz in the band's [cellar where we practise](/blog/rehearsal-room/). If you want to know what a [B-side](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-side_and_B-side) is, it is the second song on a single, and often the band's favourite.
