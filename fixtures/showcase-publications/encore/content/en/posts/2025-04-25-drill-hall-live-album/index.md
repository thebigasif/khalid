Our first live album, Live at the Drill Hall, is out today. It is the last night of our 2024 tour, at an old drill hall on the east coast, recorded straight from the mixing desk with two microphones hung over the crowd.

## Why a live album now

People kept telling us the songs sound different live: faster, louder and less polite. They were right. So on the last night of the tour we brought a recorder and hoped for a good show. It was the best night of the year, and nothing broke.

## Track list

1. Northlight (4:20)
2. Paper Boats (3:44)
3. Lewis in the Rain (5:02)
4. Coal Road (3:31)
5. Station Clock (4:10)
6. The Long Way Round (6:48)
7. Small Hours (4:05)
8. Last Orders (3:12)
9. Reel for the Van (2:56)

## How we recorded the live album

There was no truck and no engineer. The sound engineer at the hall fed the desk into my laptop, and I hung two microphones from the balcony to catch the room and the crowd. Back home I mixed it in our [cellar under the arches](/blog/rehearsal-room/) in three evenings. I fixed nothing. The wrong note in "Coal Road" stays, and so does the crowd singing the chorus of "Paper Boats" back at us.

The last song, "Reel for the Van", is a set of fast tunes Ruth brought from her ceilidh band days. We end every show with it now.

## Formats

- **Double vinyl:** a run of 600, with the tour poster printed inside the sleeve.
- **Download and streaming:** everywhere.

## Hear the songs where they started

Most of these songs first came out on [our 2024 debut](/blog/northlight-parade/). If you want to hear them live yourself, the [twelve autumn shows](/blog/autumn-tour/) are listed on the front page. This is a [live album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_album) in the old sense: one night, in order, with nothing added.
