Northlight Parade, our first album, is out today. It has the band's name because we could not agree on anything else. It has ten songs about leaving the islands for the city and finding your people there.

## The songs on Northlight Parade

1. Northlight (4:01)
2. Paper Boats (3:30)
3. Lewis in the Rain (4:48)
4. Coal Road (3:19)
5. Station Clock (3:55)
6. Tenement Summer (3:37)
7. The Long Way Round (5:52)
8. Small Hours (3:58)
9. Second Hand Piano (2:44)
10. Last Orders (3:06)

## How it was made

We had a week and very little money. A friend lent us a converted mill outside Paisley, and Tomasz set up his gear in the old loading bay. We slept on the floor and recorded from noon until the neighbours complained. The piano on "Second Hand Piano" really was second-hand; it cost forty pounds and two of its keys do not work.

I wrote most of these songs in my first two years in Glasgow, homesick and broke. Hearing them back now, they sound braver than I felt at the time.

The songs follow one year in order. "Northlight" is the night I left Stornoway on the ferry, and "Last Orders" is the first Christmas I spent in the city instead of going home. In between are the jobs, the flats and the friends who became the band. Tomasz wanted to shuffle the order for the record. I said no, and I still think I was right.

We mixed the album in Tomasz's bedroom over the following month, on headphones, with the window open for air. Some of the hiss you hear is the street outside. We sent it to Fieldnote Records on a memory stick and heard back the same evening.
