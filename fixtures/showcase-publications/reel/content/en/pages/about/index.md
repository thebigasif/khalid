Tenth Row reviews new films, TV series and documentaries every week. Each review gives a rating out of five stars, the cast, who made it and where to watch it. Tenth Row, its critics and every film, series, actor and streaming service on this site are made up, to show the Reel template.

## Who writes here

- **Nadia Brennan** edits the site and reviews most new films.
- **Tomás Reyes** reviews TV series, always after the whole season.
- **Ines Varga** reviews documentaries and writes our features and watch lists.

The [critics page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their reviews.

## How we rate

Every review ends on a rating out of five stars. Half stars are allowed.

- **5 stars:** one of the best films or series of the year. Go out of your way to see it.
- **4 stars:** very good, with small faults. Easy to recommend.
- **3 stars:** worth your time if the subject or the cast appeals.
- **2 stars:** has something good in it, but mostly does not work.
- **1 star:** we would not recommend it to anyone.

A critic watches the whole film or the whole season before rating it. Where to watch is checked on the day the review goes up, and we update it when a film moves to another service.

## What we do not do

Studios and services do not pay for reviews, and they do not see a review before it is published. When a critic has worked on a film or knows its makers, the review says so in its first paragraph.

## How to reach us

Write to desk@example.com and a critic answers within a week. The photographs on this site are mood pictures, not stills from the films; they come from photographers who share their work under open licences, and the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
