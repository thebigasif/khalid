# Tenth Row: art direction

Tenth Row is a made-up site of film and TV reviews. Every film, series and actor it names is made up, so it has no posters or stills. Each review shows a mood picture instead: a real photograph of a place or thing that the made-up film could have been shot in or about. A lighthouse in a storm stands for a lighthouse drama, a nebula for a space film, a laundromat for a sitcom set in one.

## Tone

- **A frame from a film that does not exist.** Each picture should look like it could be a still: a strong subject, a clear mood and a sense of place.
- **Night, dusk and weather.** Low light, fog, snow and storms suit a cinema programme. Daylight is fine where the film is set in it, such as the desert road movie.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, captions or labels. A sleeper car with its company name, an engraving with a printed caption and a diner photograph with a survey number and an exit sign were turned down for this reason. The laundromat's machines carry small instruction panels that are too small to read at card size.
- **No faces.** Two tiny figures sit in the cinema photograph; no person is the subject of a picture.

## Palette

Deep blues and blacks of night, with warm accents: the gold of a dusk sky, the red of autumn leaves, the copper and green of an old mill. These sit well beside the cinema red of the template and the warm paper of the page.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 6 are wide scenes and 5 are closer details or interiors.

- **Wide scenes (6):** a lighthouse in a storm, a nebula, a foggy forest, desert badlands, a snowy village track at dusk, a glasshouse in autumn.
- **Details and interiors (5):** a ferry deck lit blue at night, a rusted vat in a closed paper mill, a row of washing machines, a cinema auditorium, a film projector.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a post and Now showing | 21:9 | 1600 px wide | The letterbox frame crops the middle band of the picture, between two ink bars. |
| Poster card | 2:3 | the same file | The cards crop the middle third of the picture, so each subject sits near the centre. |
| Critic mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the letterbox fills the width and the poster cards sit two across. The lighthouse photograph was cropped from the left to 1200 by 1067 px so the lighthouse stays inside the 2:3 poster crop. The nebula, forest, desert, snow and laundromat pictures are even textures that read the same at any crop.
