Larkfield Nursery is a family plant nursery on Mill Lane. Ruth's parents opened it in 1968 with one greenhouse and a sign at the gate, and three generations of the family have run it since. The nursery, the village and everyone on this site are made up, to show the Hearth template.

We grow most of what we sell ourselves, in four greenhouses and an old orchard: bedding plants, herbs, fruit trees, bulbs and plants for bees.

## Who writes here

- **Ruth Hale** owns the nursery and writes its news.
- **Tom Hale**, her son, grows the plants and writes about how.
- **Esi Mensah** runs the shop and writes plant notes for beginners.

The [family page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with what they have written.

## What we write about

Our posts fall into three topics: [jobs in the garden](/blog/category/in-the-garden/), [growing food](/blog/category/growing-food/) and news [from the nursery](/blog/category/from-the-nursery/). Good places to start are [our apple day](/blog/apple-day/) and the story of [three generations of a family nursery](/blog/family-nursery/).

## Visiting

We are open every day but Monday, from nine till five, and until four from November to February. There is free parking in the field by the gate, and the paths are flat. Dogs on leads are welcome.

Write to hello@example.com and one of us will answer within a few days. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
