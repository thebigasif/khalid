# Oven Spring: art direction

Oven Spring is a made-up home baking blog. Its photographs should look like real bakes in real kitchens: a loaf cooling on a rack, cookies in a bowl, a pie on the table. They show what the recipe makes, close enough to see the crumb and the crust.

## Tone

- **The bake is the subject.** One bake fills most of the frame. Hands may appear, but no faces.
- **Home settings.** Worktops, wooden boards, plates, cooling racks and tins. Café plates are fine when the bake fills the frame.
- **Warm daylight.** Soft window light, true colour, browns and golds that read as baked, not burnt.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, logos or packaging text in the picture.

## Palette

Warm, floury colours that sit well with the sample brand's cherry and butter:

- the golds and browns of crusts, crumbs and wooden boards;
- the whites and creams of flour, plates, icing and cloths;
- small spots of colour from fruit, such as blueberries, lemons and apples.

One picture, the shortbread on a red checked cloth, carries strong red; it sits apart from the others on the page.

## Subject mix

Of the 12 photographs, 10 show a finished bake and 2 show baking in progress.

- **Finished bakes (10):** a sourdough loaf on a rack, a tin loaf on a board, a cinnamon bun, a lemon loaf, a slice of banana bread, cookies in a bowl, shortbread fingers, a tray of brownies, an apple pie, iced scones.
- **Baking in progress (2):** ingredients set out on a worktop from above, a hand lifting dough from a bowl.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Index card | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 4:3 in the card. Most sources are 4:3 or 3:2; three are portrait and crop to their middle. |
| Fresh from the oven | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | The newest recipe, about 730 px wide beside its text on a wide screen. |
| Lead picture on a post | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Beside the title and ticket on wide screens, full width on a phone. |
| Baker mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one cherry line on a light cherry tint. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone a card's picture fills the width at 4:3. Each bake sits near the middle: the loaf, the bun, the bowl of cookies and the pie are centred, and the portrait shots of the pie, the worktop and the dough crop to their middle third, where the subject is.
