This chocolate banana bread is a one-bowl cake. It is the best use for the bananas that went black in the fruit bowl, and the riper they are, the better it tastes.

Bake it on a Sunday and slice it through the week. It is lovely toasted with butter. For a lighter loaf cake, see our [lemon and lavender drizzle loaf](/blog/lemon-lavender-loaf/).

## Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas, about 350 g peeled
- 100 g melted butter
- 120 g light brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 220 g plain flour
- 1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
- 100 g dark chocolate, chopped

## Method

1. **Prepare.** Heat the oven to 170°C and line a 900 g loaf tin.
2. **Mash.** Mash the bananas in a large bowl until almost smooth.
3. **Mix the wet.** Stir in the butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla.
4. **Add the dry.** Sift over the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt and fold until just mixed. Fold in most of the chocolate.
5. **Bake.** Scrape into the tin, scatter the last of the chocolate on top and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a skewer comes out with moist crumbs but no wet batter.
6. **Cool.** Leave in the tin for 15 minutes, then lift out onto a rack.

## Why ripe bananas matter in chocolate banana bread

As a banana ripens, its starch turns into sugar, so a black-skinned banana is much sweeter and softer than a yellow one. It also mashes into the batter, which keeps the cake moist. If yours are not ripe yet, freeze them and thaw them: the skins go black and the flesh goes soft.

## Ways to change it

- **Almonds:** swap 50 g of the flour for ground almonds, for a richer crumb.
- **Nuts:** use walnuts or pecans in place of half the chocolate.
- **Spice:** add a teaspoon of cinnamon with the flour.

The Wikipedia page on [banana bread](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana_bread) tells its history.
