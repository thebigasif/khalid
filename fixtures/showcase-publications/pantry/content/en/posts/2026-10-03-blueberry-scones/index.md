These blueberry scones are tall and tender, with a crisp top and a soft middle. They take half an hour from start to finish, so they are a good bake for a weekend breakfast.

Like pastry, scones need cold butter and a light touch. The method is close to the pastry for our [apple pie](/blog/apple-pie/), with buttermilk for lift.

## Ingredients

- 350 g self-raising flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
- 60 g caster sugar
- 85 g cold butter, diced
- 175 g buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 120 g blueberries, fresh or frozen
- 80 g icing sugar
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice

## Method

1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 220°C and put a baking tray in to heat.
2. **Rub in.** Rub the butter into the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar until it looks like fine crumbs.
3. **Mix.** Beat the buttermilk and egg together. Keep 2 tablespoons back to glaze, and stir the rest in with a knife until the dough just comes together.
4. **Add the berries.** Fold in the blueberries with as few turns as you can.
5. **Shape.** Pat the dough out 3 cm thick and cut 8 rounds with a floured cutter, pressing straight down without twisting.
6. **Bake.** Brush the tops with the kept glaze, set them on the hot tray and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until risen and golden.
7. **Glaze.** Mix the icing sugar and lemon juice and drizzle it over the cooled scones.

## Getting tall blueberry scones

Do not twist the cutter. A twist seals the edge, and the scone rises lopsided. A hot tray and a hot oven also help them rise fast before they spread.

Wikipedia's page on the [scone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scone) explains how it is eaten in different places.

## Using frozen blueberries

Frozen blueberries work well, but add them straight from the freezer. If they thaw, they bleed purple through the dough. Toss them in a spoon of flour first, so they stay spread out.

## Storing blueberry scones

Scones are best on the day you bake them. After that, warm them in a low oven for five minutes. Unglazed scones freeze well for a month; glaze them after they thaw.
