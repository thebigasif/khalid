These cinnamon buns are soft and not too sweet, with ground cardamom in the dough and a buttery cinnamon filling. They take a morning, but most of it is the dough rising on its own.

The dough is an enriched dough, like a richer version of our [overnight tin loaf](/blog/overnight-tin-loaf/), so it rises more slowly. Give it time.

## Ingredients

### For the dough

- 450 g plain flour
- 7 g fast-action dried yeast
- 50 g caster sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 6 g fine salt
- 240 g warm milk
- 1 egg
- 60 g soft butter

### For the filling and glaze

- 80 g soft butter
- 70 g light brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 egg, beaten, to brush
- 2 tablespoons golden syrup, warmed

## Method

1. **Make the dough.** Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, cardamom and salt. Add the milk and egg, mix, then knead in the butter for 10 minutes, until smooth.
2. **First rise.** Cover and leave until doubled, about 1.5 hours.
3. **Fill.** Roll the dough into a 40 by 30 cm rectangle. Beat the butter, sugar and cinnamon together and spread it to the edges.
4. **Roll and cut.** Roll up from the long side and cut into 12 slices. Set them in a lined 30 by 20 cm tin.
5. **Second rise.** Cover and leave until puffy and touching, about 45 minutes.
6. **Bake.** Brush with egg and bake at 190°C for 20 to 25 minutes, until deep golden.
7. **Glaze.** Brush the hot buns with the warm syrup and leave them for 10 minutes before you pull them apart.

## Getting soft cinnamon buns

Soft buns come from a soft dough. It should feel tacky after kneading, not dry. Resist adding flour: oil your hands and the worktop instead. Taking the buns out when they are just done keeps them soft for a day.

The Wikipedia page on the [cinnamon roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinnamon_roll) covers the Swedish and American styles.
