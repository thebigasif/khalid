This apple pie uses two kinds of apple: a sharp cooking apple that softens into a sauce, and a firm eating apple that keeps its shape. Together they give a filling that is juicy without running everywhere.

The pastry is quick to make by hand. Keep everything cold, and set out what you need before you start, as we explain in our note on [setting out your ingredients](/blog/set-out-ingredients/).

## Ingredients

### For the pastry

- 350 g plain flour
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
- 1 tablespoon caster sugar
- 225 g cold unsalted butter, diced
- 6 to 8 tablespoons iced water
- 1 egg, beaten, to glaze

### For the filling

- 3 cooking apples, about 600 g
- 3 eating apples, about 450 g
- 100 g light brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons plain flour
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Juice of half a lemon

## Method

1. **Make the pastry.** Rub the butter into the flour, salt and sugar until a few pea-sized pieces are left. Add the water a spoon at a time until the dough holds together. Split it in two, wrap and chill for 1 hour.
2. **Make the filling.** Peel, core and slice all the apples and toss them with the sugar, flour, cinnamon and lemon juice.
3. **Line the dish.** Roll out one piece of pastry and line a 23 cm pie dish, leaving the edge hanging over.
4. **Fill and top.** Pile in the apples. Roll out the second piece, lay it over or cut it into strips for a lattice, and press the edges to seal.
5. **Chill.** Chill the pie for 20 minutes while the oven heats to 200°C.
6. **Bake.** Brush with egg, cut a few slits, and bake for 25 minutes, then turn down to 180°C for 30 to 35 minutes more, until the juices bubble.
7. **Rest.** Leave for at least an hour, so the filling sets before you cut it.

## Keeping an apple pie crust crisp

Bake the pie on a hot baking tray on a low shelf. The heat from below cooks the base before the filling makes it soggy.
