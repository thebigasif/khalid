This lemon lavender loaf is the cake we bake when friends are coming over. It is a lemon drizzle cake that adds a little dried lavender, which tastes floral and fresh, not soapy, as long as you keep to a small amount.

It keeps well for three days in a tin. For a cake with less butter, try our [chocolate banana bread](/blog/chocolate-banana-bread/).

## Ingredients

### For the cake

- 175 g soft butter
- 175 g caster sugar
- 3 eggs
- 175 g self-raising flour
- Finely grated zest of 2 lemons
- 1 teaspoon dried culinary lavender, finely chopped
- 3 tablespoons milk

### For the drizzle and icing

- Juice of 1.5 lemons
- 80 g granulated sugar
- 100 g icing sugar

## Method

1. **Prepare.** Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 900 g loaf tin with baking paper.
2. **Cream.** Beat the butter and caster sugar until pale and fluffy, about 4 minutes.
3. **Add the eggs.** Beat in the eggs one at a time, with a spoon of the flour after each.
4. **Fold.** Fold in the rest of the flour, the zest, the lavender and the milk.
5. **Bake.** Spoon into the tin, level the top and bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
6. **Drizzle.** Mix the juice of one lemon with the granulated sugar. Prick the hot cake all over and spoon the syrup over it.
7. **Ice.** When the cake is cold, mix the icing sugar with the last half lemon's juice and pour it over the top.

## Keeping a lemon lavender loaf moist

Pour the syrup while the cake is hot and the syrup is cold. The cake drinks it up, and the sugar that stays on top sets into a crisp crust. Do not bake the cake past the point where the skewer comes out clean.

## About the lavender

Buy lavender sold for cooking. Lavender for gardens or crafts may have been sprayed. Leave it out for a plain lemon drizzle.
