These walnut brownies are fudgy rather than cakey, with a thin, shiny top that crackles when you cut it. The trick is to whisk the eggs and sugar until thick before the chocolate goes in.

Toast the walnuts first. It takes ten minutes and makes them taste twice as nutty. For a bake with no chocolate, see our [shortbread fingers](/blog/shortbread-fingers/).

## Ingredients

- 100 g walnuts
- 200 g dark chocolate, the bitter kind
- 175 g unsalted butter
- 3 eggs
- 250 g caster sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 80 g plain flour
- 30 g cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt

## Method

1. **Toast the nuts.** Toast the walnuts at 180°C for 8 to 10 minutes, then chop them roughly. Line a 20 by 30 cm tin.
2. **Melt.** Melt the chocolate and butter together in a bowl over a pan of barely simmering water. Leave to cool a little.
3. **Whisk.** Whisk the eggs, sugar and vanilla for 5 minutes, until thick and pale.
4. **Combine.** Fold in the chocolate, then sift over the flour, cocoa and salt and fold until no streaks are left. Fold in half the walnuts.
5. **Bake.** Scrape into the tin, scatter the rest of the walnuts on top and bake at 180°C for 22 to 25 minutes. The middle should still wobble a little.
6. **Cool.** Leave to cool completely in the tin, then chill for an hour before cutting.

## How to tell walnut brownies are done

Brownies set as they cool, so take them out while the middle still looks soft. A skewer from the middle should come out with sticky crumbs. If it comes out clean, they will be cakey.

Read about the [brownie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocolate_brownie) on Wikipedia.

## Storing walnut brownies

Keep the brownies in a tin for up to four days. They get fudgier on the second day, as the moisture spreads through the middle. To freeze them, wrap each square in baking paper and keep them for up to two months. Thaw them on the worktop for an hour.

## Cutting clean squares

Chill the whole slab first, then cut it with a long, sharp knife. Dip the knife in hot water and wipe it dry between cuts, so each square has a clean edge.
