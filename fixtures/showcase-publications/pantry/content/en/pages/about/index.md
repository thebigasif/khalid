Oven Spring is a home baking blog. We write tested recipes for bread, cakes, biscuits and pastry that work in an ordinary home oven. Oven Spring, its bakers and its recipes are made up, to show the Pantry template.

## How our recipes work

Every recipe starts with the facts: how long it takes, what it makes, the oven setting and how hard it is. Then come the ingredients, weighed in grams, and the method in numbered steps. The story comes after, for anyone who wants it.

We weigh everything, and we explain why in our note on [setting out your ingredients](/blog/set-out-ingredients/). Oven settings are for a conventional oven. For a fan oven, set it about 20°C lower.
