Nell started Oven Spring to write down the breads she bakes every week, with weights that work in a home oven. She writes the bread, pastry and kitchen notes, and tests every recipe at least three times before it goes up. Her rule is that a recipe should work on a busy weekday, not just on a free Sunday.
