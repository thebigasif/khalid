Kite is a small magazine of good ideas, arranged by topic. Each piece takes one question, such as why the sea is salty or why vinyl came back, and answers it in plain words. Kite, its writers and their stories are made up, to show the Spectrum template.

## Six topics, six colours

Kite covers six topics, and each one keeps its own colour across the magazine:

- [Culture](/blog/category/culture/): records, libraries and how we spend our time.
- [Food](/blog/category/food/): baking, seasonal cooking and where food comes from.
- [Nature](/blog/category/nature/): weather, trees and the living world outside the door.
- [Science](/blog/category/science/): how things work, explained from the ground up.
- [Travel](/blog/category/travel/): slow journeys by train and on foot.
- [Design](/blog/category/design/): objects made well, and the people who make them.

## Who writes it

- **Mara Lindqvist** is the editor and writes about science and nature.
- **Jonah Pereira** writes about food and travel.
- **Ada Whitlow** writes about culture and design.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists everything each of them has written. A good place to start is the [slow train over the Bernina Pass](/blog/slow-train-over-the-bernina/).

## How to reach us

Write to hello@example.com with a question you would like Kite to answer. The photographs in Kite are in the public domain or shared under CC0; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
