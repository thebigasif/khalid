# Kite: art direction

Kite is a made-up magazine of good ideas in six topics. Its photographs should look like a magazine's own: one clear subject per picture, in natural light, with room around it. Every topic has its own colour in the Spectrum template, so the photographs stay true to life and let the topic colours do the colour coding.

## Tone

- **One subject, close or wide.** A loaf, a bee, a chair, a train in the snow. No crowds and no posed portraits.
- **Natural light and true colour.** No heavy filters. One black and white picture, the potter's hands, is kept for the craft piece.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs or captions in the picture. A first candidate for the travel piece on walking a city, a riverside at dusk, was turned down because the photographer's name was printed on it.

## Licences

Every photograph is CC0 or in the public domain, so no credit has to show on the page. Each is still credited on the photo credits page and in ATTRIBUTION.md.

## Subject mix

Of the 16 photographs, 9 are close details and 7 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (9):** a bee on an aster, a sourdough loaf, citrus slices, a cut onion, a teapot, a record on a turntable, a chess board, a potter's hands, a wooden chair.
- **Wider scenes (7):** a breaking wave, a train in the snow, canal houses, a library reading room, heaped clouds, an autumn tree, a forest floor of moss.

## One ratio per slot

- **Lead story:** 4:3, cropped from the centre. On a phone it keeps 4:3 at the full width.
- **The large card of a topic row:** 3:2. On a phone it keeps 3:2.
- **The small cards of a topic row:** 1:1, a square beside the title, on every screen.
- **Cards on lists:** 3:2.
- **A post's lead picture:** 16:9 under the topic band, as wide as the page. The chair and the onion, both tall pictures, lose their top and bottom but keep the subject in view.
