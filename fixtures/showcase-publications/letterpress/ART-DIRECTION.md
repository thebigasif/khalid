# The Proof Sheet: art direction

The Proof Sheet is a made-up weekly letter about things made by hand. Each issue's photograph shows the craft that issue visits: type in a case, clay on a wheel, glass in a flame, paper drying in a yard. They should feel close, warm and hands-on, like standing at the maker's bench.

## Tone

- **Hands and materials, not faces.** Hands at work, tools, type, clay, thread and glass. No face is the subject of a picture. Author pictures are drawn marks.
- **Workshop light.** Window light, lamplight and the glow of a flame. No heavy filters.
- **No text on a picture.** Titles, numbers and contents are set in type beside or under the picture, never on it. The type in the cover photograph is a tray of small sorts, too small and too scattered to read.

## Palette

Warm browns and ochres of wood, clay and paper, with the occasional bright thread or flame:

- the browns and creams of wood, leather and unbleached paper;
- the terracotta of wet clay;
- small bright accents: dyed yarn, a ribbon, a torch flame.

These sit well beside the sample brand's letterpress red and cream paper.

## Subject mix

Of the 9 photographs, 6 are close views of hands or materials and 3 are wider workshop scenes. One issue, No. 42, has no photograph, so the template's text-only issue shows too.

- **Close views (6):** type in a case, glass in a flame, a carver's hands, a sewing tin, clay on a wheel, a stack of leather books.
- **Wider scenes (3):** paper drying in a yard, yarn and dyes at a market, a wheelwright's barn.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover issue on the front page | 3:2, in a plate | 1600 px wide | Cropped to the middle. Every cover photo has its subject in the middle. |
| Lead picture on an issue | 3:2, in a plate | 1600 px wide | Shown at the issue's width, about 46rem. The paper yard, shot wider, crops to its middle. |
| Past issues | none | | The small printed covers carry no pictures, so the front page stays light. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the cover's picture stands over its text at the full width, and each issue's picture fills the column. The 3:2 crop keeps every subject whole.
