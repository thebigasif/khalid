Tobias made furniture for twenty years before a bad wrist moved him from the bench to the notebook. He now travels to the workshops The Proof Sheet writes about, spends a day at each one and writes up what he saw. He still teaches an evening class in hand tools, where he is stricter about sharp chisels than about straight cuts.
