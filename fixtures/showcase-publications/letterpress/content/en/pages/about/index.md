The Proof Sheet is a weekly letter about things made by hand and the people who make them. Each issue visits one workshop, looks closely at one tool and ends with letters from readers. The letter, its writers and the workshops on this site are made up, to show the Letterpress template.

## How an issue is made

Maren Holt writes most issues and Tobias Reyne visits most of the workshops. Every issue has a number, and its first lines list what is inside: the workshop visit, the tool and the letters. We print a small run of each issue on a hand press before it goes out by email, which is how the letter got its name. A proof sheet is the first print a printer pulls to check the type.

## Getting the letter

The letter goes out every Thursday morning. It is free, it carries no advertising and every email has a link to leave. Sign up with the form on the front page, or write to letters@example.com and we will add you by hand.

## Back issues

Every past issue is on this site, from [the first printed issue](/blog/why-this-letter-is-printed/) onwards, and the [weekly issues](/blog/category/weekly/) page lists them all. The special issues, such as the season's list of workshops, are under [specials](/blog/category/special/).

## Letters

We read every letter and answer a few at the end of each issue. Tell us about a maker near you, a tool you cannot live without or a craft you would like us to visit. The photographs on this site are shared by their photographers under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
