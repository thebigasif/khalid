Studio Ilvane is a small fashion label that makes coats, tailoring and knitwear in small runs. Every collection is shown here as a lookbook, one look at a time, with the pieces named. Studio Ilvane, its people and every product on this site are made up, to show the Atelier template.

Each post is either a look, with a photograph and the list of pieces worn, or a note from the studio about cloth, care and making.

## Who we are

- **Noor Hallsten** designs every piece and runs the studio.
- **Tomas Achterberg** styles the looks and writes about them.

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their looks and notes.

## How we make

We make each piece in a run of forty or fewer, with mills and workshops we visit. When a run sells out, we make it again only if the cloth is still there. Our note on [the sample rail](/blog/sample-rail-season/) explains how a collection grows.

## Visit the studio

The studio is at 14 Weaver Lane, Harlow Mill. Fittings are by appointment from Tuesday to Saturday. Write to studio@example.com to book one. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work freely; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
