Black tailoring can look stiff. We wanted a suit you could wear to a long dinner, sit in for hours and still look sharp in at the end of the night.

## A soft shoulder

The jacket has no padding in the shoulder. Instead, the sleeve is set into the body with a little ease, so the shoulder follows your own line. It looks relaxed when you lean on a table and clean when you stand. This is an old way of cutting a [suit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit), and we think it suits how people dress now.

## Black tailoring in one colour

In this look, everything is black: the jacket, the poplin shirt, the pleated trousers and the loafers. When the colour is the same from top to toe, the eye goes to the cut and the cloth. It is also the easiest outfit to pack.

## The cloth

The jacket and trousers are a dry wool with a light twist. It creases less than a soft wool and breathes better than a blend. It drapes well enough for a pleat to hang straight.

## Wearing it apart

The jacket also works over a knit. Try it with [our merino crew](/blog/navy-merino-crew/) for a day look. The trousers go with a white shirt in summer, as in the [white cotton shift](/blog/white-cotton-shift/) look from our spring collection.

- **Fit:** the jacket is cut to close with room for a knit. Take your usual size.
- **Alterations:** we shorten sleeves and trousers free of charge in the studio.

## Why we cut black tailoring this way

Most of our customers own one suit and wear it to weddings, interviews and funerals. We wanted that one suit to be useful on every other day too. Black tailoring with a soft shoulder does both: worn together it is formal, and worn apart it is easy. Read how we pick the cloth in our [guide to winter cloth](/blog/winter-cloth-guide/).

Black tailoring is made to order. Allow three weeks from the fitting.
