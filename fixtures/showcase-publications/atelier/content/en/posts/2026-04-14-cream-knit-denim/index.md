Not every look is for going out. This one is for a slow Sunday at home: a loose cream knit, worn-in denim and bare feet on a wooden floor.

## The cream knit itself

The knit is a thick cotton yarn, not wool, so you can wear it from April to June. It has a wide neck and long sleeves that you can push up. The stitch is a simple jersey with a deep rib at the hem and cuffs. Cotton knits grow a little with wear, so we cut it close and let it relax.

## The denim

The jeans are a relaxed cut in a light blue wash. We did not tear the knee for this picture: they are a pair Noor has worn for six years, and the knee went on its own. That is the best thing about good [denim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denim). It fades where you move and keeps a record of how you live.

## Why we show it

A lookbook is full of perfect outfits. We wanted one look that shows how our clothes live after the photograph, when they are washed, worn and loved. The cream knit is made to be lived in like this.

## Wearing it out

For a day out, wear the cream knit with [the pleated trouser](/blog/printed-tee-pleated-trouser/) from look 07 and loafers. Over a white shirt, it also works under the [field coat](/blog/field-coat/) in early autumn.

- **Care:** wash it at thirty degrees and dry it flat.
- **Colours:** cream, stone and a faded black.

## Living in a cream knit

People worry that a cream knit will look dirty in a week. In our experience it does not, because cotton washes well and the colour forgives small marks. Wash the cream knit after five or six wears, not after every one, and it keeps its shape and softness.

The cream knit is one of our three summer knits. All of them are made by the same family workshop.
