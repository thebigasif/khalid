The white cotton shift is the simplest dress we have made. It has no zip, no darts and no sleeves. It is one clean line from a high neck to just below the knee.

## The poplin

We use a dense cotton poplin with a slight sheen. It is crisp enough to hold the shape of the dress and light enough for a hot day. It is also opaque, which matters in white. You can read about [poplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poplin) and why its fine ribs make it smooth.

## The shape of the white cotton shift

The dress falls straight from the shoulders and skims the body. The neck is high and close, with a single button at the back. The armholes are cut high too, so you can wear it without a slip or a layer underneath. Two side pockets sit in the seams, deep enough for keys and a phone.

## From morning to evening

In this look, the dress is worn with flat tan sandals, a raffia tote and small brass hoops. For the evening, add a light jacket. [Our soft-shoulder jacket](/blog/black-tailoring/) from look 04 sits well over it, and so does a cotton cardigan. For a bolder day, swap the dress for [a printed tee and pleated trouser](/blog/printed-tee-pleated-trouser/).

## Packing it

The dress folds into a small square and comes out of a bag with few creases, because poplin springs back. Roll it rather than fold it if you are away for more than a night, and hang it in a steamy bathroom when you arrive. That is all the ironing a white cotton shift needs on holiday.

## Keeping white white

- Wash it at forty degrees with a little extra powder.
- Dry it in the shade, since the sun yellows cotton over time.
- Iron it while it is still slightly damp.

## Why a white cotton shift

A white cotton shift is cool in heat, smart enough for a lunch and simple enough for a beach. It is also the easiest dress to pack, because it needs nothing else. For a cooler day, wear it over a [light cotton knit](/blog/cream-knit-denim/) or under a jacket.

The white shift comes in white and in a deep indigo. Both are back for next summer, and we keep a small stock all year.
