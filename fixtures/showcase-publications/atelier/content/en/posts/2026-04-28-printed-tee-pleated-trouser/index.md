A pleated trouser looks smart but feels like a pair of pyjamas. That is why we made it the base of this spring look, under a loose printed tee.

## The pleated trouser

The trouser sits high on the waist, with two deep pleats on each side. The pleats open when you walk and close when you stand, so the leg looks long and straight. The waist ties with a belt in the same fabric, which you can knot at the side or the front.

The cloth is a washed cotton and linen mix. [Linen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linen) keeps you cool, and the cotton stops it creasing too much.

## The print

The tee is printed with a bold black pattern on ecru cotton. We drew the print in the studio, from shapes we cut out of paper. It is loud on purpose, because the rest of the outfit is plain.

## How to wear it

- Tuck the tee in at the front only, so the waist and the tie belt show.
- Keep the shoes simple: white trainers by day, black sandals at night.
- Add tortoiseshell sunglasses, as here, and leave it there.

If print is not for you, the pleated trouser works with [a soft cotton knit](/blog/cream-knit-denim/) from look 08, or with a plain white shirt.

## Why a pleated trouser

A pleated trouser gives room at the hip and the thigh, so you can sit, cycle and climb stairs in comfort. The pleats also hold a crease, so the trouser looks pressed even after a long day. For a summer evening, wear it with the [white cotton shift](/blog/white-cotton-shift/) look's sandals.

## Fit

Take your usual waist size. The leg is long, so we hem each pair in the studio to the shoes you wear most. The trouser comes in black and stone, and the tee in two prints, both made in small runs.
