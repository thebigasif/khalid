# Studio Ilvane: art direction

Studio Ilvane is a made-up fashion label. Its photographs should look like a small label's own lookbook: one person, one outfit, plain backgrounds and natural light. The clothes are the subject.

## Tone

- **The outfit is the subject.** Each look shows one person wearing the outfit its post names. Several are seen from behind or cropped at the face, so the clothes lead.
- **Plain grounds.** A pale wall, a street, a field, a wooden floor. No crowds, no shop fronts.
- **No brands.** No logo, label, sign or printed name may show. The street style pictures from a fashion week on Wikimedia Commons were turned down for this reason: sponsor signs showed in most frames.
- **Natural light,** with true colour. One look, black tailoring, is black and white on purpose: it shows an outfit in one colour.

## Palette

Warm neutrals with a few strong colours:

- ecru, cream, sand and stone;
- ink, black and slate;
- one saffron coat and one pink wall as the accents.

These sit well on the sample brand's ecru page and its terracotta accent. Avoid cold blue light and neon colours.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 8 are looks and 3 are studio details.

- **Looks (8):** the field coat and felt hat, the saffron coat from behind, the navy crew on a pink wall, black tailoring, the leather jacket by a brick wall, the white shift in a field, the printed tee and pleated trouser, the cream knit with denim.
- **Studio details (3):** a houndstooth tweed, folded shirts with brown brogues, the sample rail.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Look in the lookbook grid | 4:5 | 960 px wide | Cropped from the middle by the template. Each subject sits in the middle third. |
| Lead picture on a post, in the spread | its own | 960 px wide | Shown at its own shape on the start side, at most about 44rem wide. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the grid is one or two columns of 4:5 pictures, and the post's picture fills the width at its own shape. The landscape looks (the saffron coat, the leather jacket, the white shift, the cream knit) keep their person in the middle third, so the 4:5 crop keeps the whole outfit.

## Sources and licences

Every photograph is CC0 1.0, from StockSnap through Openverse, fetched with `scripts/demo-photo.py`. Openverse hands out StockSnap photos at 960 px wide only, so they were fetched with `--min-width 960`. That is wide enough for a grid column and for the spread's picture on a normal screen. `photos.lock` records each one, and `ATTRIBUTION.md` and the photo credits page credit them, although CC0 asks for no credit.
