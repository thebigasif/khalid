Mossboard is a project board for small teams: lists of cards you move from left to right as work gets done, with due dates, checklists and a calendar. Mossboard, its team and every change on this site are made up, to show the Versions template.

## How this changelog works

Each post is one change. It is marked with its type and the version it shipped in:

- **[New](/blog/category/new/)** is a feature Mossboard did not have before.
- **[Improved](/blog/category/improved/)** is something that already worked and now works better.
- **[Fixed](/blog/category/fixed/)** is a bug we fixed, with what went wrong and who it affected.

The front page shows every change by version, newest first. The links at the top of each list show only one type, and the areas at the foot of each page, such as [boards](/blog/tag/boards/) or the [mobile apps](/blog/tag/mobile-apps/), show only one part of the product.

## Follow the changes

Sign up on the front page to get an email when a new version ships. You can also follow the whole changelog, or only one type such as the fixes, in a feed reader: each type's page links its own feed.

## Who writes it

- **Priya Natarajan** leads product and writes most of the notes on new features.
- **Tomás Ferreira** leads engineering and writes the notes on fixes and speed.

The [team page](/blog/authors/) lists both, with everything they wrote. Write to hello@example.com with a question or a bug report, and someone from the team answers within two working days.
