Mossboard now has recurring cards: a card can repeat. Open any card, choose **Repeat** and pick when it should come back: every day, on chosen weekdays, every few weeks or on a day of the month. When you finish the card, Mossboard makes the next one in the same list with the same checklist, labels and people.

## Why we built them

Most small teams have work that comes round again: the weekly invoice run, the monthly report, the backup check on Fridays. Until now you copied those cards by hand, or kept them in a separate calendar. In our survey this summer, repeating work was the most asked-for feature by a long way, well ahead of anything else on the list.

## How recurring cards work

- **The next copy appears when you finish this one.** It does not pile up while you are away, so a holiday never leaves you with ten copies of the same task.
- **Each copy keeps its own history.** Comments and files stay on the card where they were written.
- **You can skip once.** Choose **Skip this time** and the card moves to the next date without a copy.
- **Stopping is one click.** Turn off **Repeat** and the card stays as it is.

Good [task management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_management) keeps routine work in the same place as everything else, so nobody has to remember it. That is the whole idea here.

## Where you will see them

Recurring cards show on the [calendar view](/blog/calendar-view/) on every date they will come back, drawn in a lighter colour. Each one also opens from the board without a wait, thanks to the [faster board loading](/blog/faster-board-loading/) in the same version.
