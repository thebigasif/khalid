Removing a card from a board now archives it. Archived cards leave the board but keep their comments, files and history. You can find one again and bring it back at any time.

Archived cards stay archived for as long as the board exists. They do not count toward any limit on your plan, and they keep their labels, people and due dates, so a card you bring back looks just as it did.

## The problem with deleting

Deleting a card used to be the only way to clear it from a board, and deleting is final. Teams told us they lost decisions written in the comments of cards they had cleared too quickly. Archiving keeps the board tidy without losing anything, much like moving paper to a filing cabinet in place of the bin. [Archives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive) exist for the same reason.

## Finding archived cards and bringing them back

- **To archive,** open a card and choose **Archive**, or press **A**.
- **To find one again,** open the board menu and choose **Archived**, or tick **Include archived** in search.
- **To bring one back,** open it and choose **Send to board**. It returns to the list it came from.

Deleting is still there for a card you truly want gone, such as one made by mistake. It is now in the card's menu under **More**, and it asks you to confirm.

## Lists too

The same applies to whole lists. Archiving a list archives its cards with it, and bringing the list back brings them all back, in the same order.

## How it fits with the rest

The [card search](/blog/card-search-comments/) of version 3.3 can include them, so an old decision is a search away. Each archive also sends a message through the [webhooks](/blog/webhooks/) that shipped in this version, and the [recurring cards](/blog/recurring-cards/) of 3.4 stop repeating when you archive them.
