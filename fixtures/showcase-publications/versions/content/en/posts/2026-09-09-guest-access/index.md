Mossboard now has guest access: you can invite people from outside your team to a single board. A guest sees only the boards you share with them. They can read cards, add comments and tick checklist items, but they cannot change lists, invite others or see your other boards.

A guest's view of a board is quieter than a member's. They see the cards and the comments, but not the board's settings, its member list or the activity of your other boards. Guests can still use the calendar view and search inside the boards they can see.

## Why we built it

Many teams on Mossboard work for clients. Until now they had two poor choices: add the client as a full member, or copy updates into emails by hand. Guest access lets a client follow the work where it happens, without seeing anything else.

## How guest access works

- **Invite by email.** Open a board, choose **Share**, then **Invite a guest**. The guest gets an email with a link and makes a password.
- **Guests are free.** They do not count as a seat on your plan.
- **You choose what they can do.** A guest can only read, or read and comment.
- **Guests are marked.** Their name has a small "Guest" tag, so your team always knows who can see a comment.

This follows a plain rule of [access control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_control): give each person only what they need.

## Keeping private things private

A card can be marked **Team only**, and guests do not see it. Use this for prices, internal notes or anything not ready to share. A list can be marked the same way.

## Plans and digests

Guests are on the Team and Business plans. A guest can also get a daily digest of the boards they can see; the [digest fix in 3.2.1](/blog/digest-emails-twice/) makes sure they get only one. The [keyboard shortcuts](/blog/keyboard-shortcuts/) in this version work for guests too.
