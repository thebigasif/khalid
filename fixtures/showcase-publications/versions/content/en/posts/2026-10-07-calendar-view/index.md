Every board now has a calendar view. Open a board and choose **Calendar** next to **Board** and **List**. Each card with a deadline sits on its day, in the colour of its first label. Cards without one wait in a tray at the side.

The calendar view works on the web and in both mobile apps. On a phone it opens in the week layout, which fits a narrow screen better, and you can swipe between weeks.

## What you can do in the calendar view

- **Switch between month and week.** The week view shows times for cards that have one.
- **Drag a card to another day** to move its deadline. Drag it into the tray to clear it.
- **Filter by person or label,** the same filters the board uses, so you can see only your own work.
- **Add a card on a day** by clicking an empty space, and it gets that day.

## Follow a board in your own calendar app

You can also follow a board in the calendar app you already use. Choose **Share**, then **Calendar link**, and paste the link into your app. It uses the [iCalendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICalendar) format, so it works with most calendar apps, and it updates about every hour. The link is private to you; anyone who has it can read the board's dates, so keep it to yourself.

## Why a calendar

Boards are good at showing what stage work is in, and poor at showing when it is needed. Teams told us they kept a second calendar for deadlines and copied dates between the two. Now there is one place, and moving a card in one view moves it in the other.

## What works with it

The [recurring cards](/blog/recurring-cards/) of version 3.4 show here on every day they come back. The [card search](/blog/card-search-comments/) in this version can switch to this view with one key, and the [time zone fix](/blog/due-dates-time-zones/) means every teammate sees each card on the same day.
