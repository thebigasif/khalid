Card search now looks in more places. Before this version it read only a card's title and description, so a decision written in a comment was hard to find again. It now also reads every comment and checklist item, on every board you can open.

Search works the same on the web and in both mobile apps. On a phone, pull down on any board to open it. Results come back in the order of how well they match, with the newest cards first when two match equally well.

## What changed

Each result shows where the words were found: in the title, the description, a comment or a checklist. For a comment, you see the line around your words and who wrote it, so you can tell the right card at a glance. Choose the result and the card opens at that comment.

You can still narrow a search to one board, one person or one label. Archived cards are left out unless you choose **Include archived**.

## Faster card search

Searching more text could have made each search slower. We rebuilt the [search index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_engine_indexing) at the same time, and a search across all your boards now answers in under half a second for nearly every team. The index updates within a few seconds of a new comment, so a note you just wrote is found straight away.

## Tips for card search

- Put a phrase in quotes to find those words together.
- Add a person's name to see only cards they are on.
- Press the slash key from anywhere to start; the [keyboard shortcuts](/blog/keyboard-shortcuts/) page lists the rest.
