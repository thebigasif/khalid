# Mossboard changelog: art direction

The Mossboard changelog is a made-up changelog for a project board app. A changelog is read for its words: what changed, in which version, and for whom. So this publication has no photographs.

## Tone

- **Words first.** Each change is a short title, a one-line summary and a few plain paragraphs, sometimes with a table.
- **No stock pictures.** A photograph of people at a laptop says nothing about a change. A real team would add a screenshot of the new screen where it helps; the demo leaves pictures out, so the version rail and the type chips carry the page.
- **Drawn marks for people.** Each author has a simple drawn mark in the brand's moss green, never a photograph of a person.

## Palette

Moss green on warm white, with a pale leaf surface for the update box and the facts of each change. Fills of the accent are kept for the New chips and the newest version's node on the rail.

## Subject mix

None: 0 photographs. The logo and the two author marks are SVG drawings.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 by 160 SVG, shown at 5rem in the team grid |
| Logo | 4:1 | 320 by 80 SVG, shown at 2.5rem tall in the header |
| Lead picture | none | A real changelog may add a screenshot; Versions shows it under the facts, framed by a hairline |
