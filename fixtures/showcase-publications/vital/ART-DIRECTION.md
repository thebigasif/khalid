# Fernhill Wellbeing: art direction

Fernhill Wellbeing is a made-up movement and wellbeing studio. Its photographs should look like ordinary people doing the routines at home, at a desk or outdoors, in calm daylight. They show what a routine feels like, never a body to aim for.

## Tone

- **The activity is the subject.** A stretch, a walk, a cup of tea, a still lake. People may appear, but the move or the place fills the frame, not a face.
- **Everyday settings.** A living room floor, a kitchen table, a park bench, a path. No gyms full of machines, no competition, no before and after.
- **Soft daylight.** Morning or overcast light, true colour, no heavy filters.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, logos or text in the picture.

## Palette

Warm, calm colours that sit well with the sample brand's clay and cream:

- the greens and browns of parks, paths and trees;
- the greys and creams of floors, mats and walls;
- small spots of colour, such as a water bottle or a mat.

Avoid photographs dominated by saturated red or neon.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 7 show a person doing a routine, 2 show a group or a class, and 2 are calm details or places.

- **A person doing a routine (7):** a stretch on a mat from above, a seated stretch on a mat by a window, a desk stretch, a walk down a tree-lined path, a push-up, a one-leg balance, a leg stretch on a park bench.
- **A group or a class (2):** a studio class with arms raised, a walking group after a walk.
- **Details and places (2):** hands holding warm mugs, a still lake in the morning.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session card | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Cropped to 3:2 in the card. Sources are 3:2 or 4:3, so little is lost. |
| Lead picture on a post | as shot, 3:2 or 4:3 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the reading column's width, about 37rem, with rounded corners. |
| Coach mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one clay line on a light clay tint. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone a card's picture fills the width at 3:2. Each subject sits in the middle third: the stretching figures, the walker on the path, the mugs and the group are centred, and the lake is an even texture that crops well anywhere.
