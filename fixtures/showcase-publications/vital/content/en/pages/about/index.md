Fernhill Wellbeing is a small movement and wellbeing studio. We teach gentle mobility, strength, walking and breathing classes, and we write short routines that anyone can try at home. Fernhill Wellbeing, its coaches and its timetable are made up, to show the Vital template.

## General wellbeing, not medical advice

Everything on this site is general wellbeing content: everyday ideas for moving, breathing and resting well, the kind of common sense a good class teaches. It is not medical advice, and it makes no claims to treat, cure or prevent any condition.

Go gently and stop if anything hurts. If you have a health condition, an injury, or you are pregnant, ask your doctor before you start a new routine.

The [coaches page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their routines.

## How the routines work

Each routine says how long it takes, how hard it is, how often to do it and what you need. Its steps are numbered, so you can follow along. Our routines sit in three programmes: [move well](/blog/category/move-well/), [walk more](/blog/category/walk-more/) and [breathe and rest](/blog/category/breathe-rest/). Studio news is under [studio notes](/blog/category/studio-notes/). A good place to start is the [morning mobility routine](/blog/morning-mobility-routine/).
