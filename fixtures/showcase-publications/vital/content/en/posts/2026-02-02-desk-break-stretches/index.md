Long hours at a desk leave most of us stiff by the afternoon. These desk break stretches take five minutes and need nothing but your chair. Do them every two hours, or whenever you notice you have not moved for a while.

Sit near the front of the chair with both feet flat on the floor, and let your shoulders drop before you start.

## Five desk break stretches

1. **Neck tilt.** Drop one ear towards your shoulder and hold for five slow breaths. Then change sides.
2. **Shoulder rolls.** Roll both shoulders up, back and down ten times.
3. **Seated twist.** Hold the back of the chair with one hand and turn gently towards it. Stay for five breaths on each side.
4. **Wrist circles.** Lace your fingers together and draw slow circles, ten each way.
5. **Stand and reach.** Stand up, reach both arms over your head and take three deep breaths.

## Why short breaks help

Sitting still is what makes us stiff, so the break itself matters as much as the stretch. Moving for a few minutes also gives your eyes a rest from the screen, and many people find they come back to their work with a clearer head.

## Small habits that help

- Set a quiet reminder every two hours.
- Stand up for phone calls.
- Keep a glass of water on the desk, so refilling it gets you walking.
- Have lunch away from the desk when you can.

None of this needs to be perfect. A short break done often helps more than a long one you never take.
