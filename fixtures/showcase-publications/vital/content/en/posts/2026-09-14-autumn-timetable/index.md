Our autumn timetable starts on Monday 28 September. Most classes stay the same, and there are two new sessions for early risers and for Sunday evenings.

We asked people in our classes over the summer what would help them come more often. Many said an early slot before work, and many asked for something calm to end the week. So the autumn timetable adds both, and keeps everything else where it was.

## New on the autumn timetable

- **Early mobility,** Tuesday and Thursday at 7.00. Thirty minutes, based on [our home mobility routine](/blog/morning-mobility-routine/), so you can carry on at home on the other days.
- **Breathe and rest,** Sunday at 18.30. Forty-five minutes of gentle stretching and breathing, including [box breathing](/blog/box-breathing-reset/), to end the week.

## What stays the same

| Day | Time | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 18.00 | Strength basics |
| Wednesday | 12.30 | Lunchtime stretch |
| Friday | 9.30 | Gentle mobility |
| Saturday | 9.00 | Walking group, from the park gates |

Every class is open to beginners. Bring water and wear clothes you can move in. We have mats to borrow, and chairs for anyone who prefers to work seated.

## Who teaches what

Hana teaches the mobility and strength classes, Tomás leads Breathe and rest, and Priya walks with the Saturday group. Each class follows one of the routines on this site, so you can look it up before you come.

## Booking

Book a place at the front desk or by email at hello@example.com. Classes hold twelve people, so book early for the new early mobility slot. The [Saturday walks from the park gates](/blog/saturday-walking-group/) need no booking at all.

## Between classes

If you want to keep going at home, read [how we write our routines](/blog/short-routines/) and pick one to start with. The [Sport England guide to getting active](https://www.sportengland.org/jointhemovement) has more ideas for the days in between.
