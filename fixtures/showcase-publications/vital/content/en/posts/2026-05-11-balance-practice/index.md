Good balance makes everyday things easier, from stairs to uneven paths. This balance practice takes eight minutes and fits around the kitchen, so you can do it while the kettle boils. Stand next to a wall or a counter and hold on whenever you need to.

## Five moves for balance practice

1. **Heel raises.** Rise up onto your toes and lower slowly, ten times.
2. **Heel to toe.** Walk ten steps in a straight line, placing one heel just in front of the other toes.
3. **One-leg stand.** Lift one foot a little off the floor for 20 seconds, then change feet.
4. **Side leg lift.** Stand tall and lift one leg out to the side, eight times for each leg.
5. **Sit to stand.** Stand up from a chair without using your hands, if you can, eight times.

## Making it harder, slowly

When a move feels steady, hold on with one finger instead of a whole hand. Later, let go for a few seconds. Then try it while you look at a spot further away.

Change one thing at a time, and only when the last version feels easy. Balance improves in small steps, so there is no need to rush.

## Three times a week

Pick three days and link the practice to something you already do, such as making tea. Over a few weeks most people notice they feel steadier on their feet.

## Keep it safe

Clear the floor around you first, and wear flat shoes or bare feet rather than socks on a smooth floor. Keep something sturdy within reach for every move, even when you no longer need it.
