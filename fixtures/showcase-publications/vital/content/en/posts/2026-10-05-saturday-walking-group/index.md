Our Saturday walking group meets at 9.00 by the park gates, every week of the year. It is free, it takes about an hour, and it goes at a chatting pace, so nobody is left behind.

## What the walking group does

We walk a loop of about four kilometres through the park and along the river. There is a shorter loop for anyone who wants it, and a leader always walks at the back.

Most weeks, ten to twenty people come, of every age and pace. Some walk with us every Saturday, and some come once a month. Many first came alone and now walk with friends they met here.

## What to bring

- Comfortable shoes.
- A layer for the weather.
- Water on warm days.

After the walk, we do five minutes of [the stretches we do after every walk](/blog/after-walk-stretches/) on the grass, and some of us stay for coffee at the park cafe.

## A walking group for beginners too

The group is a good place to start if you have not walked much for a while. If you also want a plan for the rest of the week, try [four weeks of walks that build up slowly](/blog/four-week-walking-programme/).

## Who comes along

Walkers range from people training for a long hike to people getting back on their feet after a quiet year. We pair new walkers with a regular for their first walk, so there is always someone to chat to and someone who knows the way.

## In bad weather

We walk in light rain, and we cancel only for storms or ice. When we do, we post a note on the studio door and send an email the evening before. The [full class timetable](/blog/autumn-timetable/) lists every other session.

## Walking elsewhere

Away for the weekend? The [Walking for Health network](https://www.walkingforhealth.org.uk/) lists free group walks in other towns. If you like the idea of short, regular sessions, our note on [why our posts are short routines](/blog/short-routines/) explains how we think about them.
