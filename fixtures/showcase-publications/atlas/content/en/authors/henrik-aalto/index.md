Henrik spent eight years planning timetables for a regional rail company before he started writing about how to get around cities without a car. He covers Northern Europe for Meridian Atlas, and he checks every transport section we publish, wherever the city is. He would rather take a slow train with a window than a fast flight with an aisle seat.
