Asha lived in Tokyo for four years and in Hanoi for two, and she still spends part of each year in both. She writes our Asia pages, with a soft spot for markets, noodle counters and the neighbourhoods a first visit tends to miss. She pays for every meal she writes about and never says where she was given one free.
