Meridian Atlas is a travel magazine about cities. We write for people who would rather walk a neighbourhood than tick off a list: where to stay, how to get around without a car, which markets to eat in, what a week really costs and which month suits a city best.

We are four writers and a shared spreadsheet. We pay for our own travel, and nobody pays to be mentioned.

## Who writes here

- **Elena Marsh** edits the magazine and writes most of our Southern Europe pages. She walks every route we publish.
- **Henrik Aalto** is our rail and transport editor. He covers Northern Europe and checks every "getting around" section.
- **Asha Kerr** writes our Asia pages, with most of her time spent in markets and at noodle counters.
- **Rosa Delgado** runs the costs desk and writes our pages on the Americas.

## The destination index

Every city sits in one region: Southern Europe, Northern Europe, Asia or The Americas. The menu and the index on the front page follow those regions. Themes cut across them as tags, so you can read every [walking route](/blog/tag/walking/), every [market guide](/blog/tag/markets/), every [rail trip](/blog/tag/rail/) or every [week we costed](/blog/tag/budget/), wherever it is.

Good places to start are [Lisbon on foot](/blog/lisbon-walking-route/), [what a week in Porto costs](/blog/porto-prices-week/) and [where to stay in Tokyo](/blog/tokyo-neighbourhoods-first-visit/).

## How we check what we publish

- **We walk it.** Every route is walked at least twice by the writer, at different times of day, before it goes live.
- **We say when.** Prices change fast, so every cost is "about" a figure and comes with the month we paid it. We never print a price we did not pay or see ourselves.
- **A second reader.** Each post is read by a second writer before it goes live, and the post names them.
- **We update.** When a route closes or a price moves a lot, we change the post and say what changed.

## How to reach us
