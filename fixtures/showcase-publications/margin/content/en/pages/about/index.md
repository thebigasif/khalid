I am Nell Ashby, and I write personal essays from a house on the coast. Nell Ashby, the house and everything on this site are made up, to show Khalid's Margin template.

## What I write about

Most of my essays begin on my morning walk: the path, the tide, the weather and the birds. Others are about the work of writing itself, and about the small, repeated jobs of a home, like mending and growing food. A new essay comes out most months.

## Notes in the margin

My essays carry small notes. On a wide screen they sit in the margin beside the text, where you can read them or ignore them. On a phone they follow the essay. The notes are where I put the asides I could not bear to cut.

## Guest essays

Now and then a friend writes a guest essay. [Owen Rees](/blog/authors/owen-rees/) wrote about [his father's allotment](/blog/my-fathers-allotment/).

## The monthly letter

Once a month I send a letter with the new essay and three things I read that month. It is free, there are no adverts, and every letter has a link to leave.

## Get in touch

Write to nell@example.com. I read everything and answer most things, more slowly in summer. Start with [walking the same path](/blog/walking-same-path/) if you are new here.
