# The Marlbeck Courier: art direction

The Marlbeck Courier is the made-up local paper of Marlbeck, a made-up market town on a river. Its photographs should look like a staff photographer's week in a small town: the river, the town hall, a market stall in the rain, a school library, a pitch, a park in autumn. Plain daylight and ordinary weather, never a tourist brochure.

## Tone

- **Daylight and weather, as they were.** Rain, low autumn sun and bare winter trees are welcome, because a local paper reports the weather. The two night photographs, the fireworks and the lit high street, keep their streets and crowd readable.
- **Places and things first.** People appear at a distance or as part of a scene: pupils in a library, players with the referee, a trader at his stall, a crowd under fireworks. No face is the subject of a photograph.
- **Close details between the scenes.** A young tree's first leaves and a tower of secondhand books break up the wider views.
- **Little to read.** No brand or shop name is readable at the size the template shows. The library's small sign is the one exception and names no place.
- **Nowhere real by name.** Every photograph was taken somewhere other than Marlbeck, which does not exist. Alt text describes what is visible and never says the photo shows Marlbeck.
- **Free to use.** Every photograph is CC0 or public domain, from Wikimedia Commons or the WordPress photo directory, so none needs a visible credit. Each is still listed on the photo credits page.

## Palette

Greens and brick with grey river light: grass, autumn leaves, a green library interior, red brick buildings and stone bridges, with the grey water of the flood and the river. This sits well beside the template's newsprint ground and the sample brand's deep green, which stays the page's colour for flags, places and links. The night photographs add warm gold.

## Subject mix

Of the 13 photographs, 3 are close details and 10 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (3):** a young tree's leaves, a stack of secondhand books, vegetables on a market stall.
- **Wider scenes (10):** a flooded riverside path, a stone arch bridge, a brick town hall, a school library, a football pitch, an autumn park path, cricket nets under floodlights, fireworks over a crowd, a high street at night, a brick library.

Five of the 18 stories have no photograph, as short news stories often do. Gazette shows a photograph only for the top story, each section's newest story and on the story itself, so these stories read as headlines.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top story | 3:2 | 1440 to 1600 px wide | The template crops to 3:2 from the same file. |
| Section block | 16:9 | 1440 to 1600 px wide | The template crops to 16:9. The bookshop photograph is portrait and crops to the middle of the stack. |
| Story lead | as shot | 1440 to 1600 px wide | Shown at the column width with its caption. |
| Reporter mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn lines of type, ink and green on a light green tint. No photographs of people. |
