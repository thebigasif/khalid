Marlbeck's sixth form open evening on Thursday 12 November will be held at the leisure centre on Station Road, because the school hall is being rebuilt. The school expects about 600 visitors, so the main sports hall has been booked for the night.

Head of the college Daniel Okoro said families should not let the change put them off. "It will look like a sports hall full of stalls, because it is," he said. "Every subject will be there, and so will students who can tell you what it is really like."

## What happens at the sixth form open evening

The sixth form open evening runs from 6pm to 8:30pm. There are short talks in the studio at 6:15pm and 7:15pm about choosing subjects, applying and how the college supports students who want to go to university, start an apprenticeship or go straight into work.

Each subject has a stall, staffed by teachers and current students. The college offers 24 A level subjects and four vocational courses. New this year are A levels in computer science and environmental science.

## How to get there

The leisure centre is a ten-minute walk from the school, along Station Road. There is parking for 120 cars at the leisure centre and more at the station car park, which is free after 6pm. Buses 12 and 31 stop outside.

The Mill Lane bridge is still open on 12 November, but it closes four days later, so [check the bridge closure](/blog/mill-lane-bridge-closure/) if you visit the school after that.

## Applying

Applications open on the evening and close on 31 January. Students from any school can apply, and many decide at the sixth form open evening. The college takes about 280 students a year, and last year every student who met the entry grades got a place. More on [sixth form study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_form) and the courses is on the school's own pages.
