Marlbeck's bonfire night returns to Riverside Park on Saturday 7 November, run by the Rotary club for the 42nd year. The organisers expect about 5,000 people. Gates open at 5:30pm, the bonfire is lit at 6:30pm and the fireworks start at 7:15pm.

Organiser Phil Beddows said the display would be the longest yet. "Twenty minutes of fireworks, set to music for the first time," he said. "And the bonfire is built from the pallets the market traders saved for us all year."

## Bonfire night timings

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5:30pm | Gates open, food stalls and fairground rides start |
| 6:15pm | Lantern parade from the market square to the park |
| 6:30pm | The bonfire is lit |
| 7:15pm | Fireworks, about 20 minutes |
| 9:00pm | Rides close and the park empties |

Entry costs £5 for adults and £2 for children aged 5 to 15. Under-fives go free. All the money goes to local charities, after costs.

## Road closures and parking

Riverside Road closes from 4pm to 10pm, between the Mill Lane bridge and the boatyard. Bridge End is closed to traffic from 6pm to 8pm while the lantern parade passes. Parking is free after 4pm in the station car park and the leisure centre car park, ten minutes' walk from the park. There is no parking at Riverside Park itself, apart from blue badge spaces, which must be booked with the Rotary club in advance.

Buses run as normal, with an extra late bus to Fellgate and Ashby Cross at 9:30pm from the market square.

## What to leave at home

Sparklers, fireworks, glass bottles and dogs are not allowed in the park. Dog owners are asked to keep pets indoors from 7pm, since the noise carries across town. The RSPCA's advice on keeping pets calm during [bonfire night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Fawkes_Night) is on its own pages.
