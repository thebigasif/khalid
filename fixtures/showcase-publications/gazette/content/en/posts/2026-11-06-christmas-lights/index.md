Marlbeck's Christmas lights switch-on will be on Saturday 28 November in the market square, three weeks after [bonfire night](/blog/bonfire-night-guide/). This year the lights reach Corn Street for the first time, after traders there raised £3,500 to join the scheme.

The lights will be switched on at 5pm by pupils from the town's primary schools, who won a drawing competition run by the Courier and the town hall. Their winning designs are printed on the banners that hang across the square.

## The Christmas lights switch-on evening

- **From 2pm:** the Saturday market stays open until 7pm, with extra food stalls.
- **3:30pm:** a children's lantern workshop in the Corn Exchange, free, no booking.
- **4:15pm:** the Marlbeck Community Choir sings carols on the steps of the town hall.
- **5pm:** the lights go on, followed by a short fireworks display from the church tower.
- **5:15pm to 8pm:** shops on Cobb Street, Corn Street and the square stay open late.

The market square is closed to traffic from noon. The car parks are free from 3pm, as they are on every Saturday in December.

## The new lights

The town's Christmas lights were last replaced in 2016. This year £18,000 went on low-energy lights for the square and the high street, which should cut the electricity bill for the lights by two thirds. The Corn Street lights are paid for by the traders, with £1,000 from the [draft budget](/blog/council-tax-bands/).

The newly reopened [Cobb Street bookshop](/blog/cobb-street-bookshop/) will hold a story hour for children at 4pm, and the [silent film club](/blog/silent-film-club/) shows a short comedy in the Corn Exchange at 6pm.

## Advent and after

The lights stay on every evening until 6 January, from 4pm to midnight. The town hall is looking for volunteers to help put them up on 21 and 22 November. Strings of electric [Christmas lights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_lights) have lit the square since 1957, when the first set was bought with money raised at a dance.
