Marlbeck Town are in the county cup quarter-final for the first time since 2015, after a winner in the 89th minute beat Ashby Rovers 2-1 at Hollins Road on Saturday. A crowd of 640, the biggest of the season, saw substitute Jordan Pike head in a corner to settle a tight derby.

Manager Steve Lister said the win was for the supporters who stayed in the rain. "Nobody left," he said. "They were still singing when we went 1-0 down, and that is why we came back."

## How Marlbeck Town won

Ashby took the lead after 23 minutes, when their striker turned in a low cross at the far post. Town struggled to get going on a heavy pitch, and Ashby's goalkeeper made two good saves before half time.

Lister changed the shape at the break and sent on two fresh wingers. The equaliser came on 67 minutes: captain Dan Hollis won the ball in midfield and hit a shot from 20 yards that went in off the post. Both sides had chances to win it before Pike rose at the near post from Hollis's corner with a minute left.

## The quarter-final draw

The cup quarter-final draw is on Tuesday evening. Town could face a club from a higher league, which would mean a gate share worth up to £4,000 to the club, going by last season's figures (2025). Chairman Pete Ward said the money would go towards new floodlights. "The bulbs are older than some of the players," he said.

Hollins Road has been Marlbeck Town's home since 1921. The rules of the county cup follow the [association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) cup format, with extra time and penalties if a quarter-final is drawn.
