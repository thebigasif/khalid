Marlbeck library on Church Walk will extend its Saturday hours from 7 November and stay open until 4pm, three hours longer than now. The county council cut the Saturday hours to the mornings in 2023 to save money. The extra hours are possible because 24 volunteers have offered to help staff them.

Librarian Grace Pemberton said the Saturday afternoon was the time families most wanted. "Parents work in the week, and Saturday morning is swimming and football," she said. "We kept hearing that by the time they got here, we were shut."

## New hours at Marlbeck library

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday and Thursday | 9:30am to 7pm |
| Wednesday and Friday | 9:30am to 5pm |
| Saturday | 9:30am to 4pm |
| Sunday | Closed |

A paid member of staff is always on duty. Volunteers help at the desk, shelve books and run the children's corner.

## What is on at the library

On Saturday afternoons there will be a story time for under-fives at 2pm and a games club for older children at 3pm. The library also lends laptops, has free computers and wifi, and holds a help session for people who need support with online forms every Tuesday evening.

The library runs class visits for every primary school in the area. Fellgate Primary, which has just opened [its own new library](/blog/fellgate-school-library/), will keep its visits on Thursday mornings.

## Volunteers

The library is still looking for volunteers, especially to cover the new Saturday hours. Volunteers are asked to give three hours a fortnight. The county council trains them and pays their travel. Ask at the desk or write to the librarian.

Several of the new volunteers came from the [repair café](/blog/repair-cafe-first-year/), which meets in the church hall next door on the last Saturday of the month. Pemberton said the two groups now share a noticeboard and a kettle. "Somebody brings a broken lamp to the café, then comes over here for a book while they wait," she said. "On a good Saturday it feels like the middle of town again."

## Why Saturday hours matter

A survey of library members in the spring asked when they would most like the library to be open. More than half chose Saturday afternoon, ahead of weekday evenings. Families with children under ten were the most likely to choose it, and many said they had stopped visiting since the cut. The longer Saturday hours will be reviewed after six months.

Public libraries in England are run by county councils, which must by law provide a [public library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_library) service that is comprehensive and efficient.
