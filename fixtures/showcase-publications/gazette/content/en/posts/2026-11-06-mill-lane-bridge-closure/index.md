The Mill Lane bridge will close to all traffic from Monday 16 November for six weeks while the county council repairs its stone arch and resurfaces the road. The bridge carries about 6,000 vehicles a day across the River Marl, so drivers face a diversion of nearly three kilometres through the town centre.

People on foot and on bikes can still cross for most of the closure. A temporary footbridge goes up beside the arch on the first weekend, and stays until the work ends.

## Why the Mill Lane bridge needs repairs

An inspection in the summer found loose stones in the arch and water getting into the road above it. The bridge is 220 years old and listed. County engineer Tom Pickering said the repairs could not wait for spring. "Frost gets into wet stone and splits it," he said. "If we left it another winter, the job next year would be far bigger."

The work replaces the loose stones with matching stone from the same quarry, seals the arch and lays a new road surface with a waterproof layer under it.

## Diversions and buses

| Route | Change during the closure |
|---|---|
| Cars and vans | Signed diversion via Station Road and the Corn Street bridge |
| Lorries over 7.5 tonnes | Signed diversion via the A-road bypass |
| Bus 12 to Fellgate | Runs via Station Road, stops on Mill Lane moved to Corn Street |
| Bus 31 to Ashby Cross | No change |
| School buses | Marlbeck High buses leave 10 minutes earlier |

The [new Marlbeck High bus route](/blog/high-school-bus-route/) that started after half term also uses the diversion, so families on the Northfield estate should allow extra time.

## On foot and by bike

The footbridge opens on Saturday 14 November. It has a ramp at each end, so it works for wheelchairs and buggies. For two nights in December, when the crane lifts the last stones, the footbridge closes from 8pm to 6am and a free minibus runs between the two ends of the bridge every 20 minutes.

## When it reopens

The county council expects to reopen the bridge on Monday 28 December. Any change will be posted on the county's [road works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadworks) pages and here. The bridge work is separate from the [flood wall plan](/blog/flood-wall-approved/), which starts in March.
