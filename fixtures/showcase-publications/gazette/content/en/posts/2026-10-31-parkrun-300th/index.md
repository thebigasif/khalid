The Riverside parkrun held its 300th Saturday run this morning, and 412 runners and walkers turned out to mark it, more than twice the usual number. The free five-kilometre run has started at the bandstand in Riverside Park every Saturday at 9am since 2019, apart from the months it was stopped in 2020 and 2021.

Run director Helen Marsh, who has volunteered at all but eleven of the 300 events, set off the runners with a cowbell. "We started with 38 people and a borrowed stopwatch," she said. "This morning I could not see the end of the queue for the finish tokens."

## The Riverside parkrun in numbers

Since its first run, the Riverside parkrun has had:

- 9,840 different runners and walkers;
- 58,200 finishes in total;
- 1,130 different volunteers, from timekeepers to marshals.

The course record is 15 minutes 41 seconds, set in 2023. On the same morning, Marlbeck Town fans [celebrated a cup quarter-final](/blog/town-cup-quarter-final/) a few hundred metres away. The fastest runner today finished in 17 minutes 2 seconds, and the last walker, an 84-year-old who walks every week, came in at 58 minutes to the loudest cheer of the morning.

## Volunteers wanted

Every run needs about 20 volunteers. Marsh said the team is short of people for the winter, when the course is muddy and the mornings are cold. "You do not need to run," she said. "You need a coat and an hour." Volunteers sign up on the parkrun pages. Several also helped at the [Northfield tree planting](/blog/northfield-tree-planting/) this autumn.

## The course this winter

The course follows the river path for most of its length. When the [flood wall work](/blog/flood-wall-approved/) starts in March, the river path between the bridge and the boatyard closes, and the run will move to a course of three laps of the park. The team will test the new course in February.

Parkrun began in London in 2004 and now holds free timed runs in more than 20 countries, according to its [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkrun). The Riverside event is one of three in the county.
