The Marlbeck Courier is the local news site for Marlbeck, a market town on the River Marl, and the villages of Fellgate and Ashby Cross. We report what the councils decide, what happens in the schools, which shops open and close, how the town's teams did at the weekend and what is on in the diary. We publish every weekday morning, and at weekends when there is news.

Marlbeck, its councils and everyone named in our stories are made up. The Marlbeck Courier is a demonstration publication, written to show how a local paper with many sections reads in this template.

## Who writes here

- **Priya Rowntree** is the editor. She covers the council, the river and community news.
- **Callum Ash** covers schools in the week and sport at the weekend.
- **Hannah Okafor** covers business, the market and what's on.

## How we check a story

Every story names where its facts come from: a meeting, a report, a club or a person who was there. A second member of the newsroom reads each story before it goes live. When we get something wrong, we correct the story and say what changed at the foot of it.

## How to reach us

Write to newsdesk@example.com, or use the link at the foot of any story. The photographs on this site are shared by their photographers under CC0 or as public domain, and none of them shows Marlbeck. The [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
