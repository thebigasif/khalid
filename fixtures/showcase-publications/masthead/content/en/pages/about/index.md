The Common Room is a small monthly magazine of culture, cities, nature, food and ideas. We publish long, unhurried stories about the places and habits a town shares: cinemas, benches, libraries, markets and the coast. The Common Room, its writers and its stories are made up, to show the Masthead template.

## What we cover

Our stories sit in five sections: [culture](/blog/category/culture/), [cities](/blog/category/cities/), [nature](/blog/category/nature/), [food](/blog/category/food/) and [ideas](/blog/category/ideas/). A good place to start is [a Saturday market, stall by stall](/blog/saturday-market/).

## Who we are

- **Ruth Okafor** edits the magazine and writes about libraries, newspapers and public places.
- **Daniel Marsh** writes about streets, transport, travel and the coast.
- **Isla Whitmore** writes about food, markets, growing and the countryside.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their stories.

## The monthly issue

Once a month we send one email with every new story, and nothing else. Sign up at the foot of any page.

## How to reach us

Write to letters@example.com. We read every letter and print the best in the next issue. The photographs on this site are in the public domain or shared under CC0; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
