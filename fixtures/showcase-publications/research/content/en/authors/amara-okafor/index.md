Amara leads the Tidemark Coastal Lab. She studies how salt marshes and seagrass meadows store carbon and how they keep pace with rising seas. She started the lab's long-term marsh plots in 2009 and still measures them every spring. She teaches the coastal ecology course and supervises the lab's PhD students.
