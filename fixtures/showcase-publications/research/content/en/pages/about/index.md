Tidemark Coastal Lab is a coastal ecology group at Northsound University's marine station. We study seagrass meadows, salt marshes and oyster reefs: how they store carbon, protect the shore, shelter young fish and recover when they are damaged. Tidemark, Northsound University and every paper on this site are made up, to show the Research template.

Each post on this site is either a short summary of one of our papers, written by its lead author, or lab news. A paper's post lists its journal, year, DOI and citations, and ends with a box that gives the citation to copy.

## Who works here

- **Amara Okafor** leads the lab and the salt marsh work.
- **Lukas Lindqvist** is a postdoctoral researcher who studies oyster reefs and fish.
- **Mei Tanaka** is a PhD student who maps seagrass from the air.
- **Rafael Mendes** runs the boats and field kit, and looks after the lab's data.

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists everyone with their papers.

## What we study

Our work falls into four research areas: [seagrass meadows](/blog/category/seagrass-meadows/), [salt marshes and carbon](/blog/category/salt-marshes/), [oyster reefs](/blog/category/oyster-reefs/) and [methods and data](/blog/category/methods-data/). Good places to start are our paper on [whether marshes can keep pace with rising seas](/blog/marsh-elevation-sea-level/) and the newest one on [turtles and seagrass recovery](/blog/turtles-seagrass-recovery/).

## Open data

Every dataset behind a paper is open, with a plain note on how it was collected and checked. Rafael reviews each one before it is published.

## How to reach us

Write to lab@example.com and a member of the lab answers within a week. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
