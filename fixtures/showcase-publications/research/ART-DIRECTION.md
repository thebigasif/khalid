# Tidemark Coastal Lab: art direction

Tidemark Coastal Lab is a made-up coastal ecology group. Its photographs should look like field work on a real coast: the habitats the lab studies, the water and the kit, in plain daylight. They are evidence, not decoration.

## Tone

- **The habitat is the subject.** Seagrass, marsh, mudflat, reef and water fill the frame. People may appear at work, small in the frame, but no face is the subject of a picture.
- **Plain daylight.** Overcast or clear light, true colour. No sunsets, no heavy filters, no dramatic skies.
- **Nothing to read.** No signs, labels or text in the picture. A first candidate for the marsh elevation post, a marsh with an information board, was turned down for this reason. The new boat carries a small agency emblem on its cabin; it is too small to read at card size.

## Palette

Cool sea colours with the warm colours of the marsh:

- blues and greens of shallow water and seagrass;
- the gold, rust and green of cordgrass;
- the greys and browns of mud and shell.

These sit well beside the sample brand's deep sea blue and the template's white page. Avoid photographs dominated by red or purple.

## Subject mix

Of the 11 photographs, 5 are close details and 6 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (5):** a green turtle grazing seagrass, eelgrass blades, an oyster block reef, autumn marsh grass, an oyster reef at low tide.
- **Wider scenes (6):** a cordgrass marsh with channels, a tidal creek, volunteers planting a marsh, a small boat on green water from above, a research boat under way, mudflats at low tide.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a post | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the text width, about 37.5rem. Sources are 4:3 or close to 3:2, so a 3:2 crop loses little. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

The Research template shows no pictures in its lists: papers are a text register. Only the post page shows the lead picture.

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead picture fills the width. Each lead has its subject in the middle third: the turtle, the oyster blocks, the boat and the small sampling boat are centred, and the marsh, mudflat and seagrass pictures are even textures that look the same at any crop.
