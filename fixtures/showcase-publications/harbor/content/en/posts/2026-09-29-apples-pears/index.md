The short rule for apples and pears is this: bake the sharp, firm apples and the hard, slightly underripe pears, and eat the sweet, juicy apples and the soft, ripe pears raw. Sharp apples keep their shape and taste bright after an hour in the oven, while sweet ones turn to mush and lose their flavour. Pears work the other way round from what most people expect: the ripe ones that are best raw fall apart in the oven.

Autumn is when apples and pears fill our boxes, and it is also when we get the most questions about them. Here is how to choose between them without memorising variety names, which change from farm to farm.

## Two questions to ask in the shop

You can sort almost any apple with two questions:

1. **Is it sharp or sweet?** Ask for a taste, or read the label. Sharp apples make your mouth water.
2. **Is it firm or tender?** Press the skin near the stalk. A firm apple feels like wood. A tender one gives a little.

Sharp and firm means baking. Sweet and tender means eating. Sweet and firm, which many popular apples are, does both: it bakes into slices that hold together, and it eats well raw.

## A quick guide to apples and pears

| Fruit | Taste | Texture | Best for | Keeps in the fridge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sharp green cooking apple | Very sharp | Firm, breaks down | Sauces, crumbles, purées | 2 to 3 months |
| Sharp red eating apple | Sharp and sweet | Firm, crisp | Tarts, baking whole, raw | 6 to 8 weeks |
| Sweet red eating apple | Sweet | Crisp, juicy | Eating raw, salads | 4 to 6 weeks |
| Soft early apple | Mild, sweet | Tender | Eating within a week | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Long, firm pear, underripe | Mild | Hard | Poaching, roasting | 3 to 4 weeks |
| Round, juicy pear, ripe | Sweet, fragrant | Soft, melting | Eating raw, cheese boards | 3 to 5 days |

The keeping times come from our own cold room, where the fruit stays at about 3°C. A home fridge is a little warmer, so allow a week or two less.

## How to tell when a pear is ripe

Pears ripen from the inside out, so a pear that feels soft all over is already past its best inside. Press gently at the neck, just below the stalk. When it gives slightly, the pear is ripe. Most pears reach the shop hard, so leave them on the counter for two to five days and check the neck each day.

## How to keep apples and pears fresh

- **Keep them apart from vegetables.** Apples and pears give off [ethylene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethylene_as_a_plant_hormone), a ripening gas that makes greens yellow and potatoes sprout.
- **Store apples cold.** The fridge salad drawer keeps them crisp for weeks. On the counter they soften in days.
- **Ripen pears warm, then chill them.** Once a pear is ripe at the neck, the fridge holds it for a few days more.
- **Check for bruises.** One bruised apple can spoil those around it. Use it first, in a sauce.

:::callout{kind=tip}
A soft apple is not a wasted apple. Peel it, chop it and cook it with a splash of water for ten minutes. Apple sauce keeps a week in the fridge and goes on porridge, yoghurt or pork.
:::
