Harbor Kitchen packs five dinners a week for home cooks who want to cook on a weeknight without planning every meal from scratch. Each box holds the produce, the measured pantry items and a recipe card for each dinner, and every recipe is tested three times in an ordinary home kitchen before it goes in.

Harbor Kitchen Notes is our blog. We write it for anyone who cooks at home, whether or not they buy a box.

## Who writes here

- **Ruth Calder** is our head cook. She writes every recipe and the posts on planning and weeknight cooking.
- **Joel Amari** leads produce and sourcing. He works with every farm that grows for us and writes about what is in season.
- **Nell Whitcombe** tests our recipes on beginners and writes about kitchen skills.
- **Theo Brandt** runs the packing kitchen and writes about what happens behind its door.

## What we write about

We write about planning a week of dinners, choosing and storing produce, the kitchen skills that make every recipe easier, and how our kitchen works. Good places to start are [how to plan a week of dinners in twenty minutes](/blog/plan-week-dinners/), [how we test a recipe](/blog/test-every-recipe/) and [how we choose our farms](/blog/choose-farms/).

## How we check what we publish

Every post is read by a second member of the kitchen team before it goes live, and the post names them. When we test something, such as how long herbs last in paper, we say how we tested it and in which kitchen. We do not publish numbers we have not measured ourselves, and nobody pays to be mentioned.

## How to reach us

Write to hello@example.com and a person from the kitchen team answers within two working days. The photographs on this blog come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
