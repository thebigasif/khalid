Ruth writes every Harbor Kitchen recipe and cooks each one three times in an ordinary home kitchen before it goes in a box. Before Harbor Kitchen she ran the line at two busy neighbourhood restaurants, where she learned to plan a service so nothing waits and nothing goes to waste. Here she writes about planning a week of dinners, batch cooking and getting food on the table on a weeknight without stress.
