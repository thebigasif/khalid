Joel chooses the farms that grow for Harbor Kitchen and decides what goes in each week's box. He spent eight years on the floor of a wholesale produce market, where he learned to judge a crate of tomatoes by smell before he looked at the price. He writes about what is in season, how to pick it and how to keep it fresh at home.
