Nell taught evening cookery classes for twelve years before she joined Harbor Kitchen to test our recipes on people who are new to cooking. If a step confuses a beginner, she rewrites it. She writes about the skills that make every recipe easier: holding a knife, keeping it sharp, cooking rice and making stock.
