Home buying costs beyond the deposit came to 13,000 on a made-up home priced at 300,000, which is more than four tenths of the 30,000 deposit itself. They include purchase tax, legal fees, a survey, lender fees, moving and the first repairs. Most first-time buyers plan for the deposit and are surprised by the rest, so this post adds them up in the order you meet them.

Every country names and sets home buying costs differently. Some charge a large purchase tax and others none; some have notaries, others solicitors or conveyancers. The list below uses made-up round numbers to show the kinds of cost and their size relative to each other. Put your own local figures in their place.

:::callout{kind=note}
**This is not financial advice.** Taxes, fees and rules for buying a home differ by country and region. Check them with the official sources where you live before you set your target.
:::

## The home in this example

The home is priced at 300,000. The buyer puts down a deposit of one tenth, 30,000, and borrows the rest. Our post on [how long a home deposit takes to save](/blog/saving-home-deposit/) works out the deposit. This post covers the other home buying costs.

## Home buying costs before you get the keys

| Cost | When you pay it | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Survey of the building | Before you commit | 600 |
| Lender's valuation | When you apply | 300 |
| Lender's arrangement fee | When the loan is agreed | 1,000 |
| Legal fees and searches | Through the sale | 1,500 |
| Purchase tax, 2 in 100 of the price | On completion | 6,000 |
| Land registration | On completion | 400 |
| **Before the keys** | | **9,800** |

Purchase tax is the largest item in most places that charge it, and it usually rises with the price. Some places lower or waive it for first-time buyers. This single line can change the total more than all the others together, so check it first.

Some lenders let you add the arrangement fee to the loan. That lowers the cash you need now but means paying interest on the fee for the life of the loan, as our guide to [APR and interest rates](/blog/apr-interest-rate/) shows for smaller loans.

## Costs on and after moving day

| Cost | When you pay it | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Removal van or moving help | Moving day | 900 |
| Building insurance, first year | From completion | 300 |
| First repairs and essentials | First three months | 2,000 |
| **On and after moving day** | | **3,200** |
| **All costs beyond the deposit** | | **13,000** |

"First repairs and essentials" covers what every new home seems to need: a lock changed, curtains that fit, a broken tap, a few tins of paint. It is the line buyers most often leave out, and the one most likely to go on a credit card if they do.

## The full cash target

| Part | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Deposit | 30,000 |
| Costs before the keys | 9,800 |
| Costs on and after moving day | 3,200 |
| **Cash needed in total** | **43,000** |

A buyer who saves only for the 30,000 deposit, and forgets the home buying costs, is 13,000 short on the day. At 500 a month, that is about two more years of saving. Plan for the full figure from the start, or you may find a home you can afford and not be able to complete.

## The yearly costs of owning

Buying also changes your yearly costs. A tenant's landlord pays for repairs; an owner pays for them. These are the yearly costs this made-up home brings, which belong in your [sinking funds for once-a-year bills](/blog/sinking-funds-yearly-bills/).

| Yearly cost of owning | Amount a year | Set aside each month |
|---|---:|---:|
| Maintenance and repairs, about 1 in 100 of the price | 3,000 | 250 |
| Building insurance | 300 | 25 |
| Local property tax or service charge | 1,200 | 100 |
| **Total** | **4,500** | **375** |

The maintenance line is a rule of thumb, not a bill. In a quiet year you spend little; in the year the boiler fails, you spend more than the line. Over ten years it tends to even out.

## A checklist before you start viewing

Use this list to estimate your own home buying costs before you look at a single home.

1. Find the purchase tax for your price, region and situation.
2. Ask two firms for a written quote for legal fees.
3. Ask lenders for their fees and whether they can be added to the loan.
4. Add a fixed sum for moving and first repairs.
5. Add up the yearly costs of owning and compare them with your rent.

For a general picture of what these fees are called around the world, the reference page on [closing costs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closing_costs) lists the common ones.
