Miriam edits every issue of The Ledger Review and writes its budgeting studies. She spent twelve years turning household spending surveys into tables for a regional statistics office, and she still reads a budget the way she read those surveys: line by line, with a pencil. She started the Review because most money advice skips the arithmetic, and the arithmetic is the part a household can check for itself.
