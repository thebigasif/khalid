# Fernbrook Press: art direction

Fernbrook Press is a made-up studio that restores old prints and drawings and prints them again. Its pictures are the work it shows, so they must read as one studio's taste: printed pictures with fine lines, flat colour and the texture of paper.

## Public domain only

Every picture is an old plate, print or textile by its own artist, from Wikimedia Commons: in the public domain, or a museum's scan shared under CC0. No CC BY picture is used, so none needs a visible credit; each is still credited on the picture credits page and in `ATTRIBUTION.md`. The posts never claim the studio made the original work: each project is a restoration and a new edition.

## Tone

- **Printed pictures.** Natural history plates, botanical plates, cyanotypes, Japanese woodblock prints and a textile. No photographs of places or people.
- **The paper shows.** A plate keeps its margin, its tone and its small marks, so each picture reads as a thing on a wall.
- **Words on a plate stay small.** Some plates carry a plant's name or a printer's line at the foot. None has large text.

## Palette

Deep blues (cyanotype, ultramarine, the Great Wave), coral and vermilion (Red Fuji, the flamingo, Haemanthus), sea greens and the warm cream of old paper. They sit well on the template's plaster ground and the sample brand's ultramarine.

## Subject mix

Of the 20 pictures, 12 are tall plates and prints, 5 are close to square and 3 are wide.

- **Natural history (8):** jellyfish, siphonophores, sea anemones, hummingbirds, a flamingo, a kingfisher, two doves, a pair of great tits.
- **Botanical (6):** three cyanotypes of algae, two plates from Surinam, one Haemanthus.
- **Woodblock (4):** rain on a bridge, a plum garden, Red Fuji, the Great Wave.
- **Pattern (2):** two printings of Strawberry Thief.
- **Shapes:** the wall's columns mix tall and wide pictures, so no row lines up and the wall reads as a hang, not a grid.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall | The picture's own | 1600 px wide | Never cropped. Drawn on a mat a quarter to a fifth of the frame wide. |
| Project plate | The picture's own | 1600 px wide | Never cropped, at most 82% of the screen's height, in the middle of its mat. |
| Body figure | The picture's own | 1600 px wide | In the reading column, on a mat. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | Drawn, one ink colour on a light tint of the same ink. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

Nothing is cropped at any width. On a phone the wall has two columns, so a tall print is about 170 px wide; every lead picture has one large shape in the middle that still reads at that size.

## Sources and licences

Every picture was fetched from Wikimedia Commons through `scripts/demo-photo.py`, which read its licence (Public Domain Mark or CC0) on the file's own page and kept it at most 1600 px wide. `photos.lock` records each one.
