Fernbrook Press is a small studio that restores old prints and drawings and prints them again. Museums, publishers, shops and festivals ask us to turn a plate from an old book into a poster, a calendar, a book cover or a pattern. Fernbrook Press and its people are made up, to show Khalid's Gallery template.

## Whose pictures these are

Every picture on this site was made by its own artist long ago: Ernst Haeckel, Anna Atkins, Maria Sibylla Merian, Pierre-Joseph Redouté, Utagawa Hiroshige, Katsushika Hokusai, John James Audubon, William Morris and Japanese print makers of the early 1900s. Each is in the public domain or shared under CC0 by the museum that holds it. The [picture credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page links to every original. None of them has any part in this site.

## The project details

Each project shows four details beside its title: the client, the year, the medium and the size of the edition. On the front page each work hangs on the wall with a number, and the list of works under the wall uses the same numbers.

## Who works here

- **Maren Holt** restores the plates and writes most of the notes.
- **Tomas Kerr** runs the press room and writes about paper and print.

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists both, with their work.

## Finding a project

The work is grouped by discipline: [natural history](/blog/category/natural-history/), [botanical](/blog/category/botanical/), [woodblock](/blog/category/woodblock/) and [pattern](/blog/category/pattern/). The [archive](/blog/archive/) lists every project, newest first. A good place to start is [Haeckel's jellyfish](/blog/jellyfish-plates/).

## Asking about a commission

Write to hello@example.com with the picture you have in mind and what you want to make of it. One of us answers within a week.
