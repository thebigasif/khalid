# Larkmoor Rovers: art direction

Larkmoor Rovers is a made-up amateur football club. Its photographs should look like a Saturday at a small club ground: the pitch, the goals, the ball and the kit, in plain daylight. No real club may be named or recognised.

## Tone

- **The ground is the subject.** Pitches, goals, nets, lines, flags and balls fill the frame. People may appear only far off and small, never as the subject, and never with a readable shirt or crest.
- **No real clubs.** No crests, club names, sponsors or signs in the picture. Candidates with a club's crest on a corner flag, a sponsor's board or a branded ball were turned down. The balls in the Kingsmoor and Wexcombe pictures carry no readable maker's name at card size.
- **Weather is welcome.** Fog, frost and grey skies are part of amateur football. No night games under strong colour casts, no heavy filters.

## Palette

Pitch greens with the white of lines and nets, and the greys and pale blues of a winter sky. These sit well beside the sample brand's navy and chalk and the template's white page. Avoid pictures dominated by red or by a club's colours.

## Subject mix

Of the 12 photographs, 6 are close details and 6 are wider scenes.

- **Close details (6):** goalkeeper gloves with a cone, a net on a striped pitch, a fallen corner flag, a ball on grass, a ball on the centre spot, the halfway line.
- **Wider scenes (6):** a village pitch behind a rail, a small goal in fog, a view through a net across a muddy pitch, a pitch with a floodlight pole, a goal on a frosty, foggy pitch, a playing field under hills.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Source size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead picture on a post | 3:2 | 1600 px wide | Shown at the text width, about 44rem. Most sources are 4:3 or 3:2. The gloves are close to square and crop to the gloves and cone. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | 160 px SVG | A drawn shirt with the player's number or a letter for staff, one navy ink on a light tint of it. No photographs of people. |

The Fixture template shows no pictures in its lists: matches are scoreboard rows. Only the post page shows the lead picture.

## Phone crop

On a phone the lead picture fills the width. Each detail has its subject in the middle third: the gloves, the ball, the flag and the net. The pitch scenes are even textures of grass and sky that look the same at any crop.
