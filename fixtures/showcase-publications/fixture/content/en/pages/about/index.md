Larkmoor Rovers is an amateur football club at Larkmoor Fields, founded in 1974. We run a first team, a reserve team and eight junior teams, and we are open to everyone. Larkmoor Rovers, its league, its opponents and every match on this site are made up, to show the Fixture template.

Each post on this site is a match report, a preview of the next match or club news. A match lists the opponent, the score or kick-off, the ground and the competition, and the front page shows the club's form over the last five results.

## The teams

- **The first team** plays in the Vale and District League, Division One, and in the County Cup. [Marcus Bell](/blog/authors/marcus-bell/) manages it.
- **The reserves** play in the Vale and District Reserve League.
- **The juniors** have eight teams from under-8s to under-14s. [Priya Nair](/blog/authors/priya-nair/) coordinates them.

The [squad and staff page](/blog/authors/) lists the players and volunteers.

## Where we play

Home matches are at Larkmoor Fields on Mill Lane, behind the pavilion, on Saturdays at 2:30pm. The clubhouse has a tea hut, changing rooms and a bar on match days.

## Joining the club

New players are welcome at any level. Training is on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 7pm. Read about [pre-season training](/blog/pre-season-training-and-new-kit/) for what to expect, or the [under-12s trials](/blog/under-12s-open-trials/) for juniors.

## How to reach us

Write to secretary@example.com and Dee Harker, the club secretary, answers within a few days. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
