Marcus was away at a wedding, so the captain writes this one. We drew 2-2 at Thornlea Athletic on a heavy pitch, and I headed in the equaliser two minutes from time. I will be talking about it for weeks.

## How the game against Thornlea Athletic went

Thornlea Athletic are a good side. They finished third last season, and they pass the ball well on a pitch that slopes from one goal to the other. We played up the slope in the first half and found it hard to get out.

They scored first, after 22 minutes, from a quick free kick that caught us talking. Ola Bakare levelled before half-time. Connor Walsh won the ball high up the pitch and slipped Ola in, and he lifted it over the keeper.

## The second half

Thornlea went ahead again on the hour with a shot from 25 yards that dipped under the bar. Jamie Price got a hand to it, but not enough.

We kept going. Lewis Hart and Connor both went close. In the 88th minute Connor swung in a corner from the right, and I got in front of my marker at the near post. It was my first goal of the season.

## Player of the match

Jamie Price. Two saves in the last five minutes kept us in the game after we equalised.

## The team

Price; Moss, Ridley, Chandra, Okoro; Walsh, Fenwick, Doyle; Hart, Bakare, Lowe. Subs used: Bright for Lowe, Kaur for Fenwick.

A point away at one of the best sides in the league is a fair result. Thanks to the twelve supporters who made the trip. Next is the first round of the County Cup at home to Pellow Bridge.
