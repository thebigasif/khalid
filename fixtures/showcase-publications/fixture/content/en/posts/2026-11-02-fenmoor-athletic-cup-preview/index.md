The draw for the second round of the County Cup was made on Monday night, and we are away at Fenmoor Athletic. The tie is on Saturday 21 November, with a 2pm kick-off. Fenmoor Athletic play in the league above us, so this is the biggest test of our season so far.

## About Fenmoor Athletic

Fenmoor Athletic finished fifth in the Premier Division last season and reached the county semi-final in 2025. They play on a big, open pitch at the edge of the moor, and the wind can be fierce in November. Marcus Bell has seen them twice this season and says they are quick on the break and strong at set pieces.

We last met them in 2019, in a friendly, and lost 3-1. Only Tom Ridley and Jamie Price are still in the squad from that day.

## Travelling to the game

The club has booked a coach for players and supporters. It leaves the clubhouse at noon and comes back straight after the game. Seats are £8 for adults and £4 for juniors. Book with Dee at secretary@example.com by Wednesday 18 November.

If you drive, the ground is about 40 minutes away. Parking at the ground is limited, so please share cars where you can.

## What the County Cup means

The winner of this tie plays at home in the quarter-final in January. The final is in May at a league ground, and the club has never reached it. Our [first-round win over Pellow Bridge](/blog/pellow-bridge-county-cup-report/) was our first cup win in four years.

Before the cup tie, we have a league game at home to Wexcombe Town on 7 November. A [cup competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cup_competition) has no second chances, so we will need every voice at Fenmoor.
