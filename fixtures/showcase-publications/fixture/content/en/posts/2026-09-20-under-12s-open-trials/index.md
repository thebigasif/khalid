Our under-12s are looking for new players, and we are holding open trials on two Saturday mornings: 26 September and 3 October, from 10am to 11:30am at Larkmoor Fields. Girls and boys born between 1 September 2014 and 31 August 2015 are welcome. You do not need to have played for a club before.

## What happens at the open trials

The open trials are not a test. Your child will warm up with the squad, play some small games of four against four, and finish with a match on the small pitch. The coaches watch how players move, pass and talk to each other, not only who scores.

We will tell every family by 10 October whether there is a place this season. If the under-12s squad is full, we offer a place in our development group, which trains on Wednesday evenings and plays friendlies.

## What to bring to the open trials

- Boots for grass, or trainers if the ground is hard.
- Shin pads. We have spares if you forget.
- A water bottle with your child's name on it.
- A coat for whoever comes to watch. The field is open to the wind.

## About the junior section

The [juniors section](/blog/category/juniors/) has eight teams, from under-8s to under-14s, and about 140 players. All 23 of our junior coaches are volunteers, and each one holds a coaching badge and a safeguarding check. Junior subs are £60 for the season, and nobody misses out because of cost: talk to me if that is a worry.

Small-sided games, where each child gets many touches of the ball, are the heart of how we coach. The [Football Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Football_Association) sets the format for each age group, and the under-12s play nine against nine.

To book a place, or to ask anything, write to juniors@example.com. You can also read about [pre-season training](/blog/pre-season-training-and-new-kit/) for the senior teams.
