After the [late defeat at Ambersett](/blog/ambersett-report/), we answered in the best way, with a 4-1 win over Kingsmoor Wanderers at Larkmoor Fields. Ola Bakare scored twice, Lewis Hart and Tom Ridley got one each, and we are back up to second in the league.

## A quick start against Kingsmoor Wanderers

We scored with our first attack. Connor Walsh found Lewis Hart on the left after four minutes, and his low cross was turned in by Ola at the near post. Ola doubled the lead on 19 minutes with a header from Sam Moss's cross.

Kingsmoor Wanderers pulled one back before half-time with a free kick that went through the wall. It was the only shot Jamie Price faced all afternoon.

## The second half

Tom Ridley made it 3-1 on 61 minutes, at the back post from a Connor corner. That is two goals in five games for the captain, and he has told everyone.

Lewis finished the scoring on 78 minutes, when he ran from halfway and slid the ball under the keeper. It was the best goal we have scored this season.

## Player of the match

Ola Bakare. Two goals, and he never stopped running. He now has six for the season.

## The team

Price; Moss, Ridley, Chandra, Okoro; Walsh, Fenwick, Doyle; Hart, Bakare, Lowe. Subs used: Bright for Lowe, Kaur for Doyle, Ellis for Bakare.

A good week. Thanks to the volunteers who ran the tea hut and raised £140 for the [floodlight fund](/blog/floodlight-fund-target/). Next Saturday we travel to Ospring Vale. In football, a [hat-trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat-trick) is three goals in one game, and Ola is still waiting for his first.
