On Saturday we play Wexcombe Town at Larkmoor Fields, kick-off 2:30pm. They are fourth in the league, a point behind us, so a win would open a gap of four points over them.

## Wexcombe Town this season

Wexcombe Town have won three of their five league games. They beat Coldmere United 4-0 last week, and their striker has scored seven goals already. They like to play long balls early, so Tom Ridley and Raj Chandra will be busy.

We beat them twice last season, 2-1 at home and 3-2 away, but they have a new manager and several new players since then.

## Team news

Danny Lowe is back from his holiday. Ben Fenwick has a sore ankle after Ospring Vale and will have a fitness test on Thursday night. Everyone else is fit.

After two league wins in a row, I do not expect to change much. We will keep the same shape, with Ola Bakare up front and Lewis Hart on the left. The squad meets in the clubhouse at 1:30pm, and the warm-up starts on the pitch at 1:50pm.

## Getting to Larkmoor Fields

The ground is on Mill Lane, behind the pavilion. Parking is free in the field by the gate, and the bus stops outside. Entry includes a programme, and under-16s come in free. The [about page](/blog/about/) has the full address and directions.

The tea hut opens at 1:30pm. All money from it this month goes to the [floodlight fund](/blog/floodlight-fund-target/), which has just reached its target but still needs to pay for the new fuse board.

After Saturday, the first team turns to the cup: read the [County Cup preview for Fenmoor Athletic](/blog/fenmoor-athletic-cup-preview/) for travel details. Our last home game was the [4-1 win over Kingsmoor Wanderers](/blog/kingsmoor-wanderers-report/). Bring a scarf and some noise. If you are new to football grounds, the [Wikipedia page on association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) has the basics.
