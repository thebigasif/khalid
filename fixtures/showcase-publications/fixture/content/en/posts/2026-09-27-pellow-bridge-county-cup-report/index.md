We are through to the second round of the County Cup after a 2-0 win over Pellow Bridge at Larkmoor Fields. Lewis Hart scored his first goal for the club, and Ola Bakare added a second late on. Jamie Price kept his first clean sheet of the season.

## A tight first half in the County Cup

Pellow Bridge play a division below us, but they came to defend and did it well. They sat deep with five across the back and made it hard to find space. We had most of the ball and one real chance, when Connor Walsh hit the bar from a free kick.

## The second half

We changed to two up front at half-time, with Ola and Danny Lowe side by side, and the extra body in the box made the difference. On 58 minutes Lewis cut in from the left and curled a shot into the top corner. It was worth the wait.

Pellow Bridge had to come out after that, which left space behind them. Ola used it on 84 minutes, when Connor's long pass sent him clear and he finished calmly.

## Player of the match

Lewis Hart. He kept running at his full-back all afternoon, and his goal broke the deadlock.

## The team

Price; Moss, Ridley, Chandra, Okoro; Walsh, Fenwick, Doyle; Hart, Bakare, Lowe. Subs used: Kaur for Doyle, Bright for Hart.

Thanks to Pellow Bridge for a sporting game, and to their supporters for the long trip. The draw for the second round is next week. The [County Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_cup) is open to every club in the county, and the final is played at a league ground in May. Our [draw at Thornlea](/blog/thornlea-athletic-report/) was the last league game before this one.
