We lost 2-1 at Ambersett, the league leaders, to a goal in the last minute. It was our first defeat of the season, and it hurt, because we were the better side for most of the second half.

## The first half at Ambersett

Ambersett have won every game so far, and it showed. They moved the ball quickly on their artificial pitch and scored after 14 minutes, when their winger got behind Sam Moss and squared it for a tap-in.

We settled after that. Tom Ridley and Raj Chandra won everything in the air, and Jamie Price had little to do for the rest of the half.

## The second half

We drew level on 55 minutes. Ola Bakare was pulled down in the box, and Connor Walsh sent the keeper the wrong way from the penalty spot. For the next half hour we pushed for a winner. Lewis Hart had a shot cleared off the line, and Ola headed over from six yards.

Then, in the 90th minute, we lost the ball on halfway and Ambersett broke three against two. Their number 10 finished well. We had no time to reply.

## Player of the match

Raj Chandra. He made two blocks that kept us in the game in the first half and won every header.

## The team

Price; Moss, Ridley, Chandra, Okoro; Walsh, Fenwick, Doyle; Hart, Bakare, Lowe. Subs used: Bright for Lowe, Kaur for Fenwick.

We learn from this one. The lads were gutted, but we matched the best side in the league on their own pitch. Next is Kingsmoor Wanderers at home. Our [cup win over Pellow Bridge](/blog/pellow-bridge-county-cup-report/) showed what this side can do. A [penalty kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_(association_football)) is never easy, and Connor made it look so.
