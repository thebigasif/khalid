On a cold, foggy afternoon at Ospring Vale, we won 1-0 with a goal from Lewis Hart eight minutes from the end. It was not pretty, but it was our second league win in a row, and Jamie Price kept a clean sheet.

## A hard pitch at Ospring Vale

The fog came down an hour before kick-off, and for a while we were not sure the referee would start the game. The pitch was hard with frost in the shade of the trees, so both sides found it difficult to pass the ball along the ground.

Ospring Vale are strong at home, and they had the better of the first half. Jamie made a fine save from their striker after 30 minutes, tipping a shot onto the post. Our best chance fell to Ola Bakare, who shot straight at the keeper.

## The second half

We kept it tighter after the break. Connor Walsh and Ben Fenwick won the midfield, and the back four did not give Ospring Vale a clear sight of goal. Tom Ridley and Raj Chandra were excellent again.

The winner came on 82 minutes. Ola held the ball up on the edge of the box and laid it off to Lewis, who hit it first time into the bottom corner. The away end, all nine of them, went mad.

## Player of the match

Jamie Price. His save in the first half won us the game as much as Lewis's goal did.

## The team

Price; Moss, Ridley, Chandra, Okoro; Walsh, Fenwick, Doyle; Hart, Bakare, Lowe. Subs used: Bright for Lowe, Kaur for Doyle.

That is 13 goals in six games and a place in second, three points behind Ambersett. See the [league table](/blog/league-table-after-nine-weeks/) for the full picture. Next is Wexcombe Town at home, and the County Cup at Fenmoor Athletic after that. A [clean sheet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clean_sheet) is the goalkeeper's favourite stat, and Jamie now has two.
