Pre-season training starts this Tuesday at Larkmoor Fields. Sessions run on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 7pm to 8:30pm, on the floodlit pitch behind the pavilion. Everyone is welcome, whether you played last season or have not kicked a ball in years.

## What pre-season training looks like

Marcus Bell, our first-team manager, keeps the first two weeks simple. The sessions build fitness with short runs and small-sided games, then move on to shape and set pieces. Bring boots for grass and trainers in case the rain moves us onto the hard court.

The first friendly is on Saturday 29 August, at home, kick-off 2pm. Marcus will name a squad from the players who come to training, so turn up if you want a game.

## New players

We run a first team, a reserve team and eight junior teams. If you are 16 or over and want to play, come to any session and introduce yourself to Marcus or to Tom Ridley, our captain. There is no trial. You train with us for a few weeks, and then we sign you on.

Subs are £120 for the season, or £10 a month. They pay for pitch hire, referees and the kit. Students and anyone out of work pay half. Ask [Dee](/blog/authors/dee-harker/) if you would like to spread the cost.

## The new home kit

The new home kit is navy with a chalk collar, the colours the club wore when it was founded in 1974. The shirts arrive this week, paid for by our friends at the Mill Lane bakery. Away shirts stay chalk white for one more season.

Every player gets a shirt with their own number. Ask Dee for your number at training.

## Juniors

Junior training starts on 5 September, with trials for the under-12s later in the month. Priya Nair has the details in the [juniors section](/blog/category/juniors/).

If you are new to the game, the [laws of association football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laws_of_the_Game_(association_football)) are a good read before your first match. See you on Tuesday.
