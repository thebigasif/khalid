## What the table tells us

We have scored 11 league goals and let in 6. Only Ambersett have a better goal difference, and only they have beaten us. If we had held on in the [defeat at Ambersett](/blog/ambersett-report/), we would be top on goal difference. The [win at Ospring Vale](/blog/ospring-vale-report/) kept us level with Thornlea.

The gap from first to fifth is five points, so nothing is settled. Each side plays the others twice, so there are 13 league games still to come.

## The next four weeks

- Saturday 7 November: Wexcombe Town, home, league.
- Saturday 14 November: Hollin Green, away, league.
- Saturday 21 November: Fenmoor Athletic, away, County Cup second round.
- Saturday 28 November: Stavely Cross, home, league.

Wexcombe Town are fourth, a point behind us, so Saturday matters. Read the [Wexcombe Town preview](/blog/wexcombe-town-preview/) for team news. Three points for a win and one for a draw is the usual [points system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_points_for_a_win) in English football.
