We started the league season with a 3-1 win over Coldmere United at Larkmoor Fields, after going a goal down before half-time. Ola Bakare scored twice and Connor Walsh got the other.

## First half against Coldmere United

Coldmere United pressed high from the kick-off, and we struggled to play out from the back. They scored after 31 minutes, when a long throw dropped at the back post and their number 9 headed it in. Jamie Price had no chance.

We had our moments. Lewis Hart went past his full-back twice on the left, but nobody got on the end of his crosses.

## The second half

At half-time we moved Connor Walsh further forward and asked the back four to go long when Coldmere pressed. It worked within ten minutes. Tom Ridley won a header on halfway, Ola ran onto the flick and finished low into the far corner.

Connor put us ahead on 67 minutes with a shot from the edge of the box that went in off the post. Ola made the game safe in the last minute, when he chased a back pass and rounded the keeper.

## Player of the match

Connor Walsh. He covered every blade of grass, won the ball back again and again, and scored a fine goal.

## The team

Price; Moss, Ridley, Chandra, Okoro; Walsh, Fenwick, Doyle; Hart, Bakare, Lowe. Subs used: Bright for Lowe, Kaur for Doyle.

Thanks to everyone who came along and to the volunteers who marked the pitch on Friday night. Next up is a trip to Thornlea Athletic, who finished third last season.
