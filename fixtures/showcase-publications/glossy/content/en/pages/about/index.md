The Weekend Edit is a small lifestyle magazine for slow weekends. Each story has one good photograph and one useful idea: a recipe, a trip, a habit or a thing to make. The Weekend Edit, its writers and its stories are made up, to show the Glossy template.

## What we cover

- **[Home](/blog/category/home/):** colour, plants and small rooms.
- **[Food](/blog/category/food/):** breakfasts, bread and seasonal fruit.
- **[Travel](/blog/category/travel/):** short trips, packing light and wild swims.
- **[Style](/blog/category/style/):** things to make, wear and keep for years.
- **[Wellbeing](/blog/category/wellbeing/):** walks, bikes and quiet evenings.

## Who we are

- **Mira Okafor** edits the magazine and writes about home, food and wellbeing.
- **Jonas Lindqvist** writes about travel and style.

The [people page](/blog/authors/) lists both of us with our stories.
