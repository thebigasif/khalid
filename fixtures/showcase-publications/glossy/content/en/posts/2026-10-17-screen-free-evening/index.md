A screen-free evening once a week is one of the simplest changes we have made at home. No phone, no laptop, no television, from supper until bed. It felt strange for two weeks and now it is the evening we look forward to most.

## Why a screen-free evening helps

Screens fill every quiet moment, so the day never quite ends. Without them, the evening feels longer and slower. Many people find they fall asleep sooner, because the light from a [screen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Display_device) is no longer the last thing they see.

## How we do it

1. **Pick one evening.** Ours is Thursday. It is the same each week, so nobody plans around it.
2. **A box for the phones.** At six o'clock every phone goes in a box in the hall, on silent.
3. **Light a candle.** It is a small signal that the evening has changed.
4. **Cook something simple** that takes a little time, such as soup or a tray of roast vegetables.
5. **Choose one thing to do:** a book, a board game, a puzzle, letters or a long bath.
6. **Go to bed early,** with a paper book.

## What to do instead

- Read aloud to each other, a chapter each.
- Play cards. One pack does a hundred games.
- Start a slow project, such as the scarf in [knit your first scarf](/blog/knit-first-scarf/).
- Repot a plant from our list of [easy houseplants](/blog/easy-houseplants/).
- Write a letter or a postcard to someone far away.

## If you live alone

A screen-free evening works alone too. Call a friend on the landline before six, then let the evening be quiet. Music on a radio or a record player is allowed in our house; it has no screen.

## The next morning

The best part is the morning after: you wake a little earlier and calmer. Some weeks we start the next day with [a walk for your head](/blog/mindful-walking/) before breakfast. For a calmer room to spend the evening in, read about [a wall the colour of afternoon light](/blog/warm-paint-colours/).
