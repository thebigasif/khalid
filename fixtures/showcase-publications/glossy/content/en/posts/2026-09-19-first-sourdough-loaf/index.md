Your first sourdough loaf does not need special tools or a strict timetable. It needs a lively starter, a pot with a lid and two relaxed days.

## What you need for a first sourdough loaf

- 100 g active starter, fed the night before
- 375 g water at room temperature
- 500 g strong white flour
- 10 g salt
- a large bowl, a tea towel and a cast iron pot with a lid

A sourdough [starter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourdough) is flour and water that wild yeast has made its home. If you do not have one, ask a baker or a friend for a spoonful. It keeps for years in the fridge.

## Day one

1. **Morning: mix.** Stir the starter into the water, add the flour and mix to a shaggy dough. Cover and rest for an hour.
2. **Add the salt.** Sprinkle it on with a splash of water and squeeze it through the dough.
3. **Fold four times.** Every half hour, wet your hand, lift one side of the dough and fold it over. Turn the bowl and repeat on each side.
4. **Let it rise.** Leave it until it is about half as big again and wobbly. In a warm kitchen this takes three to five hours.
5. **Evening: shape.** Turn it out, fold the edges into the middle and roll it into a tight ball. Put it seam side up in a bowl lined with a floured tea towel.
6. **Fridge overnight.** Cover it and leave it in the fridge for twelve to sixteen hours.

## Day two: bake

Heat the oven with the pot inside to 250 °C for 45 minutes. Turn the cold dough onto baking paper, cut a deep line across the top and lower it into the pot. Bake with the lid on for 20 minutes, then lid off at 230 °C for 20 to 25 minutes more, until deep brown.

## The hardest part

Wait at least an hour before you cut it. The inside is still cooking. Then eat it with good butter, or with the brown butter from our [Sunday pancakes](/blog/fluffy-pancakes/).

If your loaf is flat, your starter may need another day of feeding. Bake again next weekend; the second loaf is always better. Serve it with the quick jam from our [persimmon recipes](/blog/persimmon-recipes/), and bake on a slow day, as in [a quiet evening at home](/blog/screen-free-evening/).
