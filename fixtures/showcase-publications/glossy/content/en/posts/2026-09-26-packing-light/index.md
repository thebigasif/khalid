Packing light means one bag that you can carry up stairs, lift into a rack and never check in. A week away fits in a cabin bag if you plan your clothes in one colour family.

## Packing light starts with a colour plan

Pick two neutral colours, such as navy and cream, and one accent, such as rust. Everything you pack should go with everything else. Then three tops and two bottoms make six outfits, and a scarf or a hat changes the look.

## The list

| Item | How many | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tops | 3 | One plain tee, one shirt, one light knit |
| Trousers or skirts | 2 | Wear the heavier pair on the journey |
| Dress or smart layer | 1 | For one evening out |
| Jacket | 1 | Worn, not packed |
| Shoes | 2 pairs | Walking shoes worn, light shoes packed |
| Underwear and socks | 4 sets | Wash a set in the sink midweek |
| Swimming things | 1 | Rolled in a shoe |
| Hat or scarf | 1 | Changes every outfit |
| Wash bag | 1 | Small bottles only |

## How to pack

1. **Roll soft things,** such as tees and knits, and fold stiff things, such as shirts.
2. **Fill the shoes** with socks and chargers.
3. **Heaviest at the wheels,** lightest at the top.
4. **Keep one pocket free** for the things you buy.

A [packing cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_cube) or two keeps the bag tidy, but a few cloth bags do the same job.

## What to leave at home

- The "just in case" outfit. You will not wear it.
- Full-size bottles. Buy what you run out of.
- A second jacket. Layer the knit under the one you have.

## On the journey

Wear your bulkiest things: the jacket, the walking shoes and the heavier trousers. Keep a water bottle, a book and a snack in a small tote.
