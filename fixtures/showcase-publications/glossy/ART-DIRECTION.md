# The Weekend Edit: art direction

The Weekend Edit is a made-up lifestyle magazine. Its photographs should look like a weekend supplement: bright, close and warm, each one showing the thing the story is about.

## Tone

- **The subject fills the frame.** A loaf, a plant, a hat, a lake. Few people, and none shown close enough to be the subject.
- **Daylight and true colour.** Morning or late sun, never flash. One evening picture, the tea light, is warm and dark on purpose.
- **No brands.** No logo, label or printed name may show.
- **Every photo is CC0.** All thirteen come from the WordPress photo directory under CC0 1.0, so no credit has to show on the page. The photo credits page still lists each one.

## Palette

Warm and clear, with a few strong colours:

- cream, sand, honey and wood;
- the blue of water and sky;
- orange persimmons, a red bicycle, a yellow suitcase and a blue ball of wool as accents.

These sit well on the sample brand's cream page, its peach surface and its deep teal accent.

## Subject mix

Of the 13 photographs: home 2, food 3, travel 3, style 2, wellbeing 3. Seven are landscape, four portrait and two wide, so the card rhythm crops each one a little.

## One ratio per slot

| Slot | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cover picture | 4:3, taller on a wide screen | Fills the start side of the cover beside the panel. The lake keeps its water and peaks in the middle. |
| Wide card | 3:2, 16:9 on a tablet | The first card of each run of six. |
| Tall card | 4:5 | The second card. Portrait photos suit it best. |
| Square cards | 1:1 | The third to fifth cards, and More stories under a post. Each subject sits in the middle. |
| Band card | 16:9, 4:3 on a tablet | The sixth card, beside its text on the surface colour. |
| Lead picture on a post | its own, at most 44rem tall | Across the frame, with rounded corners. |
| Author mark | 1:1 | Drawn, one teal line on a light tint of the same teal. No photographs of people. |

## Phone crop

On a phone the cover picture is 4:3 above its panel, the wide card is 4:3, and the square cards become rows with a small square picture beside the title. Every subject sits in the middle third, so the square crops keep it.
