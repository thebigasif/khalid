Owen covers the harbour, the ferries and the council's money. Before he joined the paper he spent three summers as a deckhand on the island ferry, so he reads a timetable the way the crew does. He attends every harbour board meeting and most planning committees, and he reads the papers behind each vote before he writes about it.
