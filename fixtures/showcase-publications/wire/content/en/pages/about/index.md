Port City Wire is the local news site for Port Arden and its harbour. We report what the council decides, what changes on the quays and the ferries, what happens in the town's schools and library, and how the town's teams did at the weekend. We publish every weekday, and on weekends when the weather or the harbour makes news.

Port Arden, its council and everyone named in our stories are made up. Port City Wire is a demonstration publication, written to show how a local newsroom reads in this template.

## Who writes here

- **Judith Quinn** is the editor. She decides what leads each morning and writes the weather and emergency updates.
- **Owen Reyland** covers the harbour, the ferries and the council's money.
- **Nora Fennick** covers schools, the library, local business and sport.
- **Gil Penhale** writes a fortnightly column. His views are his own, not the paper's.

## How we check a story

Every story is read by a second member of the newsroom before it goes live, and the story names them. We name the meeting, report or person each fact comes from. When we get something wrong, we correct the story, say what changed at the foot of it and date the change. Our editor explains the rest in [how we report the council](/blog/council-reporting/).

## How to reach us

Write to newsdesk@example.com, or use the link at the foot of any story. A reporter reads every message within one working day. The photographs on this site come from photographers who share their work under open licences, and none of them shows Port Arden; the [photo credits](/blog/photo-credits/) page lists each one.
