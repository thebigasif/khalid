The library opening date for Port Arden's new library on Customs House Square is Friday 30 October, the council's library service announced on Saturday. The building replaces the library on Chapel Street, which closes for good at 17:00 on Saturday 24 October after 63 years.

The opening comes six weeks later than first planned. Library manager Saira Bell said the delay came from the shelving, which arrived in August instead of June. "We wanted every book on the shelf before we opened the door," she said. "Nobody wants to visit a new library and find half of it in boxes."

## What the new library offers

The new building has three floors. The ground floor holds the adult lending library, the cafe and twelve public computers. The first floor is for children and teenagers, with a story room that seats forty. The top floor holds the town archive, a quiet study room and two meeting rooms that groups can book.

The building also brings longer opening hours. The old library opened on five days a week. The new one opens on six.

| Day | Chapel Street library (until 24 October) | New library (from 30 October) |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Closed | 09:00 to 19:00 |
| Tuesday | 10:00 to 17:00 | 09:00 to 19:00 |
| Wednesday | 10:00 to 17:00 | 09:00 to 19:00 |
| Thursday | 10:00 to 19:00 | 09:00 to 20:00 |
| Friday | 10:00 to 17:00 | 09:00 to 17:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00 to 13:00 | 10:00 to 16:00 |
| Sunday | Closed | Closed |

## The week before the library opening date

Between 24 and 30 October, no library will be open in Port Arden. That week is also the [October half-term](/blog/half-term-dates/), so the library service has planned extra visits for families. Books due back that week will be renewed automatically, and no fines will be charged. The book drop at Chapel Street stays open until Wednesday 28 October, and staff will carry its contents to the new building.

The mobile library will stop on Customs House Square on Tuesday 27 and Thursday 29 October, from 10:00 to 15:00, for anyone who needs a book that week.

:::callout{kind=note}
Your library card works in the new building. You do not need a new one, and your loans, holds and reading lists move across on their own.
:::

## The opening day

On the library opening date, the doors open at 09:00. Pupils from Harbourside Primary will read in the story room at 10:30, and the archive team will show maps of the old harbour on the top floor through the afternoon. The cafe opens at 08:30 that morning.

Nora Fennick will take readers through every floor in [a floor-by-floor guide](/blog/inside-new-library/) on opening day. Columnist Gil Penhale looks back at the Chapel Street building in [his column on the old library](/blog/old-library-column/). The [library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library) service is run by the council and is free to join.
