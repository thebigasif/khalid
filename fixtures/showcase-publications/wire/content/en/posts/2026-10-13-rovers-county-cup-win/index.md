Port Arden Rovers won the County Cup for the first time in 19 years on Sunday, beating Harlow Bridge 4-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw at Ropewalk Park. Goalkeeper Sam Okoro saved two spot kicks in the shoot-out in front of 1,340 fans, the biggest crowd at the ground this season.

Harlow Bridge, who play a league higher, went ahead after 23 minutes through their captain, Lewis Hart. The Rovers equalised eight minutes from time when Jamie Pell headed in a corner from Callum Rees.

## How the County Cup final was won

Rovers manager Danny Coyle had picked a side to defend first. For an hour, it did not work. Harlow Bridge had the ball in the Rovers' half for most of the first half and hit the post just before the break.

The change came when Coyle brought on two fresh wingers after 60 minutes. "We had to stop waiting and start asking questions of them," he said afterwards. "The lads were brilliant. They kept going when it would have been easy to accept it."

The equaliser came from the third of three corners in a row. Pell, a centre back who works as a mechanic at the ferry terminal, rose above two defenders at the near post. Neither side could find a winner in the 30 minutes of extra time.

## The shoot-out

The [penalty shoot-out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_shoot-out_(association_football)) was taken at the Quay Street end, where most of the home fans stood.

| Kick | Rovers | Harlow Bridge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rees, scored | Hart, scored |
| 2 | Pell, scored | Morgan, saved |
| 3 | Adeyemi, missed | Clarke, scored |

Okoro dived to his right for both of his saves. "I watched their last three shoot-outs on video," he said. "Most of them go the same way when they are nervous."

## What the County Cup win means

The win puts the Rovers into the first round of the regional trophy next season, with a home tie in August. It also brings £2,500 in prize money for the 2026 final, which club secretary Pat Doran said will go towards new floodlights at Ropewalk Park.

The club's women's team plays its own county semi-final on Sunday 25 October at 14:00, also at Ropewalk Park. Entry is free.
