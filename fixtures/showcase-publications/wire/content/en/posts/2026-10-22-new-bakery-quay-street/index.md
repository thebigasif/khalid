A new bakery opens on Quay Street on Saturday 24 October, in the shop that was the harbour chandlery for more than fifty years. Tidewater Bakery will sell bread, rolls and pastries from 06:30, half an hour before the first ferry leaves for Maddock Island.

The bakery is run by Lena Varga, who baked for a hotel kitchen in the city for nine years before moving to Port Arden with her partner in 2025. She has spent the summer fitting out the shop and testing her loaves on the neighbours. "The fishermen were my first customers," she said. "They told me exactly what they thought of every loaf, which was useful, and not always kind."

## What the new bakery sells

At the new bakery, Varga bakes three breads every day: a white sourdough, a seeded rye and a plain tin loaf. Rolls, cinnamon buns and a savoury pastry filled with smoked fish from the market make up the rest of the counter. A cup of coffee costs £2.20, and a large [sourdough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourdough) loaf £4.20.

The ovens are fired at 03:00. Varga says the hours were set by the harbour. "If you open at eight on Quay Street, you miss half your customers," she said. "The boats are in, the ferry is gone, and everyone has already had breakfast."

## The old chandlery

The building on the corner of Quay Street and Ropewalk sold rope, paint and engine parts to the fishing fleet from 1971 until it closed in 2024, when its owner retired. It stood empty for eighteen months. The council gave the new bakery a £6,000 grant in September 2026 from its town centre fund towards new windows and a ramp at the door.

Varga has kept the chandlery's long wooden counter and its wall of small drawers, which now hold paper bags and napkins. "People come in and tell me what used to be in each drawer," she said. "Shackles, mostly."

:::callout{kind=tip}
Rolls left at the end of the day go to the food bank at St Brendan's church hall. If you want a particular loaf on a Saturday, order it by Friday lunchtime.
:::

The new bakery is the third business to open on Quay Street this year, after a bike repair shop and a second-hand bookshop. It sits opposite the fish market, which moved to [earlier hours](/blog/fish-market-hours/) this week.
