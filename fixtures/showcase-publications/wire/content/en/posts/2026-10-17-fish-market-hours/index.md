The Port Arden fish market will open an hour earlier from Monday 19 October, at 05:00 instead of 06:00, the harbour board said on Friday. The change lets wholesale buyers load their catch onto the 07:00 lorries to the city markets, which until now left before the auction had finished.

Public sales from the market hall will also move, to 08:00 to 12:00. Fish market manager Bill Ostrander said the fleet had asked for the change for two years. "We were landing good fish and watching it miss the lorry," he said. "An hour makes the difference between selling it fresh in the city tomorrow and selling it cheap here today."

## The new fish market hours

| What | Old hours | New hours from 19 October |
|---|---|---|
| Boats land their catch | From 04:00 | From 03:30 |
| Wholesale auction | 06:00 to 08:00 | 05:00 to 07:00 |
| Lorries leave for the city | 07:00 | 07:00 |
| Public sales in the market hall | 09:00 to 13:00 | 08:00 to 12:00 |
| Market hall closed | Sundays | Sundays and Mondays |

The market hall will now close on Mondays as well as Sundays, because few boats fish on a Sunday and there is little to sell the next morning.

:::figure{src="boats.jpg" alt="A row of blue and white fishing boats moored side by side at a harbour quay on a sunny day, the boats that land at the fish market each morning"}
Port Arden's fleet is fourteen boats, most of them under 15 metres long.
:::

## What it means for the quay

The earlier start brings more traffic onto Harbour Road before dawn. Forklifts carrying boxes from the landing bay to the market cross the road about forty times in a morning. The harbour board will put a marshal at the crossing from 03:30 to 07:30, and the speed limit on that stretch of Harbour Road drops to 10 mph during those hours.

Residents of Quay Street have asked for the lorries to wait on the North Quay car park rather than in the street. Ostrander said that would happen "from next month", once the market's new loading bay lines are painted.

## Buying fish at the market

Anyone can buy at the public counter. The market sells whole fish and fillets, and the staff will clean and fillet a fish for you while you wait at no extra charge.

:::callout{kind=tip}
The best choice is on Tuesday to Thursday mornings. Friday stock runs low by 10:00, because the restaurants on Quay Street buy ahead for the weekend.
:::

A [fish market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fish_market) has stood on this part of the quay since 1890. In 2028, the landing bay moves 60 metres east as part of the harbour works, so boats can unload right beside the hall.
