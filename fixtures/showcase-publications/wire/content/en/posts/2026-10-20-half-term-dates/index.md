Port Arden's council schools close for the October half-term from Monday 26 October to Friday 30 October, and pupils go back on Monday 2 November. The council's education service has published the term dates for the rest of the school year, which ends on Wednesday 21 July 2027.

The term dates apply to the town's eleven council primary schools and to Port Arden High School. Two schools set some of their own dates, and we list those below.

## Term dates and holidays for 2026 to 2027

| Term | Starts | Mid-term break | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Autumn 2026 | Thursday 3 September | Monday 26 to Friday 30 October | Friday 18 December |
| Spring 2027 | Tuesday 5 January | Monday 15 to Friday 19 February | Thursday 25 March |
| Summer 2027 | Monday 12 April | Monday 31 May to Friday 4 June | Wednesday 21 July |

Easter falls on 28 March 2027, so the spring term ends on the Thursday before Good Friday. The May bank holiday on Monday 3 May is a day off for every school.

## Training days

Each school also closes for five training days, when staff are in and pupils are not. Three are shared by every council school:

- Wednesday 2 September 2026
- Monday 4 January 2027
- Friday 28 May 2027

Each school picks its other two days and must tell parents by the end of October. Check your school's newsletter or ask the office.

## Schools with their own term dates

- **St Brendan's Primary** breaks up for the October holiday on Thursday 22 October, a day early, because its hall is being rewired.
- **Maddock Island School** keeps the same term dates but starts each day at 09:15, so pupils from the island's far end can catch the school bus after the morning ferry.

:::callout{kind=note}
The council's [school term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_term) dates for 2027 to 2028 will be published in January. Parents planning a summer holiday should wait for them before booking.
:::

## Things to do in the October break

The new library on Customs House Square opens on the last day of the break, Friday 30 October, with readings in the children's story room at 10:30. Our [guide to the new library](/blog/inside-new-library/) has the details of each floor.

Westgate leisure centre runs swimming lessons every morning of the holiday week, at £4.50 a session in 2026. Book at the front desk. The Rovers' women's team plays a county semi-final at Ropewalk Park on Sunday 25 October at 14:00, the day before the schools close, and entry is free; read how the men's team [won the County Cup](/blog/rovers-county-cup-win/) earlier this month.
