Port Arden Council published its draft council budget for 2027 on Monday, proposing to spend £84.2 million in 2027 and to raise council tax by 3.9 percent from April 2027. The full council will vote on the plan on Thursday 10 December, after six weeks in which residents can comment on it.

The draft keeps every library and leisure centre open. In 2027 it puts £3.1 million into the harbour works. It also cuts £1.4 million in 2027 from the council's own running costs, mostly by leaving 22 office posts empty when the people in them leave. Finance lead Councillor Owen Pryde called it "a budget that holds the line without pretending things are easy".

This guide sets out what the council budget plans to spend, where the money comes from and what changes for your bill.

::toc

## What the council budget pays for

Most of the money goes on services the council must provide by law, such as adult care and children's services. The rest pays for the services people notice day to day: bins, roads, libraries and parks.

| Service | 2026 budget | 2027 draft | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult social care | £27.6m | £29.4m | +£1.8m |
| Children's services | £17.9m | £18.7m | +£0.8m |
| Bins, recycling and street cleaning | £8.2m | £8.4m | +£0.2m |
| Roads, lighting and parking | £6.9m | £6.6m | -£0.3m |
| Harbour and coastal defences | £4.1m | £5.3m | +£1.2m |
| Libraries, museums and archives | £3.3m | £3.4m | +£0.1m |
| Parks, sport and leisure | £3.6m | £3.5m | -£0.1m |
| Housing and homelessness | £4.8m | £5.3m | +£0.5m |
| Running the council | £5.0m | £3.6m | -£1.4m |
| **Total** | **£81.4m** | **£84.2m** | **+£2.8m** |

The two biggest rises in the council budget, in adult care and the harbour, account for more than the whole increase. Every other line rises a little, stays flat or falls.

:::callout{kind=stat}
**£29.4 million**, more than a third of the whole council budget, goes on adult social care in 2027.
:::

## Where the money comes from

Council tax raises just under half of the money in the council budget. The rest comes from business rates, government grants and the fees the council charges for things like parking, harbour dues and planning applications.

| Source | 2027 draft | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Council tax | £40.1m | 48% |
| Business rates kept locally | £17.3m | 21% |
| Government grants | £14.6m | 17% |
| Fees and charges | £9.9m | 12% |
| Reserves | £2.3m | 2% |

The £2.3 million taken from reserves in 2027 is the part that worries the opposition. "You can only spend your savings once," said Councillor Freya Doyle, who leads the largest opposition group. "Next year, that gap is still there and the savings are not."

## What it means for your council tax bill

[Council tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_Tax) is set by band, based on what a home was worth in 1991. A 3.9 percent rise adds between £1.02 and £2.21 a week, depending on the band.

| Band | 2026 yearly bill | 2027 yearly bill | Extra per week |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | £1,362 | £1,415 | £1.02 |
| B | £1,589 | £1,651 | £1.19 |
| C | £1,816 | £1,887 | £1.37 |
| D | £2,043 | £2,123 | £1.54 |
| E | £2,497 | £2,594 | £1.87 |
| F | £2,951 | £3,066 | £2.21 |

Most homes in Port Arden are in bands A to C. These figures include the council's share only. The fire service and the police set their own amounts in February.

:::callout{kind=note}
If you live alone, you get 25 percent off your bill. Households on a low income may get more through the council tax support scheme. Both carry on unchanged in 2027.
:::

## What is being cut

The draft council budget names eleven savings. The largest are:

- **Office posts.** 22 jobs in the council's offices will not be filled when the people in them leave. The council says no one will be made redundant.
- **Grass cutting.** Verges outside the town centre will be cut four times a year instead of six.
- **Printed papers.** Committee papers will be online only from 2027, saving about £40,000 a year.
- **Street lights.** Lights on the ring road will dim by half between midnight and 05:00.

The council has dropped an earlier idea to close the Westgate leisure centre pool on Mondays, after 1,900 people signed a petition against it.

## The harbour money

The £1.2 million rise for the harbour pays the council's share of phase two of the harbour works, which starts in January. The rest of the cost comes from the harbour board and a government coastal grant. Our guide to [what phase two means for the quays](/blog/harbour-works-phase-two/) sets out the timetable.

## How to have your say

The consultation runs until Friday 20 November. You can comment online, in writing to the Town Hall, or in person at one of three drop-in sessions:

1. Tuesday 13 October, 18:00 to 20:00, at the new library on Customs House Square.
2. Saturday 24 October, 10:00 to 12:00, at the Maddock Island village hall.
3. Wednesday 4 November, 14:00 to 16:00, in the Town Hall council chamber.

:::figure{src="chamber.jpg" alt="An empty council chamber with rows of wooden desks and chairs on a polished floor, the kind of room where the council budget is debated and voted on"}
The full council will debate the budget in the chamber on 10 December. The meeting is open to the public and is streamed online.
:::
