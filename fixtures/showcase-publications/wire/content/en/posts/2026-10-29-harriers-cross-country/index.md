Port Arden Harriers won team silver in the senior women's race at the county cross country championships at Fenwick Park on Saturday, the club's best result since 2018. Their first runner, Jo Tamsin, finished fourth in a field of 212 on a course that had turned to deep mud after a week of rain.

The men's team finished sixth, and two of the club's under-15 girls finished in the top ten of their race. Club chair Mike Sorensen called it "a really good day out, if you do not count the shoes we lost in the mud".

## How the cross country races went

The senior women ran three laps of a 2.7-kilometre course, about eight kilometres in all. The leading group broke away on the second lap up the steep climb past the old quarry, and Tamsin hung on to the back of it until the last half lap.

"I knew I could not outsprint them, so I tried to make the hill hurt," she said. "It hurt me as well, to be fair."

In [cross country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_country_running), a team's score is the sum of its first four runners' places, and the lowest score wins. The Harriers' four finished within 20 places of each other, which is what won them the medal.

The Harriers' scoring runners were Jo Tamsin (4th), Ruth Okafor (9th), Bethan Lowe (17th) and Kate Ferris (23rd).

## The rest of the club's day

- **Senior men:** sixth of fourteen teams. First home was Adam Kerr in 19th.
- **Under-15 girls:** Lily Marsh was 6th and Ava Pryce 9th, both in their first county race.
- **Under-13 boys:** eighth of eleven teams.

## What comes next

The women's team now goes to the regional championships in January, along with Tamsin and Okafor, who qualified as individuals. The club hosts its own winter league race on the dunes behind the promenade on Sunday 22 November.

It was a good month for Port Arden sport. Earlier in October, the Rovers [won the County Cup](/blog/rovers-county-cup-win/) on penalties.
