Tallyline is a weekly letter about the markets of Varnholm. Each Friday issue reports the week's moves in the Varnholm 50 share index, the small firms index, the varn and copper, and says in plain words what moved them. Between issues we publish explainers on how markets work.

## Everything here is made up

Varnholm is not a real country. The Varnholm 50, the varn, the companies, the prices and every figure in every table and chart on this site are invented. Tallyline exists to show the Ticker template, a design for markets newsletters. Nothing on this site is investment advice, and nothing here describes a real market.

## Who writes it

- **Nadia Hargreave** edits the letter and writes the Friday wrap.
- **Tomasz Brenner** writes about copper, grain, freight and interest rates, and most of the explainers.

The [writers page](/blog/authors/) lists both, with everything each has written.

## How to read an issue

Each Friday issue opens with a chart of four numbers: the week's change in the Varnholm 50, the small firms index, the varn and copper. A bar to the right of the centre line is a rise, filled in colour. A bar to the left is a fall, drawn with stripes. The [weekly wrap](/blog/category/weekly-wrap/) desk lists every issue in one table, so you can read the weeks side by side.

New to markets? Start with [how a share index is built](/blog/share-index-weights/) and [the yield curve, explained](/blog/yield-curve-explained/).
