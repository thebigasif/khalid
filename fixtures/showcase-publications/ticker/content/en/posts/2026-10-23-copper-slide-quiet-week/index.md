Most of Varnholm's markets had a calm week. In the week to 23 October 2026, the Varnholm 50 slipped 0.4% and the small firms index rose 0.2%. The exception was copper, which fell 3.2%: the largest weekly copper slide of 2026.

## Why the copper slide happened

The Eastreach mine, one of the largest in the region, reopened on Tuesday 20 October 2026 after a six-week repair. Traders had expected it to stay shut until December. More supply, sooner than planned, pushed the price down.

Copper prices react to supply news faster than to demand news. A mine that opens or closes changes the amount for sale at once, while demand from factories changes slowly over months. Wikipedia's page on [copper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copper) explains where the metal goes once it leaves the mine.

## Miners against the buyers

The table shows how each group moved in the week to 23 October 2026.

| Group | Weekly change |
|---|---|
| Copper miners | -5.1% |
| Wire and cable makers | +1.3% |
| Builders | +0.8% |
| Varnholm 50 | -0.4% |

Wire makers gained, since cheaper copper lowers their costs. That split is common in a copper slide: the firms that dig copper lose, and the firms that buy it gain. The index as a whole barely moved, because miners are a small part of it, as our guide to [how a share index is built](/blog/share-index-weights/) explains.

## Is this the end of the rise?

Copper is still higher than at the start of 2026. One mine reopening does not change the longer story of [factory orders and the copper price](/blog/copper-price-factory-orders/), which has pushed copper up since spring. The next factory orders figure, due on 30 October 2026, will say more.

## The varn

The varn was almost flat, down 0.1% in the week. With no news from the central bank, currency traders waited for the minutes of [the last rate meeting](/blog/varn-slips-after-rate-pause/).

## What a copper slide means for readers

A fall like this one changes costs for firms that buy copper months later, not at once. Most firms buy ahead, so cheaper copper shows up in their results the following year.
