The farm ministry said the 2026 wheat harvest came in 12% above its spring forecast, the largest on record. In the week to 9 October 2026, the harvest lifted the small firms index 1.9%, while the Varnholm 50 rose a more modest 0.6%.

## Why the harvest helped smaller firms most

Most of Varnholm's food makers, mills and farm suppliers are small. A big harvest means more grain to mill, store and sell, and cheaper flour for bakers. Wikipedia's page on [wheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat) explains how the grain moves from field to flour.

The table shows how each kind of firm moved in the week to 9 October 2026.

| Firm type | Weekly change |
|---|---|
| Flour mills | +3.8% |
| Grain stores and silos | +3.1% |
| Bakers and food makers | +2.2% |
| Tractor dealers | +1.6% |
| Small firms index | +1.9% |

## Who loses from a big harvest

Farmers sell more grain but at a lower price. The wheat price on the Varnholm grain exchange fell 6% in the week to 9 October 2026. Larger farms with grain stored from 2025 lost the most, because that stored grain is now worth less.

## Copper and the varn

Copper slipped 0.7% in the week, after two strong weeks. The varn rose 0.2%. Grain is one of Varnholm's main exports, and buyers abroad need varns to pay for it. Lower [shipping costs](/blog/shipping-costs-ease/) also help grain reach those buyers.

## A word on harvest forecasts

A forecast made in spring has to guess the summer's weather. The summer of 2026 was mild and wet at the right times, so the harvest beat it. Forecasts are often wrong by this much in either direction.
