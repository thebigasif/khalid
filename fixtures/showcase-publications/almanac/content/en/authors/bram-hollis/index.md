Bram spent twelve years testing soil samples for farms and councils before he took on an allotment of his own. He now grows on two plots at Long Acre without digging, and he keeps three compost bays going all year. He writes about soil, compost, green manures and watering, and he will always tell you to feed the soil before you feed the plant.
