Rowan grew vegetables for a weekly farm stall for nine years, where a crop that rotted in store was money lost. Now Rowan grows on a half plot and fills a cool shed each autumn with potatoes, onions, squash and apples. Rowan writes about harvesting, curing and storing, and about dealing with slugs and other pests without poisons.
