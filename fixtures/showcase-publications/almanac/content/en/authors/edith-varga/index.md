Edith runs the seed library in the Long Acre trading hut, where plot holders borrow seed in spring and bring some back in autumn. She has saved her own bean, pea and tomato seed for fifteen years, and she starts most of her plants on a north-facing windowsill. She writes about sowing, raising seedlings, salad and saving seed.
