Hester edits The Field Almanac and writes the monthly jobs list. She has worked plot 14 at Long Acre Allotments for twenty-two years, on heavy clay that taught her patience. Before the Almanac she ran the training beds at a county horticultural college. She writes about planning a plot, the garden year and the crops that need the most care, such as tomatoes and beans.
