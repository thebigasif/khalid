A staff rota is the plan that says who works when. A good one covers every busy hour, shares the hard shifts fairly and reaches the team early enough for them to plan their lives. This guide shows how to build one in an afternoon, with paper, a spreadsheet or an app.

The order matters. Most rotas go wrong because the manager starts with names and fits the hours around them. Start with the hours instead.

## Build the staff rota in five steps

::::howto{name="Build a weekly staff rota"}
:::step{name="Map the busy hours"}
Write out each day in one-hour blocks. Mark how many people each block needs, using last month's tills, bookings or footfall as your guide.
:::
:::step{name="Turn the blocks into shifts"}
Group the blocks into shifts that make sense to work. A shift that starts before opening covers setup. One that ends after closing covers cleaning.
:::
:::step{name="Collect availability first"}
Ask each person when they cannot work before you place anyone. People who are asked first complain less later.
:::
:::step{name="Place the hard shifts, then the rest"}
Fill weekends, early starts and late finishes first, and share them out. The easy shifts fill themselves.
:::
:::step{name="Check breaks and rest"}
Read each person's week from top to bottom. Look for missing breaks, short gaps between a late and an early, and anyone over their hours.
:::
::::

## What to check before you share it

:::callout{kind=tip}
Send the rota out at the same time every week. A team that knows when to expect it stops asking.
:::

Check three things before you send the staff rota. Every busy hour has enough people. No one works a shift they said they could not do. Every shift has someone who can open or close.

Breaks deserve their own pass. Our guide to [planning rest breaks into each shift](/blog/rest-breaks-rota/) shows how to place them before the week starts, not on the day.
