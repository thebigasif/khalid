Open shifts are now in Fernhour. An open shift is a gap on the rota that you post for the team to claim, instead of texting people one by one. Everyone with the right role sees it on their phone. The first person to claim it gets it, once you approve.

We built open shifts because covering gaps was the most common reason managers wrote to support. Most of them described the same morning: a list of numbers, a run of texts, and the rota fixed by hand once someone said yes.

## How open shifts work

::::howto{name="Post and fill an open shift"}
:::step{name="Mark the shift as open"}
On the rota, open the shift and choose "Make open". You can do this for a new gap or for a shift someone has dropped.
:::
:::step{name="Choose who sees it"}
By default, everyone with the same role sees it. You can narrow it to your cover list.
:::
:::step{name="Approve the claim"}
When someone claims it, you get a notice. Approve it, and the rota updates for everyone.
:::
::::

Fernhour checks hours and rest before anyone can claim. A person who would go over their hours, or start too soon after a late close, does not see the shift.

## Where it helps most

Open shifts fit the routine in our guide to [covering a sick call before opening](/blog/cover-sick-call/): a cover list, a fair order and a clear record of who covered. The app keeps the order for you.

They also fit a written [swap policy for your team](/blog/shift-swap-policy/). Add one line: gaps go up as open shifts, and the rota is the only truth.

## Turning it on

Open shifts are on for every Fernhour team from today. If you would rather keep cover by phone, switch them off under Settings, then Rota.

Questions about open shifts go to our support team at hello@example.com. For the wider idea behind letting staff pick up shifts, the [overview of shift work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_work) is a useful read.
