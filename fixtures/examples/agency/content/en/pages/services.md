We do three things for local service businesses. Each one is run by the person who writes about it on this blog, so you can read how we think before you talk to us.

## Local search

Ravi sets up or repairs your Google Business Profile, writes the service pages nearby customers land on, and sets up a simple way to ask for reviews. Start with his [checklist for a Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-checklist/) to see the level of detail.

## Email marketing

Clara builds your list, writes your welcome email and sends a regular newsletter in your voice. Her post on [writing a welcome email](/blog/welcome-email/) shows how she works.

## Planning

Hannah writes a plan for the year: what to do first, what to leave, and what it should cost. If you already work with another agency, her guide to [briefing a marketing agency](/blog/agency-brief/) helps you get more from them.

## How a project starts

1. A free first call of half an hour with the person who would do the work.
2. A short written plan with a fixed price, within a week of the call.
3. Work starts once you say yes. You can stop at the end of any month.
