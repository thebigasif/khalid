Membership gives you time in the darkroom, the club's chemistry and equipment, and the members-only posts on this blog. It suits anyone who shoots black-and-white film and wants to print it, from a first roll to a full exhibition.

## What members get

- **Darkroom time** at an enlarger, booked online. Our [darkroom booking guide](/blog/darkroom-booking/) explains how.
- **Shared chemistry** for film and paper, mixed fresh by our technician.
- **A safety induction** on your first visit, so you can work alone after that.
- **Members-only posts** on this blog, such as our [notes on push processing](/blog/push-processing/).

## How to join

1. **Come to an open evening.** We show you round, and you can watch a print come up in the tray.
2. **Take the safety induction.** It takes one visit, and you need it before you book.
3. **Sign up at the desk.** We set up your booking account and add you to the members' list.

## Reading members-only posts

Members-only posts show their title and a short summary to everyone, with a note on how to join. The full post is for members.

Course handouts work differently. Each course has its own password, which we give out on the first evening.
