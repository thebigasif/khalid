Silverline Darkroom is a shared darkroom for people who shoot film. Members book time at an enlarger, use the club's chemistry and equipment, and print their own photographs. You do not need a darkroom at home, or any experience.

## Who runs it

[Joan Whitlock](/blog/authors/joan-whitlock/) founded the darkroom and runs the printing courses. [Marta Carvalho](/blog/authors/marta-carvalho/) looks after the chemistry, the enlargers and the safety induction every new member takes.

## What this blog is for

Most guides here are free for everyone. They cover developing film at home, choosing a film, working safely with chemicals and caring for negatives. A few posts are for members only: our notes on booking and the house rules, and techniques we teach in person. Course handouts sit behind a password that course members get on the first evening.
