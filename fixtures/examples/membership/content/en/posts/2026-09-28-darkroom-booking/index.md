Darkroom booking keeps the enlargers and the film sink fair for every member. You book a session online, arrive on time, and leave the space clean for the next person. This guide covers how to book, what to bring, and the house rules we ask everyone to follow.

:::callout{kind=summary}
- Book enlarger time and film sink time separately, online.
- Cancel at least a day ahead, so someone else can use the slot.
- Leave trays rinsed, chemistry logged and the enlarger switched off.
:::

## How darkroom booking works

1. **Sign in** to the booking page with your member account.
2. **Choose a station:** one of the enlargers, or the film sink for developing.
3. **Pick a session.** Short sessions suit contact sheets and test strips. Long ones suit a printing day.
4. **Confirm.** You get an email with the session and the station number.

New members can book only after the safety induction. Read [darkroom safety](/blog/darkroom-safety/) before your first visit.

## What to bring

- Your negatives in their sleeves, and the [contact sheet](/blog/contact-sheet/) with the frames you want to print marked.
- Your own photographic paper, or buy a pack at the desk. [Ilford's paper range](https://www.ilfordphoto.com/) explains the differences between papers.
- A towel and an apron. Fixer stains clothes.

## House rules

- **Arrive on time.** After a short grace period, the slot goes to anyone waiting.
- **Log the chemistry.** Write down how many sheets or rolls went through each tray, so the technician knows when to replace it.
- **Rinse and stack the trays** at the end of your session.
- **Turn off the enlarger** and cover the lens.
- **Take your prints home** or move them to the members' drying rack.

## If something goes wrong

If an enlarger fails or the chemistry looks exhausted, stop and tell the desk. Do not try to repair equipment yourself. We would rather lose a session than an enlarger.
