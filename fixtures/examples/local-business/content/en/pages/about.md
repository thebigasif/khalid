Larch Lane Cycles is a small repair workshop in Norwich. We fix and service the bikes people already own: road bikes, town bikes, children's bikes, folding bikes and cargo bikes. We do not sell new bikes. Our work is keeping yours on the road.

## Who works here

Two mechanics run the workshop. [Ruth Adeyemi](/blog/authors/ruth-adeyemi/) owns it and writes most of the repair guides. [Tom Brennan](/blog/authors/tom-brennan/) looks after the commuter and cargo bikes, and writes about riding through the winter.

## Why we write this blog

Many jobs that come into the workshop are ones you could do at home in a few minutes, with the right steps in front of you. So we write those steps down. When a job is better left to a mechanic, we say that too, and explain why.
