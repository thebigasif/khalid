The workshop is open from Tuesday to Saturday. You can drop a bike off without booking. For a full service, book ahead so we can give it a whole morning.

|---|---|
| Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday | 8:30 to 17:30 |
| Wednesday | 8:30 to 17:30 |
| Thursday | 8:30 to 17:30 |
| Friday | 8:30 to 17:30 |
| Saturday | 9:00 to 16:00 |
| Sunday | Closed |

On bank holidays we are closed. We post any other change on this page first.

## How to book a repair

1. **Call or email us.** Tell us the make of the bike and what it is doing wrong. A short video of a noise helps a lot.
2. **Bring the bike in.** We look at it with you and agree the work and the price before we start.
3. **Collect it.** We call you when it is ready. If we find something else on the way, we call before we fix it.

## Where we are

We are at 14 Larch Lane, Norwich, NR2 4PL, in the yard behind the bakery. There is space to lock two bikes outside while you wait.
