There are two ways to get The Allotment Letter by email. Both start with your email address, and you confirm it with one click.

## The Thursday letter

One letter a week, the same one you can read on this site, in your inbox on Thursday morning. It is free.

::newsletter{label="Your email address" button="Get the Thursday letter"}

## The supporters' letter

Once a month, supporters also get Margot's plot plan: what she will sow, plant and harvest in the coming month, bed by bed, with the reasons. Supporters keep the letter going.

::newsletter{label="Your email address" button="Join the supporters' list" list="supporters"}

## What happens to your address

Your address is used only to send the letters you asked for. It is never sold or shared. Every email ends with a link that unsubscribes you at once.
